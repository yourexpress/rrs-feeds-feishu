PANDA: Prior-guided Attentional Dual-path Architecture

arXiv · 2026-10-09 · 论文PDF

先验引导注意力双路架构（PANDA）

Hypergraph Neural Networks (HNNs), designed to model higher-order relations beyond pairwise interactions, typically rely either on fixed structural weights or on normalised attention. Fixed weights can propagate misleading information in heterophilic settings, while normalisation makes attention coefficients competitive, in the sense that, in a message-passing framework, increasing the weight of one sender must reduce the weight of others in the same aggregation set. Our main contribution is the introduction of PANDA (Prior-guided AttentioNal Dual-path Architecture), a plug-in for two-stage message passing HNNs applicable to both undirected and directed settings. The main path interpolates between learned receiver-normalised attention and structural incidence priors, while a non-competitive (not normalised) auxiliary path lets each sender contribute its own transformed information. We also show that, with fixed structural coefficients, PANDA recovers a certain complex-valued convolution operator adopted in previous works.
