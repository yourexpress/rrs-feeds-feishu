Parallel Sparse Identification of Nonlinear Dynamics in Python: Task- and Data-Parallel SINDy with Numba, OpenMP, Threads and Multiprocessing for the Lorenz and Rössler Systems

arXiv (cs.DC) · 2026-11-03 · 论文PDF

Python 中 SINDy 稀疏非线性动力学识别的任务/数据并行实现：Numba+OpenMP+线程+多进程四后端

The sparse identification of nonlinear dynamics (SINDy) recovers interpretable governing equations from time-series data by sparse regression over a library of candidate functions. As data sets, candidate libraries and the number of repeated fits grow, the cost of SINDy becomes dominated by the evaluation of the library matrix and its least-squares problems. We present an open-source Python implementation of SINDy in which a single set of loop-based kernels is just-in-time compiled with Numba and executed on a shared-memory multicore node by four interchangeable back ends: Numba prange, OpenMP directives through PyOMP, Python threads that release the global interpreter lock, and multiprocessing with shared memory. The normal equations are accumulated in one cache-blocked pass over the data, with an automatic fallback to a tall-skinny QR reduction for ill-conditioned problems. Two decompositions are studied on the Lorenz and Rössler systems: an ensemble (task-parallel) workload of 960 independent identifications and a library (data-parallel) workload of one trajectory with $2\times10^7$ samples and a fifth-order library of 56 candidate terms.
