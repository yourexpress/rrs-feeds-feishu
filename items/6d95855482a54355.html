Trustworthiness as a Measurement Structure for Task Conformance in 6G Service Chains

arXiv (cs.PF) · 2026-11-03 · 论文PDF

6G 服务链任务符合性的信任度量结构：按交付结果分布与契约的符合度定义可信

A 6G service crosses a chain of stages from different vendors, and its contract binds class-conditioned guarantees at the terminal interface: in the packet relay chain studied here, a priority class is owed a timely-delivery rate and a loss ceiling. We define trustworthiness as conformance of the delivered per-class distribution of outcomes to the contract and develop the measurement structure this forces. Two results fix its form: a discrepancy that treats outcomes interchangeably cannot express contract preferences, and no single expectation-valued score that determines the contracted terminal expectations can be carried from stage to stage once two or more contracted indicators remain independent at some stage. The minimal record is one signed number per independent contracted indicator, which the downstream design data carry to the terminal exactly; classical scalar trust models are the single-indicator case. The terminal deviation splits exactly into per-stage contributions.
