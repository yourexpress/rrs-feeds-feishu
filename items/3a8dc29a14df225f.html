In e-commerce advertising, ad platforms typically fail to model the externalities among advertisers when making optimization decisions: an advertiser's actual performance is largely shaped by rivals competing for the same users; these rivals typically operate in overlapping product categories and at comparable budget scales. However, capturing this rivalry as an explicit, reusable signal is challenging because it is directed, multi-faceted, and partially observable. Existing paradigms such as interaction graphs, taxonomy rules, and single-embedding models do not fully capture these properties. In this paper, we propose LACRM (LLM-driven Advertiser Competition Relationship Mining), a novel framework that constructs directed competition graphs with continuous edge intensities via a three-stage pipeline. LACRM moves beyond symmetric similarity through LLM-driven role-separated profiling, which enables asymmetric multi-route retrieval to uncover both realized and latent rivals. The framework further incorporates listwise learning-to-rank aligned with temporal auction behavior to accurately model directed competition intensity.
