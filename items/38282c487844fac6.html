Route-Aware EM Surrogate for Automated LC Matching Network Optimization

arXiv (cs.AR) · 2026-10-30 · 论文PDF

This paper proposes an open-source automated LC matching network optimization framework that combines schematic-level design optimization with electromagnetic (EM)-aware post-layout refinement. An ML-based surrogate predicts the frequency-dependent complex admittance of individual routes, enabling reuse across matching network topologies and physical layouts for the same technology node without retraining. These route models guide post-layout passive retuning to recover matching performance while preserving the layout footprint. Evaluated in a commercial 65nm CMOS technology, the surrogate achieves 1.94-4.91% mean absolute percentage error with respect to a full-wave electromagnetic solver, with no detected passivity violations. Across nine LC matching networks operating at 5-95GHz, our framework recovers S_11 from -6.37dB, for schematic-level optimization after post-layout degradation to -22.27dB after EM-aware post-layout refinement. All designs meet matching targets under full-layout EM verification in optimization runtimes below 70s.
