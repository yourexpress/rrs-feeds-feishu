SLICE-CiM: Selective Layer-wise Inference Control Engine with Confidence-Based Early Exit for SRAM based Digital Compute-in-Memory

arXiv(cs.AR) · 2026-10-27 · 论文PDF / 每日精选

SLICE-CiM：SRAM 数字存内计算 CNN 推理的分层控制引擎，置信度早退+精度寄存器等外围块协同降低开关功耗

Digital SRAM compute-in-memory (CIM) macros enable energy-efficient CNN inference by eliminating off-chip weight transfers; however, peripheral control logic introduces three inefficiencies: bit- slice over-activation at reduced precision (L1), unnecessary accumula- tion depth (L2), and idle precision-transition stalls (L3). To address these, we present four synthesizable peripheral blocks: a Flush-Merge Enable (FME) controller, a Confidence-Based Early-Exit (EE) module, a Precision Register with Decoder (PR/PD), and a Bit-Serial Cycle Controller (CC), co-designed to minimize switching activity, wordline overhead, and leakage. Implemented in TSMC 65-nm CMOS at 335 MHz, the peripheral framework occupies 1769.760 μm2 post-synthesis and 1982.131 μm2 post-PnR. Gate-level VCD analysis reports 6.0706 μW leakage and 288.2902 μW (post-synthesis) / 322.8850 μW (post-PnR) dynamic power, totaling 328.9556 μW (0.2402% of system power). The EE module dominates dynamic power (58.2%), while WLG dominates area (58.1%).
