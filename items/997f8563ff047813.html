ChampionKV: Head-Wise Adaptive Budget Allocation for KV Cache Compression

Some observation-window-based KV cache compressors use prompt attention to identify important tokens but allocate uniform or predefined budgets across heads, overlooking input-dependent differences in budget demand. We propose ChampionKV, an input-adaptive head-wise budget allocator for long-context KV cache compression. It uses the maximum aggregated observation-window attention score as a lightweight empirical proxy for attention concentration, assigning larger budgets to heads with dispersed attention and smaller budgets to concentrated heads. An abnormal-head mask and a floor-budget mechanism improve robustness under weak attention responses. ChampionKV plugs into compressors such as SnapKV and PyramidKV without changing their token-selection procedures. On LongBench and RULER, both Champion variants consistently improve over their SnapKV and PyramidKV base compressors; the improvements are largest and statistically significant on Llama3.1-70B-Instruct, especially under tight budgets, whereas results on the tested 7B/8B models remain competitive but mixed.
