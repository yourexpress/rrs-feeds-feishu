Firmware diagnostic logging (printf) is an essential debug instrument in full-chip SoC design verification, yet it imposes a heavy tax on cycle-accurate RTL simulation: formatting a 30-character, 1-argument message on a simulated CPU and serializing it through an MMIO aperture costs 3.04 to 15.23 $μ$s per call (15.27 $μ$s unweighted / 15.08 $μ$s print-weighted critical-path mean on Suite A). Unlike embedded tokenizers that target flash footprint and wire bytes with offline decoding, RTL simulation is governed by a two-regime cost function---cross-fabric SoC paths are dominated by MMIO bus stalls (84.7% of call latency) whereas local subsystem paths are dominated by on-core vsprintf formatting (61.7%)---and requires live, zero-simulation-time string reconstruction to drive UVM scoreboards. We present a host-side in-simulation tokenized logging architecture for heterogeneous multi-core SoCs that eliminates both bottlenecks with zero firmware call-site edits.
