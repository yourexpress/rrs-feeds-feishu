PiCo: Physics-Informed Concept Probing for Geo-Foundation Models

arXiv · 2026-11-06 · 论文PDF

Geo-foundation models (GFMs) are evaluated by downstream predictive performance. This reveals little about which physical properties their embeddings are sensitive to or which are used in predictions. This limits developers' ability to test hypotheses about model behaviour and users' ability to select models for new tasks. We propose Physics-Informed Concept Probing (PiCo), a post-hoc framework for explaining GFM predictions. PiCo identifies concept vectors in GFM embedding space using synthetic multispectral satellite observations from a 3D radiative transfer model coupled with a game engine. These vectors characterise GFM sensitivities to physical properties, informing domain-based hypotheses about downstream performance. Projecting embeddings onto concept vectors produces low-dimensional representations for quantifying concept importance in predictions. We evaluate PiCo on three GFMs with different input modalities using seven physical concepts covering forest leaf optics and canopy structure.
