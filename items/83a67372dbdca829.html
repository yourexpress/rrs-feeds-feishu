TriPhysBench: Evaluating Physical Intelligence for Embodied Agents

arXiv (cs.AI) · 2026-11-03 · 论文PDF

410 题物理智能基准，最强 VLM 仅 50.38% 准确率

Embodied agents need physical intelligence to understand their surroundings through physical perception, physical knowledge retrieval, and physical reasoning. These capabilities must work together to interpret events and make quantitative judgments. Existing benchmarks advance physical understanding and general-purpose tool use, yet the coordination of these capabilities in physical scenarios remains insufficiently characterized. Inspired by Popper's Three Worlds, we introduce TriPhysBench, a benchmark of 410 questions from 200 videos that brings physical perception, physical knowledge retrieval, and physical reasoning into a shared evaluation setting. Its measurement-grounded and knowledge-grounded subtasks impose complementary demands on obtaining observations and acquiring external specifications, then applying them to quantitative problems. Among six representative vision-language models, the best achieves an answer accuracy of only 50.38%, revealing limited performance on tasks that require these capabilities to work together.
