Protected Unbiased Sparsification: A Sharp Loewner Curve

arXiv cs.LG · 2026-11-11 · 论文PDF

Random sparsification replaces a probability vector by a random vector with few nonzero entries. We ask for unbiasedness, exact preservation of prescribed linear statistics, and a covariance guarantee for every downstream linear response. Let $p$ have $k$ positive coordinates, let $A$ specify the protected statistics, and let $d\le k-2$ be their effective rank. For every $d+1\le m\le k$, we construct an unbiased random probability vector $Q$ with at most $m$ nonzero coordinates, $AQ=Ap$ on every realization, and $\operatorname{Cov}(Q)\preceq h C_{p\mid A}$, where $h=(k-m)/((m-d)(k-d-1))$ and $C_{p\mid A}$ is the categorical covariance after optimal linear adjustment for the protected statistics. This coefficient is sharp uniformly over instances, even among biased estimators when error is measured by its second moment. A residual covariance bridge extends the concurrent unprotected curve to arbitrary pathwise linear protection. We also characterize instance-optimal constants for partitions with uniform weights within classes and derive bounds for peaked weights.
