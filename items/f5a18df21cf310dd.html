A Common Language for Dynamic Graphs: Interaction Tokenization Enables Foundation Models

arXiv (cs.LG) · 2026-11-02 · 论文PDF

Dynamic graphs capture evolving interactions in Web systems, including online communication, user engagement, and recommendation. Graph foundation models aim to reuse predictive capabilities across networks, but extending this capability to dynamic graphs faces two challenges. First, node identities are graph-specific, and similar interaction patterns can appear at different temporal and activity scales, making histories difficult to represent consistently across networks. Second, the predictive roles of historical patterns vary across interaction dynamics, limiting transfer from a narrow range of training processes. In this paper, we show that a common interaction language, combined with learning across diverse interaction processes, offers a basis for dynamic graph foundation modeling. We propose CL-DGFM, a Common Language Dynamic Graph Foundation Model for temporal link prediction. Its Dynamic Interaction Tokenizer encodes interaction memory, historical connectivity, and ordered destination transitions into calibrated tabular inputs, making dynamic histories accessible to existing predictors without an additional trainable graph encoder.
