Deep graph clustering (DGC) has emerged as a prominent paradigm for unsupervised attributed graph clustering. However, existing methods often struggle with two practical challenges encountered in real-world applications: the number of clusters $K$ is unknown a priori, and cluster sizes are frequently highly imbalanced. Methods relying on a pre-specified $K$ may fail to recover the underlying clustering structure when $K$ is misspecified, while many approaches implicitly favor balanced partitions, hindering the discovery of minority clusters. We propose CARROT, a Competitive Adaptive gRaph clustering framework via semi-Relaxed Optimal Transport, which introduces semi-relaxed optimal transport into deep graph clustering to jointly address both challenges. CARROT first pre-trains node embeddings to establish a meaningful embedding space, and then jointly optimizes embeddings and cluster assignments through an Adaptive Clustering Mechanism.
