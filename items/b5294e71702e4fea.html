Structural Conditional Independence Test via Neural Quantile Resolution

arXiv · 2026-11-06 · 论文PDF

Conditional independence testing is difficult when dependence is weak, localized, or expressed through distributional changes rather than a global mean shift. Existing tests usually return a scalar statistic or p-value, which summarizes evidence against independence but does not describe where the conditional law changes. We propose QRCIT, a quantile-resolution conditional independence test that compares P(X|Z) and P(X|Y,Z) through a quantile deformation learned by a shared model. A lightweight residual quantile-flow model first learns the baseline conditional quantile law and then learns the additional deformation induced by Y, yielding a global predictive-gain statistic with conditional-randomization calibration and an inspectable deformation field over context and quantile level. We also introduce the Structural Dependency Benchmark (SDB), which evaluates dependence structures motivated by real applications, such as context-local, tail-only, sign-reversing, moving-ridge, and mean-neutral patterns.
