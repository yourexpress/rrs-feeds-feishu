Practical Deep Learning-Based Block Demapping for mmWave MIMO Systems

arXiv cs.AR · 2026-11-08 · 论文PDF

Deep learning is expected to play a central role in 6G systems, by enabling data-driven physical layer designs that can adapt to practical wireless impairments. This is particularly important for millimetre-wave (mmWave) multiple-input multiple-output (MIMO) systems, which are strongly affected by residual channel distortions, interference, and hardware non-idealities that are difficult to capture with analytical models. In this paper, we propose a deep learning-based soft demapper that performs block-wise processing of received symbols instead of conventional symbol-wise detection. The demapper employs a one-dimensional convolutional neural network to learn temporal structure induced by practical propagation and hardware effects, while producing soft bit outputs compatible with soft-decision decoding stages. The proposed approach is trained and evaluated for a 4$\times$4 wideband mmWave MIMO system at 60~GHz, using an over-the-air dataset collected across multiple days and settings.
