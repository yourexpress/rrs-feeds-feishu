Multi Agent Reinforcement Learning enhanced Federated Learning for Predicting Remaining Useful Lifetime

arXiv · 2026-11-24 · 论文PDF

Remaining useful life (RUL) prediction is central to Prognostics and Health Management (PHM). The growth of industrial IoT has unlocked large multivariate sensor streams and enabled deep models - especially Transformers - for long-horizon sequence learning. However, transformers are parameter-heavy and data-hungry, while operational datasets are often siloed and privacy-sensitive. Federated Learning (FL) addresses this by training across clients without raw data sharing, but in practice many clients connect over lossy wireless links where UDP is preferred for low latency, making model exchange vulnerable to packet drops. We present a cooperative multi-agent reinforcement learning (MARL) framework that adapts FL to lossy channels by selecting per-client layer subsets to transmit and capping retransmissions. This framework is trained with QMIX value-function factorization for scalability. We instantiate the approach with a Graph Convolution Position-Aware Transformer (GCPATr) for RUL forecasting.
