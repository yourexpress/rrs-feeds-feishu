KKT-Perturbation-Aware Communication for Fully Distributed Online Convex Optimization with Nonseparable Coupling

arXiv cs.LG · 2026-11-09 · 论文PDF

Nonseparable costs and coupled constraints in distributed online convex optimization often arise when system performance and operational constraints depend on the joint configuration of all agents and cannot be decomposed into individual contributions. Such nonseparability leads to fully distributed decision-making with challenging partial local information, where each agent evaluates its local primal-dual update using estimated global information via a local belief of the joint action and the shared Lagrange multiplier to be exchanged with neighbors. While the belief consensus established by prior works ensures no-regret learning, exchanging beliefs comprising full-dimensional primal and dual states at every round imposes a communication overhead that scales with both the joint decision dimension and the network size. Existing event-triggered schemes typically activate communication according to state variation or consensus error. Under nonseparable coupling, however, errors of equal magnitude can produce markedly different perturbations after passing through the KKT operator.
