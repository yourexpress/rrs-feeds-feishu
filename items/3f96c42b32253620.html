SAGE: Optimal-Stopping Peer Selection for Decentralised Federated Learning

arXiv (cs.DC) · 2026-09-22 · 论文PDF

SAGE：用最优停时理论做去中心化联邦学习的 peer 选择，按区分难度自适应分配探测预算。

Decentralised federated learning replaces server aggregation with peer-to-peer model exchange, making collaborator selection a local decision under uncertainty. Fixed probe budgets waste effort on easy choices yet fall short when peers are hard to distinguish. We propose SAGE (Sequential Anchor-Gated Exchange), an optimal-stopping peer selector under a one-model-bearing-exchange budget. A receiver scores candidate neighbours on receiver-owned anchor evidence and selects once an advantage is certified. It continues probing only while further evidence repays its cost, and otherwise falls back to random gossip. We show that the stopping problem admits an optimal rule attained at a finite stage, and that the anchor schedule is order-optimal in the peer-risk gap and the confidence level. We further show that the selector never returns a peer worse than random gossip with high probability, and prove that no such guarantee holds for selectors that commit without a certificate. A separability threshold follows, below which no probing budget improves on gossip. Experiments span two image benchmarks, two graph families and three heterogeneity levels.
