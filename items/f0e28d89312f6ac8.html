We study coupon allocation on an online marketplace offering multiple service categories. Issuing coupons requires choosing a vector of category-specific discounts, accounting for both their direct effects and cross-category demand shifts. We present TaQFormer, a treatment-as-query Transformer for this multidimensional treatment setting. Each treatment coordinate is represented as a query token, allowing cross-attention to estimate the response to the full treatment vector. To combine large but policy-biased observational logs with a smaller stream of randomized controlled trial data, we use a two-stage pipeline: observational pretraining learns general outcome patterns, followed by LoRA fine-tuning on RCT data with a request-specific fusion gate. We evaluate the resulting system through the checks required for production, including offline uplift metrics, treatment-parallel serving, latency monitoring, and a 14-day online A/B test. At essentially the same subsidy cost rate, \model increases business value per user by 1.26\% over the production baseline and significantly outperforms both the production baseline and the RCT-only model, while meeting the latency budget.
