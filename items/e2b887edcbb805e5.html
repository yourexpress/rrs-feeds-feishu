TQ-HQNet: A Trainability-Aware and Qubit-Efficient Hybrid Quantum Neural Network for Cross-Domain Learning on Heterogeneous Data

arXiv · 2026-11-24 · 论文PDF

Hybrid quantum neural networks are a promising direction for noisy intermediate-scale quantum computing, but their practical effectiveness is often limited by poor trainability, rigid circuit design, and weak robustness across heterogeneous real-world tasks. This paper proposes TQ-HQNet, a Trainability-Aware and Qubit-Efficient Hybrid Quantum Neural Network for cross-domain learning on heterogeneous data. The proposed framework combines three mechanisms: 1) adaptive latent compression to map domain-specific features into qubit-compatible representations, 2) trainability-aware search over compact variational circuit candidates, and 3) hybrid fusion of multiple shallow quantum experts to improve robustness without increasing qubit cost excessively. TQ-HQNet is evaluated on four public datasets spanning neurological screening, phishing detection, financial direction prediction, and DNA sequence classification. Across all domains, the proposed model consistently improves over a fixed hybrid quantum baseline, with balanced-accuracy gains of +0.280, +0.170, +0.071, and +0.060, respectively.
