Sounding the Black Box: Online Training of Embedded Neural Networks in Forward-Only Physical Simulators

arXiv · 2026-11-24 · 论文PDF

Hybrid physics-neural simulators embed neural networks in partial differential equation (PDE) solvers to model unresolved subgrid dynamics while preserving resolved physics and conservation laws. Because closure outputs feed back across time steps, single-step offline regression does not directly minimize multi-step rollout error, motivating online trajectory optimization. Solver backpropagation through time (BPTT) is the standard online solution when solver derivatives are available, yet many operational Fortran and C/C++ simulators expose only non-differentiable, forward-only interfaces. We introduce FORGE (Forward-Only Response and Gauss-Newton Estimation), an online framework that trains embedded networks from forward runs alone by decoupling exact network derivatives from black-box simulator dynamics. Stage I warms up the rollout via a low-dimensional Gauss-Newton step along output-orthonormal weight directions, canceling multi-layer feature drift via a final-layer correction.
