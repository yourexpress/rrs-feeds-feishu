TSFFormer: A Frequency-Domain Spike Transformer for Green Human Activity Recognition

arXiv cs.LG · 2026-11-09 · 论文PDF

Wearable Inertial Measurement Units (IMUs) enable pervasive Human Activity Recognition (HAR), but traditional Deep Neural Networks (DNNs) suffer from an "arithmetic-latency deadlock" due to dense floating-point MAC operations. While Spiking Neural Networks (SNNs) offer ultra-low power, existing ones face spatial blind spots, rigid neurons, and gradient degradation. To address these, we propose TSFFormer, a purely spiking Fourier Transformer for Green HAR. It introduces two key innovations: (1) a MultiHeadFourier attention that performs frequency-domain global mixing, reducing complexity from \(O(V^2)\) to \(O(V\log V)\), eliminating spatial blind spots, and saving \(\sim\)50% GPU memory; and (2) a dual-compartment Temporal Spiking LIF (TSLIFNode) that utilizes dynamic thresholds to adapt to non-stationary rhythms and mitigate gradient degradation. Extensive experiments on 8 public IMU benchmarks demonstrate that TSFFormer surpasses state-of-the-art SNNs and even the classic DeepConvLSTM, achieving a \(\sim\)3\(\times\) faster training speed with an energy footprint of 0.1 pJ.
