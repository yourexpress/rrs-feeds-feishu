How to Turn an Attention Neural Network into an Interpretable Hard-Logic Predicate Circuit: Truth-Table Predicate Extraction and State-Machine Merging under Ship-of-Theseus Replacement

arXiv · 2026-11-18 · 论文PDF

把注意力网络变成可解释硬逻辑谓词电路：真值表谓词

We convert a trained attention network as a whole, attention included, into a hard-logic predicate circuit that runs in its place. Every unit of the circuit is a 0/1 predicate on tokens, and every map between predicates is a minimised truth table. Short tables, such as the product of a query code and a key code, are minimised exactly, and wide feature-to-feature transitions become implication rules by noise-tolerant minimisation. Blocks are replaced one at a time from the output down, in the manner of the Ship of Theseus, and after each replacement the remaining soft part re-adapts to the frozen circuit. Attention selection takes two forms, a row-wise comparison of integer scores (order selection) and a learned rule (rule selection). Both are fitted on the ordinary training set alone, and only on the examples that the teacher classifies correctly, so that the circuit imitates the teacher where the teacher is right.
