AlignedHPO: Efficient Surrogate-Guided Loss Hyperparameter Optimization

arXiv cs.LG · 2026-11-17 · 论文PDF

梯度对齐的损失超参数高效优化

Modern deep learning losses involve multiple hyperparameters, such as weights, scales, and margins, typically tuned manually or through expensive black-box optimization despite their differentiability. We propose AlignedHPO, an efficient framework for tuning differentiable loss hyperparameters using gradient alignment and surrogate validation objectives. Our method employs Jacobian-vector products to reduce hypergradient computation time and memory, while differentiable metric surrogates guide optimization toward task-specific evaluation criteria. We address gradient magnitude ambiguity and stochastic instability through normalized alignment and gradient smoothing. Experiments with generalized Focal Loss and ArcFace demonstrate effective adaptation across long-tailed classification and open-set face recognition. AlignedHPO achieves competitive or improved performance compared to implicit differentiation, with nearly 12x faster hypergradient computation. These results highlight the potential of efficient surrogate-guided loss hyperparameter optimization.
