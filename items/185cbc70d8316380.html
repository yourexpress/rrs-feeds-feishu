Cardiac physiological monitoring is fundamental to evaluating cardiovascular health and diagnosing related diseases. Non-contact radar sensing can capture chest-wall vibrations with spatial specificity, enabling localized physiological monitoring and cardiac assessment. Progress, however, has been limited by the absence of a reliable, research-grade hardware platform for rigorous validation of radar-based methods. Prior systems typically pair radar, namely Doppler cardiography (DCG), with only electrocardiography (ECG) or other coarse references, providing no direct mechanical ground truth and thus constraining fine-grained evaluation of radar outputs. We present a tri-modality wireless system that integrates ECG, seismocardiography (SCG), and DCG under a unified acquisition and control framework for time-aligned recording and analysis. The tri-modality system combines miniaturized wearable ECG/SCG modules with a high-resolution mmWave radar module, supporting spatially resolved sensing and subsequent feature extraction.
