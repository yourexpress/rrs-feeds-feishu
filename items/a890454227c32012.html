AvoKV-E: Payload-Aware KV Cache Eviction for Long Reasoning

arXiv (cs.LG) · 2026-10-05 · 论文PDF

面向长推理链的载荷感知 KV 缓存逐出 AvoKV-E

Long-output reasoning shifts the KV-cache bottleneck from the fixed prompt to the generated trace. Existing reasoning-cache eviction methods largely treat cached entries as routing objects, estimating whether an old key will still be read, will recur, or can be replaced. This routing-only view overlooks two effects: low-attention entries can carry large value payloads whose removal changes future predictions, and newly generated states can appear stale before later queries have had a chance to read them. We introduce AvoKV-E, a training-free eviction policy that first delays eligibility for recent states and then ranks eligible entries using candidate-normalized read pressure, key redundancy, and value-payload potential. According to empirical evaluation across different models and datasets, AvoKV-E matches or exceeds redundancy-aware, recurrence-based, and thought-adaptive eviction baselines at matched active-KV budgets, with its largest gains in the tightest-cache regime. Component and counterfactual analyses further connect these gains to delayed observation, payload-aware scoring, redundancy, and scale-robust normalization.
