KL Before and After Mixing: A Round-Complexity Separation in Parallel Sampling

arXiv · 2026-10-09 · 论文PDF

混合前后KL的并行采样轮复杂度分离

A parallel sampler can choose at random which coordinates to generate together. For a target $p$ and the output law $q_U$ of a schedule $U$ drawn afresh for each output, we compare the schedule-averaged divergence $\mathbb{E}_U D_{\mathrm{KL}}(p\|q_U)$ with the output-law divergence $D_{\mathrm{KL}}(p\|\mathbb{E}_U q_U)$, in nats. Rounds commit independent draws from exact conditional marginals frozen at the round's start, each output's schedule is drawn before sampling from a finite mixture of schedules, and every scheduled round is charged. For the uniform distribution on binary strings of even length $n$ with $n/2$ ones, the least expected number of rounds at a fixed tolerance $\varepsilon>0$ is $Θ_\varepsilon((\log n)^2)$ under the first criterion but $(e^{-\varepsilon}+o(1))\log_2\ln n$ under the second, and the double-logarithmic order is attainable with the same round count on every run. For this target, a count-state recursion gives the exact least output divergence at every round cap over all causal randomized policies.
