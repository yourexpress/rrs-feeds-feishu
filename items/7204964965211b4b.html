Simpler Tiny Recursive Models

arXiv · 2026-11-18 · 论文PDF

更简单的微型递归模型（TRM 变体）

Small models that execute deep looped computational graphs have shown great experimental results on puzzle-like problems like Sudoku, finding shortest paths through mazes, etc. The most well-known one is TRM (tiny recursive model), a simplification of HRM (hierarchical reasoning model). However, the underlying recursion is complicated and involves a high-level and a low-level state, a nested loop recursion, and the network calls with different input and output signatures depending on where we are in the loops. These mechanisms are needed to avoid memorization and overfitting. We propose STRM, a simplified design with a single state and a uniform update. STRM does not sacrifice generalization, improves training speed and final performance. Our key architectural choices that allow our simpler recursion to work are exponential moving updates on the states with varying rates that are jittered during training and readout of the solution on slow-updating channels only. We test STRM on Sudoku-Extreme, PPBench and Maze-Hard with the same data, parameters and compute as TRM: we improve over TRM by $3.8$ points on Sudoku-Extreme and $8.4$ points on PPBench and match it on Maze-Hard.
