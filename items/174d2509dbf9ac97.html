Reinforcement Learning with Verifiable Rewards (RLVR) improves reasoning in language models, but scalar outcomes provide coarse supervision over response trajectories. Self-distillation offers denser token-level guidance by comparing an unassisted policy with a feedback-informed self-teacher, yet the teacher evolves with the student, creating unstable targets and motivating additional regularization. We introduce Function-Space Policy Optimization (FSPO), a single-policy framework that combines current privileged adaptation with function-space temporal consolidation. FSPO stores sparse historical next-token distributions as fixed temporal targets, providing temporal separation without maintaining a persistent teacher model. It further introduces privileged disagreement correction to explicitly suppress locally overvalued actions, reliability-adaptive grounding that strengthens reference supervision when self-generated evidence repeatedly fails, and quality and coverage-aware temporal memory that retains verified behaviors and improves privileged demonstrations.
