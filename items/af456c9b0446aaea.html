Leap-VLA: High-Rate Robot Control from a Low-Rate VLM Backbone

arXiv (cs.AI) · 2026-11-03 · 论文PDF / 每日精选

将 VLA 解耦为低频语义锚点+高频流匹配动作专家，LIBERO-Spatial 61.4%→95.5%，缓解控制频率与语义刷新失配

Vision-language-action (VLA) models re-encode the full observation through a multi-billion-parameter vision-language model (VLM) at every control step, even though scene semantics evolve at roughly 1 Hz while reactive control requires 20-50 Hz. We present a VLA that decouples the two: a VLM runs as a low-frequency semantic anchor whose cached context is reused across control steps, while a flow-matching action expert operates at high frequency, consuming the cached semantics with a fresh, low-latency visual stream from a compact encoder pretrained on robot video with masked reconstruction, optical flow, and depth. The measured age $τ$ of the cache conditions the expert through adaptive layer normalization. Reusing the cache makes the policy a function of its age, and training covers that function explicitly: cache ages sampled from real episode history, plus an age-ramped pixel corruption that erases the stale snapshot the way deployed staleness corrupts content.
