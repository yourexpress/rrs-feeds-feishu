Sanity Checks for Attention Surgery: Null Models and Matched Interventions for Representation-Motivated Design

arXiv cs.LG · 2026-11-10 · 论文PDF

Transformer architectural modifications frequently measure a statistic on internal representations, observe that it is large, remove it architecturally, and report an improvement. Interpreting that sequence requires evaluating whether the statistic exceeds its noise floor, whether it survives a geometric null, and whether the training gain requires the specific direction removed. We formulate these requirements as three reusable checks and apply them to self-attention removal, which deletes the component of an attention head's output aligned with its own value vector. Across nine multi-head pretrained models (5,408 heads, 124M to 6.9B parameters), an earlier-position causal null accounts for 48% to 63% of the head-value cosine alignment, with no monotone scale trend.
