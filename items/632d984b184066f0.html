Robust Langevin Sampling under State-Dependent Heavy-Tailed Noise with Applications to LLM Fine-Tuning

arXiv · 2026-10-09 · 论文PDF

状态依赖重尾噪声下的鲁棒Langevin采样及其在LLM微调的应用

Existing convergence guarantees for non-log-concave sampling with stochastic gradient Langevin Monte Carlo (SG-LMC) typically assume globally Lipschitz gradients and uniformly bounded noise variance. Both assumptions can fail even in elementary sampling problems, and relaxing them better reflects practical applications such as Bayesian fine-tuning of large language models (LLMs). We relax both assumptions through generalized smoothness and our proposed state-dependent heavy-tailed oracle with finite $p$-th moments, $1<p\leq 2$. The oracle permits infinite variance when $p<2$ and includes the Blum-Gladyshev (BG-0) condition. We study these relaxations separately and jointly, proposing three methods. Under globally Lipschitz gradients, robust LMC (R-LMC) handles the proposed oracle through geometric-median centering and residual clipping. With exact gradients, adaptive LMC (A-LMC) controls discretization error under generalized smoothness. Robust adaptive LMC (RA-LMC) addresses both relaxations through robust estimation and a noise-aware step size.
