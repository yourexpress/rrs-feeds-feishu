CRAFT: Analog Circuit Design by Reasoning LLM Agents from Pre-Computed Lookup Tables

Automated analog circuit sizing methods, from black-box optimizers to recent large language model (LLM) agents, typically search the design space with a SPICE simulator in the loop, whereas an experienced designer works out a design by hand analysis. This paper presents CRAFT, a flow in which an LLM agent designs a given topology by calculation, as a designer does, and without an initial sizing. The agent works only through DeX, a service that uses pre-computed lookup tables to provide device characteristics and to solve the whole netlist for its operating point and small-signal response. It thus designs without SPICE, and every final design is verified independently in HSPICE. We test CRAFT on four circuits in a commercial 28 nm process; the largest is a 37-transistor three-stage amplifier with 53 design variables, 20 specifications and 74 saturation constraints. With Claude Opus 5.5, CRAFT meets every specification and constraint in all 20 runs, five per circuit. On that amplifier, with DeX in place of HSPICE and an identical request for both, the same agent passes in 10 of 10 runs instead of 6 of 10 within an hour and is 2.0x faster at the median.
