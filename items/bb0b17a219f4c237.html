Multi-Source and Multi-Dimensional Information Fusion in Resource-Constrained Edge Networks

arXiv · 2026-10-09 · 论文PDF

资源受限边缘网络中的多源多维度信息融合方法

Multi-source semantic information fusion enables edge networks to support global inference using observations collected by distributed terminals. However, limited communication and computation resources restrict semantic uploading, leaving the edge server with incomplete tokens that differ in modality, semantic granularity, arrival time, and channel condition. The value of these tokens also depends on the complementary or redundant information provided by other terminals. In this paper, we propose a multi-source and multi-dimensional semantic information fusion framework that coordinates terminal uploading and edge-side fusion under resource constraints. We develop Hyper-Slot to combine semantic content with available token attributes through iterative token-to-slot assignment and slot-to-token feedback, producing a compact representation for global inference. To encourage useful semantic uploading, we further design a contribution-based incentive mechanism that uses Hyper-Slot assignments to evaluate information coverage and cross-type complementarity.
