Efficient Variance-Aware Reinforcement Learning in Time-Inhomogeneous Linear MDPs

arXiv · 2026-11-17 · 论文PDF

时变线性 MDP 的高效方差感知强化学习

We study variance-dependent reinforcement learning in episodic time-inhomogeneous linear Markov decision processes (MDPs) with feature dimension $d$, horizon $H$, and $K$ episodes. While recent works have established variance-dependent regret with $\tilde{O}(1)$ deterministic-regime overhead for linear mixture MDPs (Zhao et al., 2023) and worst-case nearly minimax optimal regret $\tilde{O}(d\sqrt{H^3 K} + d^7 H^8)$ for linear MDPs (He et al., 2023),, existing variance-aware analyses for linear MDPs (Li and Sun, 2023; Huang et al., 2023) rely on a coarse concentration step whose resulting regret guarantees retain a polynomial $\tilde{O}(K^{1/4})$ additive term even in deterministic environments. We propose VA-LSVI-UCB++, a computationally efficient algorithm that achieves the variance-dependent regret bound $\tilde{O}(d\sqrt{H\min\{\mathrm{Var}_K^*,\mathrm{Var}_K^π\}}+d^5H^4)$, where $\mathrm{Var}_K^*$ and $\mathrm{Var}_K^π$ denote the cumulative conditional variances of the optimal value function and the played policies' value functions along the realized trajectory.
