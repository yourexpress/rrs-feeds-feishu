ISBC: Interpretable Sparse Binary Coding of Pretrained Text Embeddings

arXiv (cs.LG) · 2026-10-31 · 论文PDF

Pretrained text embeddings encode semantic structure in high-dimensional continuous spaces whose coordinates are difficult to interpret. We introduce Interpretable Sparse Binary Coding (ISBC), which learns a small set of binary dimensions from frozen embeddings without taxonomy labels or downstream outcomes. A trainable encoder and affine decoder combine reconstruction with controls on activity, factor utilization, dependence, and activation uncertainty. Individual bits and their conjunctions select overlapping document groups; complete activation profiles provide finer contexts for conditional comparisons. We evaluate these representations across corpora, backbones, and code configurations through reconstruction, geometry, predictive probes, and document inspection. Empirical cases identify both distinctions within existing categories and shared content across their boundaries. In a mixed-domain corpus, the most frequent profiles predominantly identify source collections, while selected within-source comparisons reveal sentiment and migration contrasts.
