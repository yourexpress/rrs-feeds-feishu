Transferable, Time-Parallel Graph Dynamics via Space-Time Factorization

arXiv · 2026-11-23 · 论文PDF

Time-parallel neural operators (FNO and successors) have transformed PDE surrogates on regular grids. Mesh-based operators (MeshGraphNets, Transolver) extend operator learning to irregular geometries, but jointly learning across diverse topologies while retaining non-recurrent temporal evaluation remains challenging. We propose space-time factorization: partition the model into local temporal operators (learned, graph-independent) and a spatial mixer that propagates information across nodes without graph-dependent learned parameters. This structural condition lets the same parameters run on unseen graphs; non-recurrent instances also admit time-parallel evaluation. Accurate zero-shot transfer further depends on controlling three information leaks (graph-dependent weights, graph-specific features, and graph-correlated training distributions) and on suitable task physics and training coverage. The protocol applies to two distinct spatial-mixer families, fixed-spectral and learned-attention. We validate the principle in three domains.
