EDISCO: Equivariant DIScrete Diffusion for Euclidean Combinatorial Optimization

arXiv cs.LG · 2026-10-06 · 论文PDF

Euclidean combinatorial optimization problems (ECOPs), such as the Traveling Salesman Problem (TSP) and Capacitated Vehicle Routing Problem (CVRP), possess inherent symmetries under the two-dimensional Euclidean group E(2), including rotations, reflections, and translations. Existing learning-based methods, including recent diffusion-based methods, rely on data augmentation or regularization to approximate E(2)-equivariance. This paper presents EDISCO, the first discrete diffusion model for ECOPs with exact E(2)-invariant generative distributions over node-index solutions. EDISCO introduces an E(2)-equivariant edge-score network coupled with a categorical continuous-time Markov chain over discrete edge variables, and exact posterior sampling provides efficient multi-step inference. This design gives EDISCO a local geometric inductive bias: edge neighborhoods with the same relative geometry and combinatorial context are represented consistently regardless of absolute position or orientation, making learning more efficient and inference more robust than non-equivariant methods.
