Concave utility reinforcement learning (CURL) unifies a diverse range of problems, including standard reinforcement learning (RL), imitation learning, and maximum entropy exploration. Unlike standard RL, CURL generally requires estimating high-dimensional state-action occupancy measures. Consequently, existing methods for CURL either sidestep the exploration problem, are restricted to the tabular setting, or lack computationally efficient policy optimization. To address this, we study CURL in finite-horizon episodic bilinear MDPs, where the transition model admits a low-dimensional representation in known feature spaces. We use the bilinear structure to efficiently estimate occupancy measures via low-dimensional feature expectations, and propose a model-based optimistic concave utility policy optimization (OCUPO) framework. We first instantiate OCUPO in the on-policy setting, where each iteration uses independent trajectories to estimate the concave utility policy gradient, and prove an $\widetilde{O}(ε^{-4})$ sample complexity.
