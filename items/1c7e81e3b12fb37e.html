TAILROLL: Tail-Aware Rollout Acceleration for RL-Based LLM Post-Training

arXiv cs.DC · 2026-11-17 · 论文PDF

RL 后训练的尾部感知 rollout 加速，区分失衡与拖尾浪费

RL post-training of large language models (LLMs) is bottlenecked by rollout: heavy-tailed responses leave engines idle as each step waits for the longest response. Asynchronous training masks, but does not eliminate, idle time. We distinguish imbalance waste (uneven placement and decode contention) from straggler waste (waiting for the longest response). Tail length, not average load, often sets the step bound. We propose TAILROLL, an online scheduler integrated into an open-source RL framework. It combines stateless in-flight migration and sufficiency-gated termination. Migration moves decoding requests between engines at chunk boundaries and resumes them with one re-prefill under a freeness-based, cost-aware tail-balancing policy. Termination stops a response when its GRPO group has enough completed samples for advantage estimation, masking its partial output from the training loss. Across ten configurations, TAILROLL reduces rollout bubble ratio by 21% to 74% and cross-engine KV-cache imbalance by 44% to 79%.
