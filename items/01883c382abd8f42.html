DGPExplainer: Generating Prototype Sequence Explanations for Dynamic Graph Neural Networks

arXiv cs.LG · 2026-11-11 · 论文PDF

Graph Neural Networks (GNNs) have been successfully applied to learning from graph-structured data. However, applying them to dynamic graphs-where nodes and edges evolve over time-presents significant challenges, particularly regarding explainability. While explanation techniques for static GNNs are well established, they do not readily extend to dynamic GNNs (DGNNs) due to the added complexity introduced by temporal dynamics and spatiotemporal dependencies, especially when generating model-level explanations. To address this gap, we introduce DGPExplainer, a post-hoc, prototype-based explainer specifically designed for DGNN models that transforms their complex decision-making processes into human-understandable, analogical, case-based explanations. DGPExplainer first employs local explanation methods to identify important elements within temporal snapshots of dynamic graph sequences. It then decomposes the graph at each time step into distinct prototypes, thereby symbolizing the dynamic graph sequence.
