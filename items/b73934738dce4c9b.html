Clock Distribution Network with Backside Clock Mesh in BSPDN Architecture

arXiv cs.AR · 2026-11-16 · 论文PDF

BSPDN架构中用背面时钟网格的时钟分布网络

We propose a clock distribution network (CDN) in which the clock mesh is located on backside metal (BM) layers, while the rest of the network remains on the frontside. Since the backside power distribution network (BSPDN) also uses the BM layers, the clock mesh and power network must share them. We identify an important problem of placing mesh receivers while accounting for keep-out zones (KOZs) between clock- and power-nTSVs. We show that this problem can be solved using a potential-field method (PFM). Machine learning models are developed to place the mesh receivers: a U-Net predicts their initial positions, and an MLP predicts force weights for PFM-guided position refinement. Both models are trained through self-supervised learning by optimizing a differentiable objective that accounts for clock latency, IR-drop, and KOZ violations. Experimental results show that, on average, the proposed CDN reduces maximum local clock latency by 26%, global clock skew by 40%, and mesh switching power by 29% compared with a frontside CDN. On the same backside CDN, ML-guided PFM satisfies KOZ constraints while lowering IR-drop by 33% and requiring 85% fewer iterations than standard PFM.
