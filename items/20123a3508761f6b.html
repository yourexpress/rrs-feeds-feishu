Matrix multiplication is the dominant computational kernel in deep learning, yet its growing scale strains conventional von Neumann hardware in energy efficiency and bandwidth. Neuromorphic architectures offer a promising alternative, but mathematical equivalence between matrix multiplication and spiking computation does not guarantee direct execution on real neuromorphic hardware, as the specific hardware paradigm may still require additional data recovery, bit-weight reconstruction, or state readout after neuromorphic execution. This paper presents BPU-SpikeMM (Brain Processing Unit-based Spike Matrix Multiplication), an exact spatio-temporal spike encoding framework for matrix multiplication on real neuromorphic hardware. We prove the exact equivalence of spike-encoded matrix multiplication under the BPU execution paradigm, distinguish mathematical equivalence from native direct-readout equivalence on BPU hardware, and build a spatio-temporal offloading pipeline supporting multiple encoding schemes.
