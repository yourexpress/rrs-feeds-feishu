A Decentralized Quantum-Resistant Notary Scheme for Blockchain Interoperability with a Novel Dynamic Trust Model

arXiv cs.DC · 2026-11-08 · 论文PDF

Blockchain interoperability is essential for enabling secure and reliable interactions across heterogeneous networks. However, existing notary schemes face two key issues: quantum vulnerability and rigid trust management. To address the first issue, this paper proposes a decentralized, quantum-resistant framework integrating post-quantum primitives ML-KEM and ML-DSA alongside AES-256, optimizing performance by using asymmetric cryptography for authentication and non-repudiation, and delegating bulk encryption to symmetric schemes. Under parallel loads of up to 270 transactions per second, this hybrid design reduces the end-to-end performance gap between the post-quantum and elliptic-curve approaches to under 6% on average, converging to within 0.2% at peak load. The second issue is handled by a dynamic trust model that uses Popoviciu's inequality to derive an adaptive discount factor $α$ based on variance in a Verifier's behavior, removing the need for fixed thresholds or multi-stage configuration, while a tunable dynamicity factor $k$ enables control over system sensitivity.
