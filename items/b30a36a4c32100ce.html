Dimension-Free Buffer Bounds for Compressed-Momentum Error Feedback

Analyses of error feedback bound the memory buffer through a compressor contraction constant -- $r/p$ for the best rank-$r$ approximation of a $p$-dimensional spectrum -- which survives into the rate. We show it need not appear at all. When the compressed momentum is re-injected into the pre-compression accumulator, the previous compressed momentum $β_1 M_{t-1}$ is an admissible competitor in the projection, and the buffer obeys $\|E_t\|_F \le (1-β_1)\|G_t\|_F + γ\|E_{t-1}\|_F$, free of the dimensions $m,n$ and of the rank $r$. The argument uses only that the compressor is a metric projection onto a closed cone, so it covers top-$k$ selection as well as truncated SVD, holds for every selection from the projection set with no condition on singular-value crossings, and in its trajectory-tracking form assumes nothing about the gradients. In the two-stage template of the standard analyses it replaces a Stage-2 constant of order $p^2/r^2$ by one of order $1$, against $Θ(p/r)$ for the sharpest contraction-based bounds.
