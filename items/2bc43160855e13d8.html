Steering Vectors as a Training Signal in LLM Post-Training

arXiv (cs.LG) · 2026-10-29 · 论文PDF

Steering vectors (SVs) are residual-stream directions that shift the behavior of LLMs when added to hidden states at inference time. While prior work has mainly used SVs as post-hoc controls, we ask whether the same directions can also guide parameter updates during post-training. We study this question across supervised learning, including SFT and distillation, and reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards (RLVR). Our framework considers two intervention regimes: a hidden-state hook during teacher-forced supervised training, and an advantage-repair mechanism on wrong rollouts in GRPO. For each regime, we analyze three design axes, which are the steering direction, its placement, and the operation used to combine it with training. In supervised settings, SV injection improves over plain SFT and distillation, with gains scaling with the off-policy gap and reaching up to a $12\%$ improvement when distilling from thinking-mode teachers. These gains hold across three random seeds.
