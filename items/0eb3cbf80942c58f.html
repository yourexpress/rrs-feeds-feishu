Bayesian Low-Rank Posteriors for Scalable Membership Inference

arXiv cs.LG · 2026-11-07 · 论文PDF

Membership inference attacks (MIAs) aim to determine whether a sample was used during the training of a target model. Strong MIAs rely on reference (or shadow) models to estimate target-conditional score distributions, but training such ensembles is computationally expensive for modern large-scale Vision--Language Models (VLMs). In this work, we propose a scalable alternative that replaces explicit reference-model training with a Bayesian approximation over low-rank adaptation parameters. Specifically, we model uncertainty only within the LoRA subspace and construct a posterior from a single training run, from which we sample a diverse set of \emph{virtual reference models}. We instantiate this approach using Stochastic Weight Averaging Gaussian (SWAG), enabling efficient approximation of target-conditional score distributions at a fraction of the cost of conventional shadow-model ensembles. We evaluate the resulting attack on three downstream-adapted VLMs across two privacy-sensitive visual question answering tasks, namely Document VQA and Medical VQA.
