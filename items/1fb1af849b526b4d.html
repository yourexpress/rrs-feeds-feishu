Wavelet-Energy-Driven Dynamic Patching for Time Series Forecasting

arXiv cs.LG · 2026-11-16 · 论文PDF

小波能量驱动动态分块用于时序预测

Patch-based transformers tokenise a time series into fixed-size, uniform windows, which commits the model to a single temporal resolution even though real signals are multi-scale. The discrete wavelet transform (DWT) is a natural alternative because it decomposes a signal across scales. A growing body of work makes the wavelet filter learnable, on the assumption that a more flexible wavelet forecasts better. That assumption has rarely been tested in isolation, because a single reported improvement usually credits three things at once. These are the use of any multi-resolution decomposition, the choice of wavelet and training the filter. We introduce a forecaster that turns a learnable-DWT per-scale energy map into variable-length patches by merging adjacent windows that share a dominant scale. Three findings follow. First, most of the improvement is there before any filter is learned. A frozen wavelet beats a fixed-patch baseline on eight of the nine datasets by 1.5% to 7.8%, and on four of them it recovers all of the gain. Traffic is the one exception, where it is 1.2% worse.
