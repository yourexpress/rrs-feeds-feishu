Accurate path loss (PL) estimation is essential for wireless network planning and optimization. This paper proposes HankelKANet, a lightweight physics-inspired network for PL estimation that structurally embeds electromagnetic propagation characteristics into the neural architecture. Built on a single residual UNet backbone, the proposed model integrates urban layout features with a convolutional Kolmogorov-Arnold network(KAN). Specifically, the convolutional kernels are parameterized by the radial amplitudes of the zeroth- and first-order Hankel functions. This formulation acts as a physics-inspired nonlinear distance encoding, enabling the network to capture dominant attenuation characteristics directly without requiring additional physics-based loss terms. Experimental results demonstrate that HankelKANet achieves state-of-the-art accuracy and efficiency on both the simulated RadioMapSeer dataset and the real-world Huawei RSRPSet.
