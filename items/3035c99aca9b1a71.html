We present CHSM8, a 210M-parameter chess transformer that plays moves conditioned on a free-text description of a playing style, e.g. "go for the Sicilian Defense", "solid, safe, risk-free chess" or "You are Magnus Carlsen in his prime". The model is pretrained without search on 1.07B filtered Lichess games, using a factorized move representation and legal-move masking. It reaches 1837 Elo against a strength-limited Stockfish and, in 1,000-game matches, beat every other public model we tested that was likewise trained without preference tuning, rewards or search, including one with 13 times as many parameters. We then fine-tune it to follow text on a synthetic dataset: Gemma-4-31B describes the playing style of 3M game sides, a 1B Gemma-3 model distilled from the 2.4M of those descriptions that contradict nothing on the board labels the rest, and every one of 30M selected game sides gets five descriptions. Supervised fine-tuning attaches a frozen text encoder through gated cross-attention, and direct preference optimization (DPO) uses the same moves under a mismatched description as the rejected sample.
