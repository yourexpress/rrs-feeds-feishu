Elon Musk's artificial intelligence group, now part of SpaceX, has laid off approximately 60 employees from its Grok Imagine team, which develops image and video generation tools. The move leaves only a small crew to maintain the existing product and signals a significant strategic pivot for the company.

The layoffs are part of a broader reorganization following the acquisition of xAI by SpaceX in February 2026. The company is reportedly shifting its focus away from compute-intensive video generation and prioritizing the development of AI coding tools and its 'Grok Bot' agent product. This follows the company's multi-billion dollar acquisition of AI coding startup Cursor in August.

This strategic retreat mirrors a similar decision by rival OpenAI, which discontinued its Sora video tool earlier in the year due to prohibitive operational costs. The move is a reversal from March, when Musk stated xAI was "doubling down" on video after OpenAI's exit.
