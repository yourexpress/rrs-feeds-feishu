ASUS announces Ascent QN10, a compact mini PC based on Qualcomm Snapdragon X2 Elite, starting at $1,350

80 TOPS NPU 的 0.7L 迷你主机定位本地 AI 开发，支持 Qualcomm AI Hub 175+ 模型

Asus has released the Ascent QN10 mini PC, equipped with Qualcomm Snapdragon X2 Elite processor. The new product runs on the Windows-on-Arm operating system. The device debuted earlier this year, but now the manufacturer has confirmed final specifications and availability information.

The Ascent QN10 is powered by an 18-core Snapdragon X2 Elite processor with third-generation Oryon core and integrated Adreno X2-90 graphics card. Qualcomm Hexagon’s embedded neural processing unit (NPU) delivers up to 80 TOPS INT8 performance, exceeding Microsoft’s Copilot+ PC requirements.

The hardware is housed in a box with dimensions of 130 x 130 x 40 mm, a volume of less than 0.7 liters, and a weight of 620 grams. The system supports up to 32 GB of LPDDR5X-9600 RAM, and the storage subsystem includes one PCIe 5.0 M.2 2280 slot and another PCIe 4.0 M.2 slot. The total amount of permanent memory can be up to 4TB.

Ascent QN10 is equipped with 3 USB4 Type-C ports (40 Gbps), 3 USB Type-A (10 Gbps), 1 USB 2.0 Type-A, 1 HDMI 2.1 connector and 1 2.5 Gbit network interface. Wireless features include support for Wi-Fi 7 and Bluetooth 6.0. Asus claims that the Ascent QN10 can connect up to four 4K displays simultaneously.

In the US, Best Buy is offering the Ascent QN10 configuration with 16 GB LPDDR5X-8533 RAM and 512 GB SSD for $1,349.99, according to VideoCardz. Asus’ specs also mention higher-end models, offering up to 32GB of LPDDR5X-9600 RAM and a 2TB SSD, but U.S. pricing for those models has yet to be announced.

Asus is positioning the Ascent QN10 primarily as a solution for developers and local artificial intelligence processing, rather than a gaming system. The company provides access to the Qualcomm AI Hub platform, which contains more than 175 optimized AI models and claims to support native and hybrid AI applications, including OpenClaw and Hermes Agent.
