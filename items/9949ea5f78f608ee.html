Labadain-MedQA: A Tetun Medical QA Dataset for Fine-Tuning and Evaluating Large Language Models

arXiv · 2026-11-06 · 论文PDF

Advances in large language models (LLMs) have created opportunities for cross-lingual transfer of medical knowledge from high-resource to low-resource languages to support access to healthcare information. However, medical question-answering (QA) datasets for fine-tuning and evaluating LLMs remain scarce for Tetun. To address this gap, we translated MedAlpaca's Medical Flashcards and WikiDoc Patient Information collections into Tetun using Gemini 2.5 Flash, producing 39,077 medical QA pairs. Translation quality was assessed on 50 randomly sampled QA pairs (100 documents) using automatic metrics against human reference translations and evaluations by two native Tetun-speaking healthcare professionals, focusing on medical accuracy and Tetun fluency. Human translations achieved higher mean ratings than LLM-generated translations for both criteria (4.43/5 vs. 4.02/5), with statistically significant differences (Wilcoxon signed-rank test, p < 0.001).
