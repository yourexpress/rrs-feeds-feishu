Infrastructure-Based Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning with Attention for Highway Ramp Merging

arXiv cs.LG · 2026-11-15 · 论文PDF

注意力基础设施多agent强化学习用于高速匝道汇入

On-ramp merging is a typical safety and throughput problem on highways. Without proper coordination, human drivers and self-interested autonomous vehicles may make risky manoeuvres and cause accidents or congestion. With recent advances in connected autonomous vehicles (CAVs) and multi-agent reinforcement learning (MARL), vehicles can be trained to coordinate for safe and efficient merging. However, in dense traffic, forming an effective observation of the traffic state is challenging. For example, observing only the neighbouring vehicles may lead to locally optimal decisions, while observing the entire traffic can introduce much irrelevant information. In this paper, we propose a transformer-based MARL vehicle control model to address these problems. A roadside infrastructure controller receives the states of all CAVs via vehicle-to-infrastructure (V2I) communication and sends control commands back to them. An attention-based observation encoder is designed where each vehicle can filter the relevant vehicle information from the global traffic state.
