Blockchain-IoT Convergence for Spatiotemporal Data Systems: Challenges, Solutions, and Research Gaps

arXiv · 2026-11-24 · 论文PDF

Blockchain in conjunction with the Internet of Things (IoT) is changing the management of spatiotemporal data in a way that has never been possible before now. In the process of gathering geospatial and time-stamped data from a large network of IoT devices, data authenticity, accuracy, and timely processing are major issues. Spatiotemporal indexing in a blockchain environment is necessary for ensure quick data retrieval; supporting real time data processing requirement. Blockchain with its decentralized consensus mechanism is a possible solution for preventing data fabrication, unauthorized access, and single point of failure in IoT based spatiotemporal applications. This paper aims to describe how Blockchain and IoT indexing can be combined to enhance the spatiotemporal data management (specifically indexing and querying of data). Literature solutions will be reviewed and compared to highlight challenges and insights for future blockchain-integrated IoT in resilient spatiotemporal data ecosystems.
