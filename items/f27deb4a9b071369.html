Many applications of synthetic time series require a series that is much longer than the windows on which a generative model is trained. Such a series can be assembled from a pool of short generated windows, which requires deciding which windows are taken, in what order, and how their seams are processed, or it can be produced directly by a long-series generator. This study presents MarathonTS, to our knowledge the first open benchmark that compares both families under one protocol. Every method produces a series of the same length on 9 open hourly datasets with 10 seeds. The series is evaluated by its fidelity to the real data (83 metrics in 12 groups, against three real references: train, validation and an out-of-time split), by its utility (models are trained on the synthetic series only and tested on real data, TSTR against TRTR, in 14 classification and regression tasks), by its memorisation of the training data and by its computational cost.
