Spatially local mini-batching for implicit neural representations

arXiv cs.LG · 2026-11-17 · 论文PDF

隐式神经表示的局部空间小批量

Neural surrogates based on implicit neural representations (INRs) are typically trained on mini-batches spread over the whole domain, since a generic INR loses accuracy when every point of a batch lies in one small region. We show that low-rank neural representations (LRNRs), INRs whose weight matrices are products of shared bases with coefficients emitted by a small hypernetwork, can be trained with spatially local mini-batches. Each step uses the points of one time snapshot of an adaptive-mesh numerical solution inside one small cell, with snapshots and cells drawn according to the refinement of the mesh. We posit that such training is enabled by the structural property of LRNRs, as a local step reaches the shared bases only through the $r$ coefficients of each layer, and keeps one cell from rewriting the network. By a Kolmogorov-width argument, we show that when restricted to a small cell, the hidden states of LRNRs lie close to a low-dimensional subspace, which suggests the local steps only affect low-rank subspaces of the shared bases.
