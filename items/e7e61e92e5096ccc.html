Rethinking Offline On-Policy Distillation: From Scalar Advantages to Distributional Supervision

arXiv cs.LG · 2026-10-15 · 论文PDF

从标量优势到分布监督重思离线on-policy蒸馏

On-policy distillation (OPD) transfers teacher capabilities through token-level feedback on student-generated trajectories, but repeatedly evaluating fresh rollouts with the teacher is computationally expensive. Offline methods such as Lightning OPD reduce this cost by caching recorded tokens' teacher log-probabilities on fixed reference rollouts. By extending the idea to a multi-turn agentic search setup, we find that it consistently underperforms the SFT starting point trained by the same teacher. We empirically observe greater teacher--reference mismatch than in mathematical reasoning and post-training distributional flattening, and theoretically show that scalar advantage supervision on fixed rollouts admits solutions with flattened output distributions under suitable conditions. Motivated by these findings, we propose Distributional On-Policy Distillation (DistOPD), a simple yet effective offline OPD method that retains candidate-token probabilities from both reference and teacher at selected positions and aggregates the remaining parts into a residual token.
