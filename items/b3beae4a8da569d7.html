Where Prefill Expert Reduction Hurts MoE Models: A Few Prompt Tokens Carry Most of the Short-Answer F1 Loss

arXiv · 2026-11-06 · 论文PDF

Running prompt tokens through fewer routed experts during prefill makes mixture-of-experts (MoE) serving cheaper, but what it damages has not been measured. We locate the damage with token-, layer- and attention-path interventions in four MoE families on short-answer question answering. At half the experts the F1 loss is mostly answer form: the model still finds the answer but no longer answers briefly. The loss is made at a few tokens: the two chat-template tokens before the answer in Qwen3-30B-A3B, and the first 8-32 tokens, where the instruction sits, in DeepSeek-V2-Lite. Keeping them at full experts removes most of the F1 loss and about half and a fifth of the increase in answer NLL. Reduction changes the first-token sink statistics by less than 4%, and restoring super experts or massive activations recovers at most a quarter of the loss. We then test placement on real prompts with about 4% of tokens at full experts. Putting them on the task interface keeps F1 within one point of the unmodified model, while the same budget scattered in the documents is about 3 points worse in both prespecified primary tests of this comparison.
