VLMs provide promising solutions to embodied reward design. Most current methods that train VLMs as reward regressors not only require extensive annotations but are also primarily designed for finetuning pretrained policies, and perform less well in the more challenging from-scratch RL setting. This issue stems from a fundamental mismatch: regressing precise rewards is misaligned with what VLMs naturally excel at. We posit that it is more aligned with VLMs' inherent multi-modal VQA capabilities for the models to function as success detectors, and propose VLM success reward via Exploration-Augmented Key-Observation Gating (VLMeg) to well harness VLM for embodied reward design. As the core of VLMeg, the proposed exploration-augmented key-observation gating first determines whether the current RL episode is judgment-worthy based on its similarity to a generated success trajectory. Then, the key observation with the lowest confusion from the judgment-worthy episode will be selected for VLM evaluation as episodic reward.
