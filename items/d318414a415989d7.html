Asymptotic-Preserving Tensor Neural Networks for 3D High-Field Kinetic Equations with Applications to Uncertainty Quantification

arXiv · 2026-11-06 · 论文PDF

Kinetic equations in the high-field regime describe particle transport dominated by electric-field acceleration and collisional relaxation. These equations are widely used in simulations of plasmas and semiconductor devices. Typical models include the Vlasov--Poisson--Fokker--Planck (VPFP) and Boltzmann--Poisson systems. Their high dimensionality and multiscale nature pose two of the major challenges to accurate and efficient numerical solution. To address these challenges, we propose an asymptotic-preserving tensor neural network (APTNN) method. Specifically, to reduce high-dimensional computational costs and memory requirements, we adopt the canonical polyadic (CP) decomposition structure of TNNs to approximate their solutions. We show that the equation residuals retain this decomposition form, allowing high-dimensional loss integrals to be evaluated through combinations of one-dimensional integrals. The resulting computational and storage costs are reduced from exponential to polynomial growth with dimension.
