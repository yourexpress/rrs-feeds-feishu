Symmetry Fingerprinting with Geometric Learnable Invariants Transformers

arXiv cs.LG · 2026-11-15 · 论文PDF

几何可学习不变量Transformer的对称性指纹识别

Geometric deep learning has made available a large variety of structurally equivariant neural network architectures, meaning a particular transformation of their input causes a predictable transformation of their output. But structural equivariance is a very strong inductive bias that can limit performance if chosen poorly. In contrast, choosing an architecture with low geometric bias necessitates data augmentation to obtain correct behavior; extending the training process. We propose a novel neural network architecture that is soft equivariant; we select a set of symmetry groups and during training the network will become equivariant only to those symmetry groups that are appropriate given the training data. After training we can inspect the network to obtain a symmetry fingerprint that will tell us exactly which symmetries ended up being relevant and to what degree. We show how our architecture and the fingerprinting process work in the case of an image classification task.
