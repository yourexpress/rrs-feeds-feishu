Multi-teacher on-policy distillation (MOPD) integrates separately trained domain specialists into a single unified model. Existing methods focus on refining the distillation procedure while initializing the student from a base model or a multi-domain SFT checkpoint, which leaves capability integration largely to distillation itself. Instead, we treat the starting point as a design variable and propose Unified MOPD (Uni-MOPD), which pairs a strong start with a simple recipe: a training-free uniform merge of specialists from the same base model initializes the student, and short MOPD with uniform domain sampling and full-vocabulary reverse KL refines it. Across five multimodal domains and 19 benchmarks, our study establishes three findings. (1) The starting point matters more than subsequent training choices: before any training, the uniform merge already matches the score that the base model reaches after 200 MOPD updates, and after short distillation it matches higher-scoring, search-based starts.
