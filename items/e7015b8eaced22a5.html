SnapShare: Isolation-Preserving Deduplication for Serverless Snapshots

arXiv · 2026-10-09 · 论文PDF

面向serverless快照的保持隔离性去重

The efficiency of serverless computing fundamentally depends on rapid function startup, a challenge increasingly addressed by restoring from virtual machine snapshots. However, because content deduplication techniques can open timing-channel attacks that leak sensitive tenant data, production platforms like AWS Lambda instead enforce strict, zero-sharing isolation between snapshots of different tenants. While secure, this isolation precludes the deduplication of common infrastructure components, wasting per-worker cache capacity and exacerbating cold-start latency through inefficient remote fetching. Our characterization reveals that non-sensitive, shareable infrastructure accounts for roughly 70% of a typical snapshot's contents and, on average, about 50% of its working set. We present SnapShare, a cluster-wide snapshot management system that co-designs storage and caching to exploit this commonality without compromising sensitive data isolation. SnapShare classifies a snapshot page as shareable only when its full content matches that of a page from a provider-approved source; any non-matching content is treated as isolated private content.
