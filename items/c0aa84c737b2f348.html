ParGLU: Partial Gating Improves Language Modeling in MoE Transformers

arXiv cs.LG · 2026-11-16 · 论文PDF

ParGLU：部分门控改进MoE Transformer语言建模

Multiplicative gating improves Transformer feed-forward networks (FFNs), but it reduces intermediate representation width under a fixed parameter budget. This trade-off is particularly relevant to Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) models, where individual experts have limited capacity. We introduce ParGLU, a partially gated FFN that combines SwiGLU and ReLU$^2$ in two parallel branches. ParGLU matches the parameter count and principal matrix computation budget of conventional SwiGLU. We evaluate ParGLU in 30M, 75M, and 152M MoE language models using three random seeds per scale. ParGLU outperforms SwiGLU across all three seeds at 75M and 152M, reducing mean validation NLL by 0.0200 and 0.0249, respectively. Results at 30M are mixed. Controlled ablations show that increasing backbone width or expert width strengthens ParGLU's relative performance. Ablation studies further show that combining SwiGLU and ReLU$^2$ improves performance over either architecture alone. Token-frequency analysis reveals larger improvements on low-frequency tokens.
