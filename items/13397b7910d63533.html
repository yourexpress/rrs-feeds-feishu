Reliability management of industrial fleets depends on early fault warnings, yet lifecycle data across similar assets are highly imbalanced. A few assets accumulate run-to-failure histories, while newly installed or rarely failing assets provide only normal-condition data. This paper asks how diagnostic knowledge can be transferred from a data-rich source asset to a data-scarce target asset when no fault data have been observed on the target. We formulate cross-asset early fault detection as a zero-shot domain adaptation problem, in which zero-shot refers to the absence of target fault samples, and propose a framework that requires only normal data from the target.
