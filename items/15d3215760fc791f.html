FLAMIA: FLAsh MIcroscaling Attention for Fast and Causally Safe Training on Blackwell

arXiv (cs.LG) · 2026-11-02 · 论文PDF

Attention remains a substantial computational cost in Transformer training, particularly at long context lengths. NVIDIA Blackwell GPUs provide high-throughput low-precision matrix multiplication, but exploiting it in attention requires addressing quantization overhead, causal information leakage through shared scales, and numerical inconsistencies between the forward and backward passes. We present FLAMIA, a family of attention kernels for Blackwell with MXFP8 and mixed NVFP4/FP8 training paths. Our design combines token-block scaling that reuses quantized operands across matrix orientations, targeted safeguards against scale-induced leakage, and consistent forward and backward computations. Fused quantization and architecture-specific kernel optimizations reduce overhead, while document-local scaling and workload-aware scheduling support variable-length documents directly in packed layouts without repacking.
