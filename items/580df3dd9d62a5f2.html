PRISM: Validation-Free Online Batch Selection for LLM Fine-Tuning via Preconditioned Conic Ghost Matching

arXiv · 2026-11-20 · 论文PDF

PRISM：免验证在线批选择用于 LLM 微调

Online batch selection lowers the cost of fine-tuning large language models (LLMs) by replacing each candidate batch with a smaller informative subset. Existing selectors typically depend on a held-out validation or proxy set, or else rely on Euclidean facility-location objectives over unpreconditioned gradients whose diameter-shifted similarities are distorted by heavy-tailed gradient norms and cross-layer anisotropy. We introduce PRISM (Preconditioned Ray-Isotropic Submodular Matching), a validation-free online batch selector that aligns candidate gradients in a factored second-moment metric and casts subset construction as conic facility location over positive ray projections. Computing the required pairwise geometry through factored preconditioned ghost inner products avoids materializing per-example parameter gradients, while the conic objective remains normalized, nonnegative, and monotone submodular---guaranteeing a $(1-1/e)$ greedy approximation whose additive error scales with total batch gradient energy rather than the worst-case outlier diameter.
