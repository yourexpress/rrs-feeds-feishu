RiDEx: Reward-Informed Directional Exploration for Diffusion Reinforcement Learning

arXiv cs.LG · 2026-11-17 · 论文PDF

奖励知情方向探索的扩散 RL

Reward-based diffusion post-training updates model parameters from task rewards. While existing approaches improve sampling and policy optimization, they still rely largely on isotropic random perturbations in a high-dimensional latent space for exploration. Such variation often spends the perturbation budget on changes with little influence on the target reward, making exploration inefficient. We study where diffusion models should explore and how to learn from that exploration. We introduce Reward-Informed Directional Exploration (RiDEx), which concentrates local exploration in reward-relevant subspaces of the denoising velocity field. Conditional residuals or differentiable reward gradients propose the subspace, and equal-and-opposite perturbations at the same generation state measure its terminal reward response. The reward difference supplies signed local supervision in the explored coordinates, while all generated images contribute to global DiffusionNFT learning. RiDEx thereby couples exploration-space selection with feedback about the changes it produces.
