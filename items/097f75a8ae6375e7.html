Depth-Linked Residual Tokens for Multimodal Retrieval and Generation

arXiv · 2026-11-19 · 论文PDF

DepThink：深度链接残差令牌用于多模态检索与生成

Iterative architectures such as looped language models improve parameter efficiency and reasoning capacity of transformers, yet their sequential backbone iterations introduce substantial computational latency. Conversely, parallelizable learnable special tokens (such as the register tokens in Vision Transformer) enable efficient single-pass inference, but often suffer from the absence of sequential refinement and exhibit redundant representations across tokens. To address these challenges, we introduce Depth-Linked Residual Tokens (DepThink), an efficient framework designed for multimodal retrieval and generation. Operating within a single pre-filling forward pass, DepThink appends a sequence of K learnable residual tokens whose hidden states are coupled through a lightweight, learnable residual recurrence. This mechanism enables each residual token to progressively capture complementary information missed by preceding tokens. Theoretically, we prove that DepThink provides strictly expanded geometric directional reachability over standard learnable tokens and guarantees a provable one-step descent advantage.
