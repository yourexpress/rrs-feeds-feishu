In the myth, Athena — goddess of wisdom and knowledge — gave humanity the olive

The most capable language models live in someone else’s data center. Using them

means an account, a bill that grows with every word, a usage policy you did not

write — and every prompt, document and line of code leaving your hands. Running

them yourself has meant racks of GPUs that almost nobody owns.

Athena’s Engine exists to change that.

Frontier-class models. On a desk. At a thousand tokens a second.

It runs DeepSeek V4 Flash and Qwen3.8 Flash Next on a single NVIDIA GB10 — a

DGX Spark or an ASUS Ascent GX10 — with the full 262,144-token context and no

cloud account, no per-token bill, and nothing leaving your machine. It is out

today.

Generation, 256 tokens at temperature 0, timed from the first token:

Read that sideways, not down. It is flat. A quarter-million-token session
