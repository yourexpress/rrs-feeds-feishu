Today, we are introducing Seedream 5.5, our next-generation image creation model.

Image creation models are becoming part of professional visual production workflows. What matters is whether they can consistently deliver usable results that meet quality standards and deadlines while staying within budget.

We believe progress in visual intelligence should both expand creative possibilities and make professional production more reliable. This calls for expressive images, accurate content, and control over edits. With that reliability, creative teams can explore more freely and refine their ideas to their full potential.

To meet these needs, Seedream 5.5 brings three key upgrades:

More stable multi-turn editing: Preserve unedited regions at the pixel level and eliminate color shifts in those areas, while reducing detail loss and image degradation across successive edits. This supports continued work on the same image across 100 rounds of editing.

Native RGBA assets with transparent backgrounds: Directly generate images with an alpha channel, including full and partial transparency. The model carefully renders edges and transparent regions, allowing generated elements to enter downstream design workflows as standalone assets without separate background removal or edge cleanup.

More realistic images and clearer text: The model renders lighting, materials, and skin textures more naturally and reduces excessive gloss and overexposure, bringing generated images closer to real photos. Text rendering also improves significantly, with more accurate rendering of complex character shapes and densely arranged small text.
