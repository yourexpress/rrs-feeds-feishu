Verifying and Strengthening Neural Tangent Kernel Theory

arXiv · 2026-11-20 · 论文PDF

Neural Tangent Kernel 理论的 Lean4 机器验证

Writing Neural tangent kernel (NTK) theory explains why very wide neural networks trained by gradient methods converge in the lazy-training regime. Its standard proof combines matrix concentration, random Gram matrices, continuous-time dynamics, spectral perturbation, and a bootstrap argument, but has lacked machine-checked verification. We present a Lean\~4 formalisation for two-layer networks, proving an end-to-end theorem: for a smooth bounded activation (instantiated with $σ=\tanh$), frozen biases, paired $\pm 1$ initialisation, and explicit width conditions, gradient flow exists uniquely with probability at least $1-δ$, and $$ \|r(t)\|^2 \leq \|y\|^2 e^{-λ_0 t/2}. $$ The sole data assumption is population NTK non-degeneracy, $λ_0>0$. Initial coercivity, kernel drift, the movement bootstrap, the residual ODE, and flow existence and uniqueness are formally proved. The formalisation also sharpens the standard analysis in two ways.
