Federated learning (FL) is increasingly used to adapt a pretrained, centrally fine-tuned model to data that remain on user devices, beyond training a model from scratch. In this report, prepared for the 4th Federated Machine Learning International Summer School, we study the privacy and integrity of this approach with a reproducible pipeline. An ImageNet-pretrained MobileNetV4-Conv-Small model is fine-tuned centrally on ten Quick, Draw! classes (97.50% test accuracy) and then adapted with head-only FedAvg over ten non-IID clients, which raises client training accuracy from 90.34% to 91.49% while preserving global accuracy. We evaluate four adversarial scenarios. First, from a single one-step client update, an honest-but-curious server recovers the complete private label set, but its reconstructions follow class prototypes rather than the private drawings (mean SSIM 0.130). Second, from a one-round FedAvg aggregate, label-count recovery falls from 87.5% with one client to 75.0% with five and ten clients, below the 80.0% of an uninformed uniform guess.
