Computationally Efficient Offline Policy Optimization with All-Policy Linear Realizability

arXiv cs.LG · 2026-11-08 · 论文PDF

We study offline policy optimization in finite-horizon Markov decision processes where every deterministic Markov policy has an action-value function linear in known features, with coefficient norm at most $B$. From independent complete trajectories of one unknown Markov behavior policy, and under all-policy occupancy coverage, we give an algorithm with $\widetilde O(\varepsilon^{-2})$ sample dependence and computational complexity polynomial in $\log(2+B)$. The algorithm performs exactly one convex regression per layer. Its stopped-return targets are the values of a single consistent Markov witness policy. A shared reference design and bounded prediction constraints convert fast clipped-regression error into pointwise confidence for the raw linear predictions. Truncated leverage controls the performance loss without requiring empirical covariance approximation at scale $B^{-2}$. Rounding confidence radii upward preserves the exact witness and yields a polynomial-time bit implementation for rational numerical inputs.
