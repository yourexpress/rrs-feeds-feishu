UniPipe: Unified Inference Serving and Fine-Tuning for Deadline-Constrained DNN Surrogate Workflows

arXiv cs.LG · 2026-11-10 · 论文PDF

DNN surrogates are increasingly used to accelerate scientific workflows that must process high-rate sensor streams in near real time, exemplified by Ptychographic imaging at next-generation X-ray light sources. Such surrogates deliver 100-1000x higher throughput than iterative solvers, enabling faster steering of experiments. To preserve surrogate accuracy during an experiment, as the input distribution evolves, state-of-the-art deployments freeze the pretrained surrogate or run a separate CUDA context that fine-tunes concurrently with serving. However, multi-context concurrency causes significant contention on the GPU, inflating both the mean and the variance of inference latency, triggering deadline misses in workflows where sensor data has to be processed within a hard per-unit deadline. To address this challenge, we propose UniPipe, a single-context system that unifies inference serving and fine-tuning of a DNN surrogate on a single edge GPU.
