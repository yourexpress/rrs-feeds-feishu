One Structure Is Enough: Record-Native Tiles for Evolving Sparse Matrices on GPUs

arXiv · 2026-11-23 · 论文PDF / 每日精选

RecTile：GPU 上演进稀疏矩阵的记录原生分片存储，12 字节边记录单数组结构

Sparse matrices that change over time, such as streaming graphs, are usually handled on GPUs with two parts: a packed format such as CSR, ELL, or SELL for computation, and a separate buffer for updates. Periodic maintenance then merges updates, rebuilds data, or changes formats. Our measurements show that this split is often unnecessary. We present RecTile, a tiled storage layer where each tile is kept in one sorted array of 12-byte edge records. For fragmented matrices, with 1 to 21 nonzeros per tile in our workloads, RecTile matches the best format-pinned slab tile at 0.56 to 1.21 times its time. Its tiled layout is also up to 21 times faster than whole-matrix cuSPARSE CSR. Specialized slabs are faster on the patterns they target at medium density, but both approaches converge by 25% density. RecTile stays within 1.1 to 1.6 times of whole-matrix cuSPARSE COO, our strongest static baseline, while COO cannot be updated in place. RecTile also uses no fixed per-tile device metadata for the stored matrix payload, so its tiles are smaller at realistic occupancies.
