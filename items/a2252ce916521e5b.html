FOE-Dreamer: Deployment-Efficient Learning of Cyber Defense Policies in Operational Networks

arXiv cs.LG · 2026-11-11 · 论文PDF

Autonomous cyber defense is widely studied with deep reinforcement learning, but most work trains policies inside simulators because learning requires many trial-and-error interactions. Simulator-based training carries a fidelity gap: trained policies face authentication delays, partial action failures, service dependencies, and user traffic patterns that the simulator did not represent, and degrade on transfer. Training directly in the network being defended removes the gap but makes every interaction slow and costly, so sample efficiency becomes the binding constraint. Model-based reinforcement learning has reduced sample requirements on games and physical robots by orders of magnitude by planning inside a learned world model, but it has not been applied to defenders trained in operational networks. We present FOE-Dreamer (Factored Opponent-Enhanced Dreamer), a model-based reinforcement learning framework that trains a defender online and directly in a network on cloud infrastructure, without a simulator stage.
