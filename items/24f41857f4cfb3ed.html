Large Language Models as Nonlinear Dynamical Systems: The Three Clocks of Depth, Training, and Generation

arXiv cs.LG · 2026-11-16 · 论文PDF

LLM 作为非线性动力系统：深度/训练/生成三时钟

Language models are dynamical systems. A transformer is iterated over its layers, over the steps of its training and over the tokens it generates, and on each of these three clocks it shows behavior that nonlinear dynamics has studied for a century: fixed points, bifurcations, synchronization, chaos and noise-driven escape from potential wells. The two communities rarely meet, so this behavior is often rediscovered under new names. We build a bridge between them. To describe, we condense about 200 papers into the Three-Clock Dictionary, which maps fifteen concepts of nonlinear dynamics onto each clock and marks every correspondence as proved, measured or conjectured, and we show each concept in a mechanical system and in a language model side by side. To explain, we derive reduced-order models: the softmax is a Boltzmann distribution, gradient descent an Euler step on a nonlinear spring, a loss spike a ball thrown out of a stiff valley, attention on the circle a Kuramoto model, and a repetition loop a well that deepens while it is occupied.
