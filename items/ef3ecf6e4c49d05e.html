VISTA: View-Independent micro-Doppler Reconstruction with Multistatic ISAC

arXiv cs.AR · 2026-11-11 · 论文PDF

The micro-Doppler (uD) spectrum provides detailed characteristics of target movement, making it highly valuable for applications such as target classification and human activity recognition. However, traditional Integrated Sensing And Communication (ISAC) systems are highly view-dependent and limited by blind spots and angle dependencies. Existing approaches rely on either configuration-specific solutions or data-driven models. The former results in poor generalization when device deployments or target orientations change, while the latter requires acquiring extensive multi-view datasets, which is time-consuming and not always feasible. To address these limitations, in this work we propose VISTA, a novel method for reconstructing the uD spectrogram in an absolute coordinate frame, yielding view-independent Doppler features that are invariant to the spatial distribution of ISAC nodes. We formulate the uD estimation as a sparse reconstruction problem that jointly leverages measurements from all ISAC receivers.
