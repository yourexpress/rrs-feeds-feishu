Replay, Don't Resample: Off-Policy Consolidation of Long-Horizon RL Experts

arXiv · 2026-11-18 · 论文PDF

长程 RL 专家的 off-policy 巩固：重放而非重采样（替代 MOPD）

Frontier post-training follows a common recipe: train one expert per domain with reinforcement learning (RL), then consolidate the experts into one model via multi-teacher on-policy distillation (MOPD). Such consolidation is expensive: the student samples in every environment again, an expert scores every sampled token, and the student inherits the drift of the final experts. Yet we argue that the experts' RL runs have already produced what consolidation needs: rollouts with their sampling probabilities and rewards, in the order the experts learned from them. We propose multi-expert replay policy distillation (MRPD), which replays these training logs through the student in the experts' order. At each step, the policy gradient is recomputed on these scored trajectories, and the final experts' distributions are distilled at the logged prefixes. Following the experts' own trajectory reduces the training-inference mismatch and keeps the student within the clip range, so the replay is off-policy in origin but quasi-on-policy throughout consolidation.
