Dr. OPD: Learning What to Follow for Optimal On-Policy Distillation of Large Language Models

arXiv / Zhenyu Wang 等 · 2026-09-30 · 论文PDF

Dr. OPD：学习「该跟谁」的最优在策略蒸馏

On-policy distillation (OPD) trains a student on its own generated responses using dense, token-level supervision from a stronger teacher. Vanilla OPD treats all teacher signals equally, assuming that the teacher's supervision is equally important for every token. However, teacher signals at different tokens may have very different effects on the student's performance: some correct important reasoning errors, while others have little effect on the final answer. Motivated by this observation, we introduce Dr. OPD (OPD Done Right), which defines the optimal weighted OPD to maximize the student's performance. We formulate Dr. OPD as a bilevel optimization problem in which the student learns from weighted teacher supervision, while the weights are selected to maximize the expected reward of the resulting student. To solve Dr. OPD, we develop an efficient iterative solver that updates the token weights and student policy alternatively. At each round, it updates weights in closed form and then takes one gradient step on the resulting weighted OPD objective.
