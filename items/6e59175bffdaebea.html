Scaling Laws for Vision-Language Fusion

arXiv cs.LG · 2026-11-17 · 论文PDF

视觉-语言融合的缩放定律

Vision-language models (VLMs) have emerged as central foundation models combining multimodal perception with text comprehension. While VLMs are traditionally built via late fusion by continued pre-training an off-the-shelf language model on multimodal (image-text) data, mixing modalities from the start of pre-training (early fusion) has recently gained traction. However, the fundamental trade-offs between these paradigms remain poorly understood, resulting in expensive and heuristic-driven training recipes. In this paper, we systematically analyze the learning dynamics of these multimodal pre-training processes, showing that validation losses during vision-language fusion can be modeled by separating the base learning trajectory, where different data types contribute at distinct rates, from a distribution shift driven by the competition between modalities. We derive a scaling law that unifiedly describes these dynamics, enabling accurate loss predictions across mixture ratios, training steps, and model sizes.
