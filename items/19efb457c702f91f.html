Testyard Benchmarks: a Dynamic RISC-V-based Benchmark Framework including DfT insertion, Structural and Functional Test Flows

动态 RISC-V 基准框架（含 DfT 插入与结构/功能测试流）

The main challenge of modern computing manufacturing is to produce silicon products capable of operating under the worst possible conditions, both in data centers and under stringent functional safety constraints. These requirements impose exceptionally high quality standards on silicon vendors, while testing costs keep escalating as transistors continue to shrink. Evaluating new testing strategies, however, presents a dual challenge for researchers. Standard academic benchmarks, such as the ITC-99 suite, remain valuable for fast test prototyping, but they lack the structural complexity of modern VLSI devices. Assessing the true computational effort of academic methodologies on industrial scale designs is equally difficult without accessible and scalable models. A further obstacle is the absence of a unified framework able to abstract the commands imposed by each EDA tool and the configurations tied to each technology library, forcing researchers to spend time adapting and debugging fragile scripts.
