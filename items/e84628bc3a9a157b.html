Federated versus Centralized PV Capacity-Factor Forecasting: A Non-Inferiority Study Across Seven Stations in North Macedonia

arXiv cs.LG · 2026-11-14 · 论文PDF

联邦与集中式光伏容量因子预测非劣性研究

Background: Photovoltaic (PV) forecasts inform energy commitments by PV operators and aggregators and guide charge-discharge scheduling in solar-plus-storage systems. Federated learning enables collaborative model training through model updates when local operators cannot pool raw operational data. This study evaluated whether federated training was non-inferior to centralized training for PV capacity-factor prediction across seven stations in North Macedonia. A common multilayer perceptron was used in an offline forecasting comparison with a 24-hour timestamp lag. Exploratory validation compared local, centralized, Federated Averaging, and Federated Proximal (FedProx) training. FedProx (E = 1, mu = 1) was selected for a confirmatory comparison under a protocol fixed before test evaluation. The comparison used past-only offline inputs, five paired training seeds, macro-averaged mean absolute error (MAE), a prespecified non-inferiority margin of 0.005, and a paired day bootstrap with 10,000 repetitions over 49 test dates.
