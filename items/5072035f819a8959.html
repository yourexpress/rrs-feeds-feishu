Dissecting Geometric Constraints in Grokking: Interaction-Dependent Acceleration in Modular Arithmetic

arXiv (cs.LG) · 2026-11-02 · 论文PDF

Spherical transformer configurations can accelerate grokking through simultaneous modifications of several model properties. The present study investigated the contributions of these modifications in the published Sphere+Fourier configuration using 960 controlled modular-arithmetic runs. A complete factorial analysis varied residual projection, readout weight normalization, logit scale, and weight decay. Across four matched settings with weight decay, the successful-run mean crossing times without projection were 8-37 times those observed with projection. However, residual projection was insufficient to induce grokking without additional interventions. Various other unprojected configurations also generalized. Since projection altered the magnitude entering the readout, these comparisons could not separate geometry from output scale. A separate 100-run fixed-horizon study demonstrated faster first crossing for the complete configuration across five train/test partitions. Faster crossing did not necessarily correspond to greater persistence over extended training. Control experiments demonstrated rapid grokking without Fourier initialization.
