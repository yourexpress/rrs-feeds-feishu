RGDM: Robust Graph Diffusion Model

RGDM：鲁棒图扩散模型，从对抗损坏图学习鲁棒生成器

Diffusion models represent a powerful class of generative models known for their solid theoretical foundations and remarkable performance across diverse tasks and domains. Existing node level diffusion methods synthesize nodes and edges within a graph, but are not designed to learn a robust generator from adversarially corrupted graphs. In this study, we introduce the Robust Graph Diffusion Model (RGDM), designed to generate labeled synthetic graph structures consisting of nodes and edges that integrate seamlessly into a given graph. The RGDM consists of a Robust Graph Autoencoder (RGAE) and a Latent Diffusion Model (LDM). Leveraging an edge selection mechanism and an innovative low-rank regularization on the latent feature, the RGDM produces clean and high-quality synthetic graph structures, even when trained on graphs subject to adversarial attacks.
