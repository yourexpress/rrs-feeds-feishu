DUEL: Exact Likelihood Reshapes Masked Diffusion Scaling Laws and Beyond

arXiv · 2026-11-06 · 论文PDF

Masked diffusion models (MDMs) are trained to predict tokens in every order but generate in one. As such, MDM likelihood is an intractable sum over all orders. The evidence lower bound (ELBO) is used as a proxy for likelihood, but it is a loose bound and assumes positions are unmasked uniformly at random. Frontier MDMs, however, select positions with deterministic rules such as confidence. To resolve this mismatch we introduce DUEL, a framework that unifies common deterministic MDM samplers. DUEL computes the likelihood exactly, under the sampler that is actually used during generation. DUEL is a simple plug-and-play algorithm applicable to any pretrained MDM with no retraining. To compute the likelihood of a text sequence, simply replay generation, reveal the true tokens instead of sampled ones, and sum their log-probabilities. Evaluating with DUEL instead of the ELBO changes what we conclude about MDMs. The perplexity gap between MDMs and autoregressive (AR) models shrinks by up to 32% in-domain and 82% zero-shot.
