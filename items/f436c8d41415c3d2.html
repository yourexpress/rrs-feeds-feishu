Quaternion-Tucker ZigZag Networks: Halving Transformer Backbone Parameters and Enabling Exact 2D Zero-Loss Continual Scaling

Quaternion-Tucker ZigZag 网络：Transformer 骨干参数减半并支持零损失持续缩放

Standard Transformers allocate independent dense weight matrices to every feed-forward network (FFN) layer, coupling parameters linearly with depth (O(L)) and discarding prior compute when scaling capacity. Conversely, weight-tied Looped Transformers decouple depth from parameters (O(1)) but enforce time-homogeneous updates. We formulate the L-layer FFN stack as a 3D Tensor Cuboid and introduce QuatTucker-ZigZagGPT, unifying: (1) 4D Quaternion Hamilton GEMMs in bfloat16 (4x FFN compression), (2) 64x64 Block-Diagonal Tucker SwiGLU Cores for depth-specific rotations over a shared basis, (3) Braided ZigZag Diamond Routing (L0 -> L2 -> L1 -> L3) for zero-FLOP top-down refinement, and (4) Exact 2D Zero-Loss Continual Scaling in both width and depth. We also introduce Annealed Gated Bounce-Back Scaffolding (GatedBounce), which self-distills to 0.0% inference overhead. Across algorithmic tasks (8-bit Parity, 56-seed routing ablations, Mod-53 Grokking) and language modeling (TinyStories, FineWeb-Edu), our model outperforms standard Transformers at 2x-7.6x compression.
