Dual Representation Enhanced Diffusion Policy for Flexible Job Shop Scheduling

arXiv cs.LG · 2026-11-11 · 论文PDF

The Flexible Job-Shop Scheduling Problem (FJSP) is a fundamental and challenging optimization problem in smart manufacturing. Although recent deep reinforcement learning (DRL) approaches can learn effective scheduling policies through interaction with the environment, their performance often degrades as problem scale and processing-time uncertainty increase. In this paper, we propose DredRL, a dual representation enhanced diffusion-policy reinforcement learning scheduler. DredRL first constructs enhanced dual representations of operations and machines through self- and cross-attention mechanisms to capture both intra-entity and inter-entity dependencies. A diffusion model is further employed as the policy network, generating scheduling decisions through iterative denoising conditioned on the encoded state to enhance policy expressiveness and robustness. Extensive experiments on synthetic, public, large-scale, and stochastic FJSP benchmarks demonstrate that DredRL outperforms state-of-the-art learning-based schedulers across most configurations.
