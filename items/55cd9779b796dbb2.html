Fast Distributed $(Δ+ 1)$-Edge Coloring in Bipartite Graphs

We show that, on bipartite $n$-vertex graphs in the LOCAL model of distributed computation, $(Δ+ 1)$-edge coloring can be solved deterministically in $O(f(Δ) \log n)$ rounds for some function $f$. In particular this implies a deterministic $O(\log n)$-round upper bound for $(Δ+ 1)$-edge coloring on bipartite constant-degree graphs. Our results improve on the previous state-of-the-art bounds (which hold already on general graphs) whose $n$-dependencies are $\widetilde{O}(\log^5 n)$ rounds deterministically and $\widetilde{O}(\log^2 n)$ rounds randomized [Bernshteyn and Dhawan, J.\ Comb.\ Theory 2025]. In fact, we obtain the aforementioned results already for a more restrictive version of $(Δ+ 1)$-edge coloring that corresponds to a \emph{local-palette} strengthening of Vizing's theorem shown recently to hold [Christiansen, STOC 2023]. In this version, every edge $\{u, v\}$ has to be colored with a color from $\{1, \dots, \max(\operatorname{deg}(u), \operatorname{deg}(v))+ 1\}$. For all of our results, the vertices do not need to know on which side of the bipartition they lie---the structural promise of bipartiteness suffices.
