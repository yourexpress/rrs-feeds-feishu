A 3D-Driven Framework for Moving-Camera Perspective-to-360{\deg} Video Generation

arXiv · 2026-11-06 · 论文PDF

Turning a moving-camera perspective video into a 360° video means generating content outside the input frame under several constraints: observations revealed over time must be preserved, unseen regions plausibly completed, and the resulting scene kept consistent as the camera viewpoint changes. Existing methods assign these different requirements to a single conditional video model, requiring them to be jointly learned from panoramic video data. We instead present a modular, 3D-driven framework that distributes these three responsibilities across specialized components, all connected through a shared scene representation. Input observations are reconstructed explicitly in 3D, while a pretrained panoramic image model completes unobserved content from a selected panoramic render. This generated content is lifted into the shared 3D scene, where it remains spatially anchored across camera viewpoints. Without requiring any additional panoramic-video training, our framework achieves state-of-the-art input-view fidelity, perceptual quality, and geometric consistency on a static-scene subset of the Argus evaluation set.
