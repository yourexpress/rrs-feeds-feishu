Adaptive Quantization Preserves Fast Rates for TD Learning over Rate-Limited Channels

arXiv cs.LG · 2026-11-09 · 论文PDF

Motivated by reinforcement learning subject to communication constraints, we study a policy evaluation problem with linear function approximation over a rate-limited channel. In our setup, an agent interacting with an unknown environment (modeled as a Markov Decision Process) transmits quantized estimates of a temporal-difference (TD) learning update direction to a server over a finite-capacity channel. Our goal is to investigate to what extent one can preserve the statistical convergence rates achievable when the channel has infinite capacity. To that end, we propose a new algorithm called Batched Adaptively Quantized TD (BAQ-TD), which uses batching to reduce the variance of the transmitted information, and adaptive quantization to encode innovations. We prove a high-probability finite-time error bound for BAQ-TD, and show that, above a certain finite channel capacity, BAQ-TD preserves the convergence rate of unquantized TD up to logarithmic factors.
