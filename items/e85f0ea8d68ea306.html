Prediction-Powered LLM Ranking with Adaptively Collected Human Feedback

arXiv cs.LG · 2026-11-09 · 论文PDF

Ranking large language models (LLMs) through human evaluations is essential for downstream decision-making. Because human feedback is expensive, evaluation platforms often collect it adaptively, prioritizing informative model comparisons using previously observed feedback. To further improve precision with limited human feedback, evaluators can draw on abundant unrated responses and relatively inexpensive LLM judges; however, judge bias and adaptive dependence challenge standard i.i.d. inference frameworks. We propose Prediction-Powered Ranking (PP-Rank), a framework that combines LLM-judge predictions with human feedback to improve precision while providing valid inference under adaptive sampling. Data-driven prediction weights minimize the asymptotic covariance trace within the proposed class. In theory, we establish asymptotic normality for general evaluation targets and simultaneous ranking inference. A joint martingale analysis handles dependence between adaptive human feedback and auxiliary data.
