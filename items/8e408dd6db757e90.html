Quota-Based Aging for Prediction-Guided Scheduling in LLM Serving

QBA：配额制老化策略，为预测引导的短优先调度防止长请求饥饿，轻量可部署。

Large language model (LLM) serving systems use token-level continuous batching to improve resource utilization, but heterogeneous response lengths complicate request scheduling. Prediction-guided short-first scheduling reduces average latency but can severely delay long requests, while unrestricted aging may over-promote delayed long requests under congestion. We propose Quota-Based Aging (QBA), a lightweight policy that combines predicted short-first admission with quota-bounded, debt-enforced promotion of aged predicted-long requests. We also use a reduced M/G/n queueing surrogate, calibrated from simulator traces, to explain first-order mean-latency trends. QBA is evaluated using a serving-aware event-driven simulator that models decode-priority batching, chunked prefill, token-budget constraints, and KV-cache-safe admission. We compare QBA with FCFS, predicted short-first, and aggressive aging under asymmetric prediction errors on synthetic and production-derived workloads. The evaluation considers queueing delay, throughput, utilization, and class-conditional tail performance.
