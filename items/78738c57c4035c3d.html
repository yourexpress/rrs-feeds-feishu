Floorplanning with rectilinear soft modules improves area utilization and wirelength by allowing modules to adapt to rectilinear polygon (RPoly) shapes. Existing methods typically construct rectilinear polygons through incremental refinement after obtaining a rectangular global solution, which confines polygon exploration to the neighborhood induced by the initial floorplan. This paper presents RPolyPlan, a unified framework for outline-free and fixed-outline floorplanning that directly incorporates continuously deformable rectilinear polygons into global floorplanning. To enable direct polygon optimization, we introduce a structured continuous representation and a differentiable polygon shaping model that dynamically optimizes rectilinear module shapes based on overlaps, making continuous shape optimization feasible for gradient-based global floorplanning. An augmented Lagrangian formulation robustly handles the coupled inequality constraints, provides theoretical convergence guarantees, and reduces dependence on manual penalty-parameter tuning.
