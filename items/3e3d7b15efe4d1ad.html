PhysRF-WorldJEPA: Transforming Heterogeneous Large Language Models into Manifold-Decoupled Millimeter-Wave Radar World Models

arXiv · 2026-11-24 · 论文PDF

Adapting Large Language Models (LLMs) to millimeter-wave (mmWave) radar enables privacy-preserving, all-weather physical scene understanding across darkness, smoke, and walls. However, standard instruction tuning treats continuous radar streams as static text-like tokens, causing near-random global radar-to-text retrieval and severe physical drift under sensor blackouts. Conversely, coupling pairwise contrastive losses (e.g., SigLIP) directly into the shared backbone improves retrieval but injects $O(B^2)$ repulsive gradients that fracture continuous coordinate manifolds ($R^2 < 0$). We present PhysRF-WorldJEPA, a manifold-decoupled Joint-Embedding Predictive Architecture (JEPA) that separates continuous electromagnetic state prediction from cross-modal language alignment. An unattenuated 1.0x clock anchor and parallel dual-scale RF encoder feed a lean ~3% LoRA LLM trained with two-step state-space EMA-JEPA (Denoise -> Rollout) and sample-axis Batch-VICReg to predict +3.0 s physical states; a stop-gradient alignment head then maps frozen prompt states onto the text hypersphere with false-negative masking.
