Private and Distributionally Robust Federated Multiclass Classification

arXiv · 2026-10-09 · 论文PDF

私有且分布鲁棒的联邦多类分类

Fault diagnosis in asset-intensive industries requires multiclass models trained on noisy, sensitive data collected across geographically distributed client sites. Federated learning enables collaborative training without centralizing data, but most approaches do not provide formal privacy guarantees for individual records stored at client sites and can degrade under label noise. To address these limitations, we develop a differentially private, distributionally robust framework for federated multiclass classification that provides sample-level privacy for records stored at each client. We build on a federated Wasserstein distributionally robust multiclass objective with the Crammer-Singer loss, solved using an Alternating Direction Method of Multipliers (ADMM)-based algorithm. To protect the released client updates, our method injects calibrated Gaussian noise into the proximal linearized client updates of both the local model and the local distributionally robust variable before aggregation at the central server, and we quantify the cumulative privacy loss using the moments accountant.
