HiddenObjects: Scalable Spatial Prior Extraction for Object Placement from Diffusion Models

arXiv · 2026-11-06 · 论文PDF

We learn explicit, class-conditioned spatial priors for object placement in natural scenes by distilling placement knowledge from text-conditioned diffusion models. Prior methods rely on sparse manual annotations or on inpainting object-removal pipelines, whose artifacts promote shortcut learning. To address these limitations, we propose a scalable pipeline that evaluates dense candidate placements by inserting objects into clean backgrounds. This produces HiddenObjects, a dataset of 27M bounding-box annotations (2M positives) across 27K diverse scenes and object categories. Human evaluation prefers our image editing composites over those guided by sparse annotations in 65.6% of comparisons. We distill the priors into a lightweight placement model that achieves the best accuracy among dedicated baselines and zero-shot vision-language models.
