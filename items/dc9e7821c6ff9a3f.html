HiPPO Functional Memory: Coordinate-Queryable Attention for Irregular Time Series

arXiv · 2026-11-21 · 论文PDF

Irregular time series are recorded at nonuniform times and are often queried at other times: between observations, after the last one, or on a regular grid. Continuous-time models answer such queries by integrating latent dynamics, and time-aware attention models answer them by revisiting the observation tokens. We introduce HiPPO Functional Memory (HiPPO-FM), an attention operator that writes an irregular history into a rank-M function space with explicit time coordinates. Masked self-attention first exchanges content among the observations; the updated values are then contracted against kernel-smoothed HiPPO-Legendre features and solved through a regularized Gram matrix, so the retained coefficients define a function that is read at any time through the same features. The write and read have the form of an interdomain sparse Gaussian-process mean: every readout lies in an M-dimensional reproducing-kernel subspace, and a pointwise bound separates the approximation error of this subspace from the error of the learned memory.
