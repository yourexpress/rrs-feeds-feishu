Dr. No: Feature Steering for Abstention in Vision-Language Models

arXiv (cs.LG) · 2026-11-02 · 论文PDF

Dr. No：VLM 中特征引导的弃权机制，识别并控制内部机制实现高可信弃权

Large foundation models often answer even when the available visual or textual evidence is insufficient to support a reliable conclusion. This failure to abstain makes them hard to trust in high-stakes or information-seeking settings, where a confident but unsupported answer can be worse than no answer at all. It also raises a natural question: can the internal mechanisms behind this behaviour be identified and controlled at inference time? We present Dr. No, a feature-steering approach that mines sparse autoencoder (SAE) features associated with abstention and commitment, then modulates a small set of them during the prefill pass, without retraining the base model. Dr. No is evaluated on LLaVA-1.6 Mistral 7B across multimodal abstention benchmarks, including UNK-VQA and MM-UPD, and additionally benchmarked on language reasoning tasks using Qwen3.5-2B on AbstentionBench subsets. Against multiple uncertainty-based abstention baselines, feature steering achieves a more favourable balance between correct answering and appropriate abstention than post-hoc rejection methods.
