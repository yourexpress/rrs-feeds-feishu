Slaying the Hydra: Interaction-Aware Circuit Discovery in Language Models

arXiv cs.LG · 2026-10-06 · 论文PDF

交互感知的语言模型回路发现

Localizing behavior to individual components of a language model is a central goal of mechanistic interpretability. However, scoring components one at a time misses context-dependent effects: a primary component can inhibit the activation of a backup, leading to issues with ranking components. Actual causality studies the structure of such interactions via witnesses: variables that provide contextual information to resolve interaction terms. However, estimation with witnesses typically requires combinatorial enumeration and is infeasible in practice. We introduce the witness-integrated set effect (WISE), a family of causal estimands that build on the witness mechanism while taking expectations over sets of causes and witnesses to remain computationally feasible. Building on this approach, we introduce JuntaLearner, a gradient-based circuit discovery method that learns to rank components by their causal impact across varying-sized sets of components and witnesses.
