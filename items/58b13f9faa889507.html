Deviation-Aware Pretraining for Cardiovascular Disease Detection from Electrocardiograms

arXiv cs.LG · 2026-11-10 · 论文PDF

Electrocardiogram (ECG) signals are critical for cardiovascular disease (CVD) detection. Most current deep learning models rely on supervised, task-specific training, which limits their ability to generalize across diseases, patient populations, and acquisition settings. This paper presents a self-supervised ECG feature extraction framework that learns from nearly one million unlabeled 12-lead ECG recordings. The framework includes two modules: Deviation Localization Embedding (DLE), which detects waveform-level deviations from normal patterns, and Association Reconstruction Embedding (ARE), which models cross-lead associations through deviation-guided reconstruction to capture abnormal patterns expressed across multiple leads. These modules enable the framework to capture subtle deviation-related patterns and their cross-lead context. The framework achieves up to 6.5% AUROC and 12.8% F1 improvements over prior methods on multiple public datasets, even without additional fine-tuning the pretrained feature extractor.
