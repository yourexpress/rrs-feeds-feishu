Knowledge-Guided Local-to-Global Anomaly Learning for Longitudinal Medication Trajectories

arXiv cs.LG · 2026-11-14 · 论文PDF

知识引导的纵向用药轨迹局部到全局异常学习

Detecting suspicious deviations in longitudinal medication trajectories requires distinguishing knowledge-consistent treatment variations from anomalous changes and linking local deviations to account-level decisions. We propose a knowledge-guided local-to-global (L2G) anomaly learning framework that addresses both challenges. It constructs proxy supervision at three granularities: medication occurrences, treatment episodes, and contiguous visit segments. Knowledge-consistent medication and episode replacements provide plausible negatives, while cross-category and cross-account perturbations provide suspicious proxies. A medication-multiset encoder and causal visit-sequence encoder feed a shared scoring head that produces visit-level anomaly logits. Top-\(α\) aggregation derives account-level scores from these logits, while account-level classification and class-balanced visit-localization objectives jointly optimize the same visit-level evidence. Experiments on two city cohorts compare L2G with native and proxy-adapted anomaly methods. On City-A, L2G outperforms the strongest comparators.
