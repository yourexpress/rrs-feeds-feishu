NOMAD-EEG: Native Omni-Montage Adaptive Diffusion

arXiv(cs.LG) · 2026-10-26 · 论文PDF

Purpose.

Purpose. Electroencephalography (EEG) montages, channel counts and recording lengths vary widely, but most EEG foundation models are bound to the channel set and input length they were trained on. We aimed to build a generative EEG foundation model independent of the number, identity and order of electrodes and of the input length. Methods. NOMAD-EEG is a 12.0 M-parameter diffusion model that alternates state-space layers along time, without patches, with attention across electrodes identified only by their 3D scalp position. It was pre-trained on 147 h of unlabelled EEG from a single corpus and evaluated with frozen linear probes on ten datasets with 1 to 64 electrodes against seven foundation models run under one protocol, and on two unseen populations.
