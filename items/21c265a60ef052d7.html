QA-CMIF:Accurate and Lightweight Cross-Modal Latent Imputation and Fusion for Fault-Tolerant sEMG-Inertial Hand Gesture Recognition

arXiv cs.LG · 2026-11-15 · 论文PDF

QA-CMIF：容错sEMG-惯性手势的轻量跨模态潜变量填补融合

Fusing surface electromyography (sEMG) with inertial measurements is widely used for myoelectric prosthesis control and wearable human-machine interfaces. In daily wear, however, socket micro-displacement, electrode lift-off, and wire failures often corrupt an unknown subset of sEMG channels through burst artifacts, lift-off shocks, or open-circuit dropouts. Existing fusion networks mix all channels in the first convolutional layer, so a few corrupted electrodes contaminate the entire latent representation, whereas gate-only attenuation of corrupted channels discards the co-activation cues needed to separate fine-grained gestures. We propose QA-CMIF, a quality-aware cross-modal latent imputation and fusion framework that encodes each sEMG channel independently, scores its reliability with a soft gate, and protects intact channels via a confidence-weighted residual bypass, while a cross-attention imputer repairs degraded channels in latent space using the inertial stream and remaining reliable sEMG channels under a bad-channel alignment loss.
