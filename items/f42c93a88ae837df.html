Accurate day-ahead wind-power production forecasting is increasingly important for energy system operations, market activity and balancing Great Britain's grid as wind generation grows. In this study, we train multi-site deep neural networks (DNNs) using pooled historical production and reanalysis weather data from 88 onshore wind Balancing Mechanism Units (BMUs). Forecasts are produced at 09:00 UK time for the following midnight-to-midnight delivery day, matching NESO's operational day-ahead forecasting schedule. We use reanalysis weather as future covariates during training and numerical weather prediction (NWP) forecasts during inference, extending training beyond the available historical NWP archive. Our multi-site recurrent neural network outperforms site-specific RNNs and a multi-site XGBoost model, while processing the full future-weather trajectory for the forecast horizon further improves accuracy relative to hour-aligned approaches.
