FG-GraphBench: Benchmarking and Enhancing Fine-Grained Visual Understanding with Multimodal Scene Graphs

Fine-grained visual understanding requires multimodal large language models (MLLMs) to recognize small visual entities and understand their relationships. Scene graphs offer a natural way to represent this information, organizing visual entities as nodes and their relationships as edges. However, we identify two challenges: existing scene graph benchmarks provide insufficient coverage of fine-grained entities and the relationships among them, and existing methods for fine-grained visual understanding have yet to effectively leverage such graph-structured information in MLLMs. To address these challenges, we introduce Fine-Grained Multimodal Scene Graph Benchmark (FG-GraphBench) and FiGRA. FG-GraphBench is a benchmark comprising three complementary tasks: fine-grained visual question answering (FG-QA), image captioning (FG-Caption), and image description generation (FG-Description). We develop a two-stage construction pipeline that discovers and grounds fine-grained entities and establishes their relations to construct the fine-grained multimodal scene graphs (MSGs) in FG-GraphBench.
