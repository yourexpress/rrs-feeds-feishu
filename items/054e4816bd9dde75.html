LLM-Assisted Multi-Fidelity Bayesian Optimization: Fusing Expert Knowledge with Numerical Data

arXiv · 2026-11-18 · 论文PDF

LLM 辅助多保真贝叶斯优化：融合专家知识与数值数据

Multi-fidelity Bayesian optimization (MFBO) reduces engineering design cost by combining inexpensive low-fidelity (LF) models with a few accurate high-fidelity (HF) evaluations and sequentially selecting designs to evaluate. However, when HF data are scarce, the LF-HF discrepancy is difficult to learn from numerical data alone, especially when physics omitted in the LF model makes it design-dependent. Yet even without sufficient data, domain experts often know which physics the LF model omits and under which conditions its effect grows. Existing multi-fidelity (MF) models cannot use such qualitative knowledge directly, but large language models (LLMs) can interpret it and compare designs accordingly. The proposed LLM-Assisted MFBO adds a knowledge basis to the conventional MF model: from an expert description of the omitted physics, an LLM judges which of two designs requires the larger signed residual correction, and a pairwise Gaussian process converts these judgments into the basis. HF data calibrate its contribution and sign, so the knowledge is not trusted blindly.
