Guarded Compression of Adam Optimizer State with Shared-Gradient Bounds

arXiv (cs.LG) · 2026-10-31 · 论文PDF

Adam keeps two FP32 moments per parameter, twice the bytes of the FP32 weights, yet compressing them is risky: the update divides by the square root of the second moment, so small reconstruction errors can change the restored computation substantially. We study guarded moment compression, in which a coordinate's decoded moments are accepted only if they reproduce the native FP32 Adam update to within a tolerance $τ$, in units of the learning rate, for every gradient in a clipped domain; all other coordinates are stored exactly. The natural certificate, which ranges each update separately over gradient intervals, fails: on one million trained OPT-125M coordinates it escapes 98.9% of coordinates and the payload grows to 126.6% of raw FP32. Our key observation is that both updates consume the same gradient and parameter. Bounding their difference directly, with FP32 rounding-error allowances and a fixed mixed linear-logarithmic gradient partition, cuts escapes to 0.53% and the payload to 28.3% at $τ=0.01$.
