Jointly Optimizing Memory, Computing and Offloading for Energy-Efficient DNN Inference

arXiv cs.LG · 2026-11-08 · 论文PDF

In edge intelligence systems, computation offloading and dynamic voltage and frequency scaling (DVFS) are widely used to improve energy efficiency. However, as applications like deep neural network (DNN) inference evolve in complexity, their demands for memory resources have progressively increased, establishing memory as a critical bottleneck that impacts both latency and energy consumption. In this paper, we propose a joint optimization scheme integrating memory, computing and offloading for energy-efficient DNN inference. We formulate the energy minimization problem under deadline constraints as a mixed-integer nonlinear programming (MINLP) problem, where the main challenge arises from the coupling between offloading and resource optimization. To address this issue, we decompose the original problem into two subproblems under a fixed offloading policy. The local subproblem is transformed into an equivalent geometric programming (GP) problem to derive the optimal memory frequency and computing frequency, while the edge subproblem is solved via convex optimization.
