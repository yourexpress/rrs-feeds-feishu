LASH-PAT: Deadlock-Free Adaptive Shortest-Path Routing for Inter-Chiplet Interconnects

arXiv cs.AR · 2026-11-17 · 论文PDF

片间互连的无死锁自适应最短路径路由

With the slowdown of transistor scaling, chipletbased systems, and in particular chiplet-based wafer-scale integration, have emerged as a promising approach to sustain growth in computing capability per cost for modern processors and accelerators. In recent years, many specialized inter-chiplet interconnect topologies have been proposed. In these topologies, the simple, well-established, and deadlock-free dimension-order routing scheme is not applicable due to its reliance on regular mesh structures. In this work, we study routing in these recently proposed inter-chiplet interconnect topologies by categorizing and evaluating nine deadlock-free routing functions for irregular topologies known from data center networking in this new context. This reveals a trade-off between turn-based routing, which enables path diversity, and layered routing, which ensures shortest-path delivery. To resolve this trade-off, we propose LASH-PAT, a routing function that extends layered shortestpath routing with adaptive path diversity.
