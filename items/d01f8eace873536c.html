SP-Allreduce: Symmetric Tree Schedules for Tori and Beyond

arXiv · 2026-10-14 · 论文PDF / 每日精选

SP-Allreduce：d 维环面拓扑的对称树 allreduce 调度，最短路径路由实现完全带宽最优。

Allreduce is a fundamental communication primitive in large-scale distributed training, and torus interconnects remain widely deployed in high-performance systems. We introduce SP-allreduce, a new allreduce algorithm for $d$-dimensional tori that exploits the symmetry of the topology and routes only along shortest paths. We call an allreduce algorithm \emph{fully bandwidth-optimal} if it incurs no bandwidth overhead, no multihop-induced traffic inflation, and achieves perfect per-round load balance. SP-allreduce is based on a family of carefully symmetrized shortest-path trees that guarantees full bandwidth optimality in the $α$--$β$ model. We prove that it uses the minimum possible number of rounds among all fully bandwidth-optimal allreduce algorithms on $d$-dimensional tori, and therefore the minimum running time in this class. In this sense, SP-allreduce refines Bucket allreduce: it preserves Bucket's key strengths while reducing the number of rounds by a factor of two. We further extend our approach to broader symmetric topologies and to communication trees of prescribed depths.
