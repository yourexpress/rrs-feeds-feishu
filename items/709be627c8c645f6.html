Confidence-Based Materials Data Quality Assessment via Domain Knowledge and Graph-Structured Learning

arXiv · 2026-11-17 · 论文PDF

基于领域知识与图结构学习的材料数据质量置信度评估

The rapid development of materials machine learning relies on large-scale, heterogeneous datasets, yet model reliability is often compromised by imperfect data from diverse experiments, simulations, and database integration. Real-world materials databases may contain measurement errors, failed calculations, missing or duplicate records, and hidden violations of physicochemical constraints. Such problems are difficult to identify because corrupted samples may remain statistically plausible rather than appearing as explicit outliers. In this paper, we propose a confidence-based materials data quality assessment framework that integrates domain knowledge with graph-structured learning. Data quality is formulated as a continuous confidence estimation problem rather than a binary anomaly identification task, enabling sample-level assessment and historical-reference-based batch comparison. A nine-dimensional feature-quality prior and duplicate-aware sample initialization are calibrated using materials domain rules and statistical evidence.
