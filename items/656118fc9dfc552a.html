FADE: Factored Ability--Difficulty Filtering for Online RLVR Scheduling

arXiv (cs.LG) · 2026-11-02 · 论文PDF

Group-based reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards (RLVR) learns only from prompts whose sampled completions disagree: all-pass and all-fail groups produce zero relative advantage and burn generation budget without moving the policy. Adaptive schedulers try to steer rollouts toward informative prompts, yet they rely on per-prompt pass-rate trackers that entangle two quantities evolving at very different speeds---the policy's shifting competence across reasoning skills and each prompt's intrinsic difficulty. When a single exponential average or Beta counter tracks both at once, short memory scrambles the relative ordering of prompts whereas long memory reports stale pass rates. We introduce FADE (Factored Ability--Difficulty Estimation), a bilinear state-space filter that separates the two timescales. FADE represents each prompt's success logit as the inner product of a whitened $K$-dimensional skill loading with a fast-drifting policy ability vector, minus a slow-shrinking scalar item intercept.
