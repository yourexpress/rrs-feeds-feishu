Towards Global Counterfactual-Based Self-Explainable Graph Neural Networks

arXiv · 2026-11-22 · 论文PDF

Graph Counterfactual Explanations (GCEs) have emerged as an effective approach for interpreting Graph Neural Network (GNN) predictions by identifying minimal graph modifications needed to change predictions. However, existing methods suffer from two fundamental limitations: (1) They rely on a post-hoc explanation paradigm, where a separate explainer is trained after the GNN. This sequential optimization prevents the explainer from leveraging how the GNN learned its representations, often resulting in lower-quality explanations. (2) They are largely local, generating counterfactual explanations only for individual samples. Consequently, they fail to capture generalizable patterns across the data distribution, a crucial gap for applications like drug discovery, where global principles enable systematic decision-making across thousands of cases. To address these limitations, we propose GCSGNN (Global Counterfactual-based Self-explainable GNN), a unified framework that integrates counterfactual generation into the GNN architecture, encouraging explanations that faithfully reflect the model's decision-making process.
