On August 5, ByteDance officially launched the native audio-video full-duplex large model SeedRealtime. According to the introduction, as a key step towards multimodal interaction, SeedRealtime integrates audio, video, and text using a unified architecture, enabling real-time interaction on continuous multimodal information streams, thereby offering a brand-new experience of "watching, listening, and speaking" simultaneously. Currently, SeedRealtime has been fully launched on the Doubao App.
