Weight-space learning treats the weights of neural networks as data, processed by other models called metanetworks. A key challenge is that different weights can realize the same function, related by a group of symmetries the metanetwork should respect. While symmetry-aware metanetworks for simple architectures such as MLPs are well developed, a comprehensive treatment of transformers, and in particular transformer-based language models, is still missing. Importantly, their weight space has a richer symmetry group: beyond neuron permutations, attention introduces continuous general-linear symmetries of the attention matrices and permutations of entire heads. Existing methods for transformer weights either capture only part of these symmetries or are restricted to a single, fixed architecture, and none accounts for language-model-specific components such as the token embeddings. Building on graph metanetworks, we propose a graph-based approach that is both symmetry-aware and applicable across depths and widths.
