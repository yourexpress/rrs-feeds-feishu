We present BriLLM-MetaPred, the first engineering implementation of the MetaPred machine-learning framework established by a mathematical theory of AGI, instantiated for language modeling. The theory argues that MetaPred is both necessary and sufficient as a machine-learning primitive for general cognition. Taking this theoretical conclusion as a design premise, we report an implementation of the primitive for language modeling. A single continuously evolving dynamical state serves as both the context representation and the state from which predictions are read out. Continuous measurement of its own state reduces finite-depth inconsistency, maintains internal consistency, and provides an endogenous dynamical self-supervision signal in fixed-point form. The single dynamical state of MetaPred intrinsically couples training and inference. MetaPred therefore exhibits symmetry across almost all key elements of machine learning, an intuitive and observable property of the mathematical optimum over the space of all machine-learning paradigms.
