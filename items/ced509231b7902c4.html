InfMasking: A Unified Variational Framework for Synergistic Multimodal Learning

arXiv · 2026-11-06 · 论文PDF

Multimodal models are expected to exploit synergistic information, that is, information available only when multiple modalities are observed together. In practice, however, a fusion module can fit the training objective by relying on a dominant modality or on information that modalities share, and the synergy between modalities is often left unused. We propose InfMasking, a unified multimodal learning framework based on Infinite Masking. Before fusion, InfMasking randomly masks the features of each modality; each sampled mask produces a different partial combination of cross-modal evidence and a fused representation of the corresponding modality interactions. All masked fused representations are required to remain consistent with a reference target: the unmasked fused view when training is unsupervised, and the class proxy of the task label when labels are available. Maintaining this consistency across diverse partial combinations discourages reliance on a dominant modality or on shared information alone, and drives the fusion module to learn cross-modal interaction patterns that remain meaningful under partial observations.
