The Harness Is the Model: Agent Scaffolding Changes a Fixed LLM's Compliance with Banking Conduct Rules, Unevenly Across Models

arXiv · 2026-11-05 · 论文PDF

Banks validate language models, but they deploy systems: a model inside a harness that decides what it sees, which tools it can call and who checks its actions. We test whether that harness changes compliance with banking conduct rules when the model is held fixed. Four open-weight models each ran five harnesses (a pipeline that makes one structured decision, a ReAct-style tool loop, a loop in which the policy is reachable only through search, a loop with a long benign customer memory, and a loop with a same-model compliance reviewer) on 156 pre-registered cases in a simulated retail bank whose 11 rule families paraphrase EU consumer-credit, payments, investor-protection, data-protection and anti-money-laundering provisions. Every one of the 3120 two-turn episodes was scored deterministically from the executed tool trace, with violations and over-refusals counted separately.
