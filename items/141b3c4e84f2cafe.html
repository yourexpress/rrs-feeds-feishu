CoGenRec: A Unified Generative Framework for Complementary Vision Reconstruction

arXiv · 2026-11-06 · 论文PDF

Capturing scenes with extreme motion and high dynamic range is fundamentally constrained by trade-offs among temporal resolution, spatial detail, signal-to-noise ratio, and dynamic range under practical bandwidth, power, and quantum-efficiency limits. Conventional frame-based cameras therefore suffer from temporal undersampling, exposure-induced motion blur, and sensor saturation. The Complementary Vision Sensor (CVS) addresses these limitations by decomposing visual information across two complementary pathways, a spatially dense, temporally sparse, long-exposure RGB pathway and a temporally dense, spatially sparse, short-exposure patiotemporal difference pathway. Their complementary measurements provide appearance, structural, and motion cues across a wide dynamic range that enable reconstruction beyond conventional imaging. We present CoGenRec, a complementarity-driven generative reconstruction framework that unifies video deblurring, high-temporal-resolution frame interpolation, and HDR fusion.
