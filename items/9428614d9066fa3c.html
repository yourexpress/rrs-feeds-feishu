Bleaching of cultured Pyropia (laver) causes losses in Korean seaweed farming. We present a system that forecasts bleaching seven days ahead for farm clusters. OceanTensorCube aligns 32 channels from public marine, meteorological, ocean-model and satellite sources on a 30 m grid, and ST-MMT, a 9.2-million-parameter spatiotemporal transformer with hub-style multi-head cross-attention between variable groups, maps a 14-day input window to a seven-day-ahead bleaching probability. On the validation set, a six-seed ensemble detects every reported positive at a false-alarm rate of at most 3.5%, an upper bound because the report-based labels are positive-unlabeled. A same-date control on separately trained models indicates that the architecture discriminates between clusters when the calendar is held fixed. We also report the system's weaknesses: skill falls steeply as the quarterly nutrient survey ages (ROC-AUC 0.94 at 30 days, 0.53 at 129 days), and a single LSTM on cluster-average series outperforms a single ST-MMT, with only the ensemble leading at the operating point.
