A Graph-informed Kriging model for sample-efficient Bayesian optimization of wind farm layouts

Wind farm layout optimization becomes increasingly difficult as turbine count grows, especially when high-fidelity aerodynamic models are used. Conventional Kriging-based Bayesian optimization often suffers from low sample efficiency in high-dimensional layout spaces because sparse training data provide limited domain coverage. We develop a Graph-informed Kriging framework that embeds reusable aerodynamic interaction information into the surrogate mean function. The wind farm is represented as a directed aerodynamic graph, where pairwise turbine interactions defined in a low-dimensional relative-coordinate space are assembled into a farm-scale mean. This structured mean captures the dominant variation in annual energy production (AEP), allowing the Gaussian process to model only the residual structure and uncertainty. Numerical experiments show that Graph-informed Kriging consistently improves AEP prediction accuracy over ordinary and generic-trend Kriging under sparse-data conditions, with increasing benefits as layout dimension and wake-interaction strength grow.
