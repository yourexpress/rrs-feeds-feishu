SONATI: Spatio-temporal ocean neighbourhood asymmetric transformer for imputation of sparse, tabular data with uncertainty

arXiv · 2026-11-20 · 论文PDF

Missing value imputation is often a prerequisite for downstream analyses. In oceanography, observational data are frequently structured in a tabular manner, highly sparse and exhibit spatio-temporal structures that classical imputers such as k-nearest neighbours or MissForest are not suited to exploit. We present the Spatio-temporal Ocean Neighbourhood Asymmetric Transformer for Imputation (SONATI), a masked, factorised time-space transformer operating on pre-computed, local time and space neighbourhoods, which reduces computational complexity compared to global attention. SONATI is designed to work with tabular data and is thus in principle able to work on both gridded and unstructured data, though evaluation here was performed on gridded North Atlantic data only. It provides aleatoric and epistemic uncertainties through a negative log-likelihood loss and multiple forward passes with Monte Carlo dropout. Systematic ablations on attention type and masking strategies guided the final architecture design.
