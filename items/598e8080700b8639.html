Random Cut Forests, Games and Diffusion over Approximately Nested Anisotropic Local Geometry

arXiv cs.LG · 2026-11-14 · 论文PDF

随机割森林、博弈与近似嵌套各向异性局部几何上的扩散

Just as the data in a dynamic stream changes, questions asked of the stream also change in intent: what to hold constant, which counterfactuals to consider, and so forth. Energy-based learning has been celebrated for its uniform approach to losses and estimation, but interpretability remains challenging. Tree-based methods, on the other hand, offer high interpretability but often rely on bespoke, disjointed estimators. Neither paradigm readily supports changing questions on streaming data. In this paper we take a step towards combining energy, interpretability, and streaming. Random cut forests retain an evolving geometry of the data. We study this geometry as the basis for diffusion dynamics, prediction, explanation, and games. We show that several existing scores are escape probabilities of random walks on the trees. The induced affinity is realized exactly by a sparse electrical network on each tree, giving a single energy calculus, a Hamiltonian, maintained along one path per update.
