Signed digit redundant number systems such as Maximally Redundant Signed Digit (MRSD) enable carry-free addition but introduce significant overhead in multiplication due to the presence of both positive and negative bits. While Unsigned Digit Redundant (UDR) systems have shown promise in radix-2, their extension to higher radices remains unexplored. This work presents the first generalized Maximally Redundant Unsigned Digit (MRUD) number systems for high-radix arithmetic, extending UDR representations from radix-2 to radix-$2^h$ while preserving carry-free addition. The proposed framework includes digit encoding, digit extension, arithmetic operations (addition, subtraction, and multiplication), sign inversion, forward/reverse conversion algorithms, overflow/underflow detection, and hardware architectures for both signed and unsigned MRUD representations. Unlike MRSD, which is inherently limited to signed arithmetic, MRUD supports both signed and unsigned representations, similar to conventional binary systems.
