Synapse: An HBM-Coordinated HBF Near-Memory Computing Architecture for Pipelined Agentic AI Execution

arXiv · 2026-11-23 · 论文PDF / 每日精选

Synapse：HBM 协调 HBF 近存计算架构，流水化 agentic AI 执行（48.92-66.60% 端到端延迟降低）

During multi-agent video reasoning, model weights and per-agent key-value caches can exceed high-bandwidth memory (HBM) capacity. High-bandwidth flash (HBF) adds capacity, but agents still wait for GPU execution. Synapse, an HBM-coordinated near-memory computing architecture, overlaps HBF attention and expert execution with GPU computation across agents. It also reduces dispatch delays and unnecessary refreshes. Across three models, Synapse achieves 48.92-66.60% lower end-to-end latency, up to $2.98\times$ throughput, and up to 64.48% lower modeled energy per output token than GPU execution with passive HBF. Wear simulations project 46% longer HBF lifetime than block-wide refresh at equal workload rates.
