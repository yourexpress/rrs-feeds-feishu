This blog is part of Accelerating Frontier Transformation, a four-part series exploring how organizations are turning AI into business value. Based on conversations with industry leaders at Microsoft Frontier Tours, the series looks at AI’s impact across employee experience, customer engagement, business processes, and innovation. This third post focuses on reshaping business processes.

Rethinking how work gets done

AI can help us prepare a document, analyze information, or summarize a meeting. But its potential extends to reshaping entire business processes. To realize more value from AI, organizations are rethinking how work is organized across teams and functions: how knowledge informs decisions, how people and agents collaborate, and where human expertise and accountability are needed. This creates an opportunity to improve business outcomes through new ways of operating, rather than simply completing the same tasks faster.

The leaders we spoke with at several Microsoft Frontier Tours around the world described how this shift is taking shape. Their examples ranged from bringing organizational knowledge into customer proposals to using AI-supported forecasts in business decisions. They also shared ambitions to move beyond personal productivity and change how their organizations deliver value.

Reshaping business processes is a key part of Frontier Transformation, built on intelligence and trust. Intelligence can ground AI in the knowledge and context that work requires. Trust means building security, governance, and human oversight into how AI is designed and used.
