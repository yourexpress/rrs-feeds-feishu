Federated zeroth-order optimization (FedZOO) reduces the memory and communication costs of collaborative training for edge intelligence by avoiding backpropagation while communicating only random seeds and scalar coefficients. However, existing methods lack efficient mechanisms for exact unlearning to fully remove the influence of training data when users request deletion. A key insight is that total variation stability of the learning algorithm creates overlap between model distributions before and after data deletion, allowing exact unlearning to reuse computation through coupling.
