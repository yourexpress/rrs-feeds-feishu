Gated Concept Adapters: Layer-Wise Concept Bottlenecks for Auditable Text Understanding

arXiv · 2026-11-24 · 论文PDF

Human-facing text understanding systems are used to infer affect, moderate harmful content, and support socially sensitive decisions. However, Transformer predictions are difficult to audit because they are produced from high-dimensional hidden states that do not provide a compact, inspectable interface for human analysis or intervention. We introduce Gated Concept Adapters (GCA), a layer-wise concept bottleneck framework that attaches gated, low-dimensional concept-producing readout adapters to selected outputs of pretrained Transformers. Token representations are mapped into concept activations, filtered by learned gates, pooled into layer-level summaries, and exposed to the prediction head as a concept-mediated interface. The dimensions can be inspected through usage statistics, signature n-grams, post-hoc concept names, and targeted concept interventions. We evaluate GCA on fine-grained emotion classification, toxicity detection, topic classification, and a controlled verb-cloze stress test.
