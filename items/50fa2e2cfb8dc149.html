Graph-XGB: A Hybrid GNN Framework for Outdoor-to-Indoor 5G Signal Reconstruction via Aerial-Only UAV RF Sensing

arXiv · 2026-11-19 · 论文PDF

Graph-XGB：无人机射频感知的室外到室内 5G 信号重建

Delivering robust In-Building Coverage (IBC) is critically impeded by severe Outdoor-to-Indoor (O2I) attenuation, a constraint that intensifies as deployments move from 5G mid-band towards mmWave and prospective 6G sub-THz carriers, where losses of 60-80 dB have been reported across modern building envelopes. While Unmanned Aerial Vehicles (UAVs) offer scalable exterior RF sensing platforms, a fundamental inversion problem persists: interior 3D signal states cannot be directly sampled from constrained exterior flight paths. This paper proposes Graph-XGB, a hybrid inference framework that decouples spatial topology reasoning from non-linear signal regression to resolve this challenge. A modified SpiderGNN encoder, operating on a topology-aware graph constructed with a physically grounded Floor-Aware 3D Distance metric and Bridge Edge Guarantees, propagates UAV boundary measurements into 128-dimensional indoor embeddings. Principal Component Analysis (PCA) compresses these into a 10-dimensional manifold retaining 96.6% of embedding variance, fused with raw spatial features and decoded by independent, physics-tailored XGBoost regressors.
