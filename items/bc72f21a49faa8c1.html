Lithium-ion batteries are widely used in industrial applications, but their performance gradually degrades over time, making accurate lifetime prediction important for safety and reliability. However, reliable lifetime assessment generally requires extensive degradation testing, which is both costly and time-consuming. To reduce this burden, recent data-driven approaches have sought to predict the end-of-life cycle of batteries from only the initial few cycles. Nevertheless, identifying reliable degradation patterns from such limited early-cycle data remains challenging because early aging dynamics are often subtle and nonlinear. To address this issue, we adopt the continuous wavelet transform to convert cycle-wise voltage, current, and capacity signals into time-frequency representations, enabling multi-resolution characterization of degradation behavior. Building on this representation, we apply scale compression to reduce redundancy while preserving structural information in the scalograms.
