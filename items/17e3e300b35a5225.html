Two-Robot Evacuation from a Unit Disk in the Segment Visibility Model

arXiv cs.DC · 2026-11-15 · 论文PDF

线段可见性模型下双机器人从单位圆盘撤离的算法研究

Under segment visibility, two robots discover a target as soon as it belongs to the segment joining their positions, even if neither robot has physically reached it. We study two unit-speed robots starting at the center of a unit disk that must both reach an exit (target) placed at an unknown point of its boundary. In our model robots are permitted to leave the disk while searching. We give a three-phase tangent-and-arc algorithm whose exact worst-case evacuation time is 2\sqrt{3}+π/3 \approx 4.511299$. We also prove a matching lower bound for the class of descending horizontal full-slice sweeps, allowing asymmetric robot trajectories. The proof uses an exterior path-length inequality and a feasible minimum-half-width symmetrization. Determining the optimal time over all segment-visibility algorithms remains open.
