LADS: Lease-Aware Delivery Scheduling of Tool Results for Language Model Agents

arXiv (cs.DC) · 2026-10-31 · 论文PDF

Web agents complete multi-step online tasks through repeated browser actions and model calls. Their returned observations lie on the critical path to subsequent decisions. When concurrent browser actions finish in bursts, observations can accumulate on a constrained return path to a shared inference service, while waiting histories compete for reusable cache state. Conventional delivery rules prioritize short transfers or immediate cache reuse but overlook how sending a result earlier can help a request resume sooner and free cache-protection capacity for other waiting requests. We present LADS (Lease-Aware Delivery Scheduling), a tool-result scheduler that couples cache-aware delivery with bounded prefix leases. Its key insight is that resumption lets extra protection references retire, making lease quota available to protect another waiting history. LADS prioritizes timely handoffs using current lease ownership and an estimated resumption delay, with bounded departures from Cache-first order.
