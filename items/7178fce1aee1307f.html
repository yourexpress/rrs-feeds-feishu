LLM-Assisted Co-Optimization of RTL and Synthesis Algorithms via Bottleneck Analysis

arXiv · 2026-11-22 · 论文PDF

Post-synthesis power, performance, and area (PPA) depend jointly on the register-transfer-level (RTL) code and the synthesis algorithms. However, the benefits of RTL optimization may be limited by bottlenecks in synthesis algorithms, and vice versa, leaving opportunities for better PPA unexplored by methods that optimize either level alone. In this work, we present a large language model (LLM)-assisted framework for regional co-optimization of RTL and synthesis algorithms via bottleneck analysis. A key insight behind our approach is that RTL structures and the Boolean networks used in synthesis expose complementary opportunities for addressing bottlenecks that lead to suboptimal PPA in different regions of the circuit. Our bottleneck analysis uses a Gaussian process (GP) to assess whether optimizing RTL or synthesis algorithms is more promising for each region. The GP learns from regional graph representations and PPA feedback. Using the GP's predictions and predictive uncertainty, Bayesian optimization (BO) provides guidance that an LLM Router combines with circuit analysis to choose how to address each region's bottlenecks.
