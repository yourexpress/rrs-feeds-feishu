Generative AI for urban planning: synthesizing land-use maps with satellite imagery via DualControlNet

arXiv · 2026-11-05 · 论文PDF

Various land-use models characterize land-use patterns and facilitate scenario planning. However, existing models are largely predictive, forecasting transitions based on numeric data without synthesizing new land-use maps from simple text prompts. Meanwhile, generative AI in urban systems remains unimodal, weakly controllable, and evaluated mostly by generic image quality rather than domain-specific metrics. To address these gaps, we introduce DualControlNet, a generative land-use model that jointly synthesizes high-resolution land-use maps and satellite imagery, addressing multimodality, controllability, and verification challenges. Trained on over 40,000 paired tiles from Orlando and Chicago, DualControlNet is evaluated using land-use, spatial consistency, and image quality metrics. Relative to unimodal baselines, DualControlNet improves land-use diversity consistency by 62.8% and building coverage ratio consistency by 16.3%, more than doubles cross-modal spatial consistency, and triples visual quality.
