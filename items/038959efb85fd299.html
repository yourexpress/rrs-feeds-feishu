INSIGHT-HPC: An Interpretable System Intelligence for Global HPC Telemetry

arXiv (cs.DC) · 2026-11-02 · 论文PDF

面向全球 HPC 遥测的可解释系统智能：基于 BMC/调度器/电源遥测检测异常并解释告警，降低能耗与节点故障

Modern high-performance computing (HPC) clusters integrate multi-generation CPUs and GPUs, which generate rich, high-dimensional telemetry streams from out-of-band baseboard management controllers (BMC), job schedulers, and facility power distribution units. Detecting anomalies within this telemetry is critical to prevent energy waste and costly node failures. Existing approaches often rely primarily on statistical deviations and require stronger labels than actual production environments can provide. In addition, these detectors have a limited operational context for interpreting alerts. We present INSIGHT-HPC, a multi-plane anomaly-detection architecture that combines statistical, workload-aware, and physics-guided evidence into operator-interpretable episodes. INSIGHT-HPC integrates heterogeneous, large-scale telemetry streams and transforms noisy minute-resolution observations into de-duplicated fault episodes with operator-facing explanations. This design allows the system to distinguish workload-explained behavior from genuine infrastructure anomalies while producing interpretable alert summaries for operators.
