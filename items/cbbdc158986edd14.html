Mayfly: Lifecycle-Aware High-Bandwidth Flash for Recursive Self-Improvement

arXiv · 2026-11-24 · 论文PDF / 每日精选

Mayfly：生命周期感知高带宽闪存，面向递归自我改进

Recursive self-improvement (RSI) lets an LLM improve its agent system by proposing and evaluating candidate variants across tasks. A generation's sessions grow their KV caches together and release them as they finish, creating a burst followed by a decline. At high concurrency, even the KV of sessions ready to run exceeds the GPU's HBM. Offloading that KV to host DRAM or SSDs adds capacity but leaves ready sessions waiting for HBM space before execution. High-Bandwidth Flash (HBF) can serve the overflow in place, but excessive reads lengthen decode steps and repeated writes consume flash endurance. Our key insight is that where ready KV is read should follow the burst and its decline: from HBF in place while the burst overflows HBM, as much as each step's bandwidth allows, and from HBM again as the decline frees it. A switch between ready and parked decides only whether a block is read, so its flash copy stays valid, and a session's end, which the orchestrator announces, decides when its private KV dies.
