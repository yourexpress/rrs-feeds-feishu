Introducing ChatGPT for Sales

Help every seller shorten the sales cycle, from finding the right accounts to following through on customer commitments, with a new Sales experience and over 30 new and expanded partner plugins.

We’re introducing ChatGPT for Sales to help every sales team understand customers better, spend less time on busywork, and close more deals. It includes our new Sales plugin with dedicated skills for sellers, and more than 30 new and enhanced partner plugins in ChatGPT, so you can use information from the tools your team already relies on to prepare for calls, tailor proposals, and follow up faster.

The new Sales plugin from OpenAI gives you ready-to-use workflows for everyday sales work, from deciding which accounts to pursue to preparing for a call and drafting the follow-up. Sales teams work best when everyone has a shared understanding of the customer. Which accounts are worth pursuing? What does a buyer need to move forward? Which deals need attention, and why? The answers often sit across teammates, prospecting tools, customer calls, and the CRM. Bringing that information together gives teams more time to make informed decisions and follow through on customer commitments. That starts with the work you do every day.

ChatGPT for Sales brings your tools, customer context, and sales workflows into one place. It also gives your team access to partner plugins, so ChatGPT can use customer context from your existing tools to help move opportunities forward. Choose the partner plugins that fit your sales process.

Create content and equip sellers. Learn your sales process and the latest product updates with Confluence, Guru, and Seismic. Rehearse pitches and practice handling objections with ChatGPT Voice. Build tailored presentations with ChatGPT Slides, Gamma, Figma, or Canva, or create an interactive demo or ROI calculator with ChatGPT Sites.
