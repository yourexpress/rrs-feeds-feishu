Can AI Mature a Model's Geometry? Training-Free Weight Baking in LLMs

arXiv cs.LG · 2026-11-16 · 论文PDF

训练免权重烘焙成熟LLM几何

Can an AI make a trained language model more mature, in the sense of a better organized latent space, by looking only at the geometry of that space? An analyst model reads a fixed-size digest of topological descriptors of the layers of a frozen target and proposes a transformation, which closed-form ridge regression writes into the feed-forward weights without gradient training. Using lightweight models, 126 runs show that category purity rose on unseen words and sentences, benchmark accuracy stayed within sampling error, and the analyst matched a category-aware heuristic with gentler edits. In a five-model round robin approach, 16 pairs raised purity and four left the target unchanged. The results provide a baseline for future work on LLM-guided, training-free model editing without using raw weights.
