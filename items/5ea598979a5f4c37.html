I am a signpost. Pointing is my trade,

and I point well: my arrow's firm and straight

at where the fair once stood, the bright parade,

the organ music drifting past the gate.

The fair packed up and went. The word came slow

to posts like me, who seldom get around,

so here I stand, the very last to know,

and aim you at a patch of empty ground.

You took my word. That was a generous thing.

The least I owe is weather: sky, a lark,

a hedge in leaf, the whole green end of spring,

a field that's good for standing in till dark.

I'm out of news, but never out of cheer,

and glad of anyone who wandered here.
