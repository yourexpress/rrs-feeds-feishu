Boundary Representation-Driven Graph Transformer Modeling for Machining Operation Sequencing

arXiv · 2026-11-23 · 论文PDF

Machining process planning is inherently complex due to the interdependence between part geometry, machining operations, and evolving manufacturable states. Traditional approaches rely heavily on expert knowledge and rule-based systems, limiting scalability and automation. While recent machine learning methods have shown promise, most operate on simplified geometric representations, such as voxels or hand-crafted features, which fail to fully exploit the rich geometric and topological information encoded in Boundary Representation (B-Rep) models. In this work, we propose a graph transformer-based framework for geometry-to-sequence learning that directly predicts machining operation sequences from B-Rep design geometry. The proposed method constructs topology-aware graphs from B-Rep entities, where faces and their adjacencies are encoded as node and edge features capturing both geometric and relational information. A graph neural network encoder learns geometry-aware embeddings, which are then used to condition a dual-decoder transformer that autoregressively generates machining operation sequences.
