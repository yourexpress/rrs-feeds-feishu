C3: Speech-Guided KV Retrieval for Long-Context SpeechLLM Inference

arXiv (cs.LG) · 2026-10-30 · 论文PDF

Speech language models encode audio at a much higher rate than text, causing the KV cache to dominate the memory, bandwidth, and decoding cost of long-form generation. KV retrieval can reduce per-step access by attending to only a query-dependent subset of the cache, but existing methods are designed primarily for text and do not account for the structure of audio attention. We first show that retrieval errors may compound autoregressively into generation drift. We then analyze full audio attention during long-form generation and identify three properties relevant to retrieval: unequal attention concentration across query heads, non-contiguous audio support, and temporal continuity across decoding steps. Guided by these observations, we propose $C^3$(Cluster-Compete-Compete), which organizes audio KVs in a hierarchical key-space index, lets query heads sharing a KV head compete for a common retrieval budget, and adaptively reallocates retrieval resolution through paired split-merge updates.
