Making Protocol Effects Explicit: LLM-to-Packet Simulation for Superpod Interconnect Exploration

arXiv · 2026-11-23 · 论文PDF / 每日精选

LLM 到包仿真：超级 pod 互连探索（协议栈-链路-拓扑联合仿真）

Large language models (LLMs) have become the foundation of modern AI services. The rapid growth of LLM sizes and context lengths has exceeded the memory capacity of a single GPU or NPU, requiring model partitioning across devices. However, partitioning models across devices introduces costly communication overhead, driving the need for superpods with large-scale, high-bandwidth, low-latency scale-up fabrics. Exploring superpod designs requires accurate interconnect simulation, as communication latency is jointly determined by link technologies, topologies, and scale-up protocol-stack choices. Latency-bandwidth models estimate communication latency using a startup term and a transfer term derived from message size and effective bandwidth. But they fail to capture contention and protocol effects. Packet-level simulators like ns-3 model routing, queueing, and packet timing, but require protocol-specific LLM-to-packet implementations. This paper presents ProtoBridge, an ns-3-based LLM-to-packet simulator for superpod interconnect exploration.
