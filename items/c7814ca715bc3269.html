PBit1: One Programmable P-Bit per Parameter - A Finite-Control Representation of Pretrained Networks

arXiv cs.LG · 2026-11-14 · 论文PDF

PBit1：每参数一个可编程p-bit的预训练网络有限控制表示

We introduce PBit1, a finite-control representation of pretrained neural networks that assigns one programmable p-bit to each learned parameter. A 12-bit command sets its probability, a shared affine range maps that probability to the weight domain, and temporal observations reconstruct the weight for inference. Operator-aware range grouping and sensitivity-based sampling allocation adapt the representation to the network. Across five independent weight draws at 32,768 observations per scalar, selected configurations preserve task quality in GPT-2, DistilBERT, and LFM3-1.7B with every learned scalar converted and no fine-tuning. GPT-2 and LFM3 perplexity increase by approximately 0.66% and 0.26%, respectively, while DistilBERT accuracy changes from 87.51% to 87.49%. A sensitivity-based policy reduces GPT-2 reconstruction observations by 41.7% while retaining the quality criterion. Complete-model evaluations reuse reconstructed weights in BF16 inference, and THRML operator experiments examine native sampling and spatial-temporal tradeoffs. Together, these results establish PBit1 as a concrete construction for studying finite-control weight representation and temporal reconstruction …
