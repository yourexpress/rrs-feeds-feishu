Agent Verifiers in Counterfactual Analysis: A Pilot Study Benchmarking Agentic Heterogeneous Treatment Effects

arXiv · 2026-11-17 · 论文PDF

Agent 验证器在反事实分析中的试点基准（agentic HTE）

Agents can expand the covariate space of randomized experiments by retrieving fast-moving local context missed by slow-changing survey baseline covariates, helping characterize who benefits most from an intervention. Verifying such covariates is difficult: individual treatment effects are unobserved, retrieval leakage can pull post-treatment information into nominally pretreatment summaries, and encoder leakage can inject post-treatment pretraining knowledge into representations even after temporal filtering of inputs. Guided by a two-channel information bound separating summary provenance from encoder weights, we propose a three-layer benchmark protocol: (i) retrieval admissibility via pre-treatment date filtering; (ii) heterogeneity validation scored rank-weighted average treatment effects (RATEs); and (iii) encoder admissibility checked with chronologically vintaged encoders, future-fact probes, and weight perturbations.
