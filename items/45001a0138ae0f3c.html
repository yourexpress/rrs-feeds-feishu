Efficient Closure Learning in Dynamical Systems: A Comparison of Data-Assimilated and Embedded Approaches

arXiv (cs.LG) · 2026-11-02 · 论文PDF

Increased computing power has transformed computational science and improved predictive capabilities for complex systems, but many phenomena still exceed current simulation capabilities or require rapid evaluations for fast design iterations. Closure models address this challenge by approximating unresolved effects in reduced or incomplete dynamical systems. Data-driven closures offer a flexible mechanism for improving model accuracy and applicability, but approaches that embed the closure within a computational model for construction and training phases can be computationally expensive and cause numerical instabilities in the computational model during training. To avoid the challenges posed by embedded approaches, staged approaches to closure-model learning have been developed. The first stage uses data assimilation to infer state and closure trajectories from observations. The second stage trains a machine learning closure model offline using the assimilated data, thereby avoiding repeated simulator calls during training.
