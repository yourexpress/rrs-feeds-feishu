Two-Phase Rapid Simulation-Based Inference with Differentiable Simulators

arXiv cs.LG · 2026-11-09 · 论文PDF

We introduce Rapid Simulation Based Inference (RSBI), a diffusion-based variational approach to likelihood-free Bayesian inference that achieves high sampling efficiency under large prior-to-posterior volumes. RSBI builds on advances in Schrödinger Bridge (SB) diffusion sampling to handle multi-modal posteriors, with an initial likelihood supplied by a distance measure via a differentiable simulator. The key innovation of this initial surrogate likelihood is that it gives a highly efficient proposal distribution, replacing sequential rounds of other methods with an efficient one-shot proposal. Subsequent refinement then transports the proposal to the true posterior as specified by the simulator and the original prior. The SB sampler is warm-started from the proposal, and its energy is corrected by a neural likelihood ratio that is learned only where the proposal places mass. As a variational method, RSBI does not suffer from prior leakage commonly observed in standard sequential posterior estimation methods.
