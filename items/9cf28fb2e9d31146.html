Cap-and-Track: Optimizing LLM Inference Request Dispatch Across Shifting Capacity

arXiv · 2026-11-18 · 论文PDF

Cap-and-Track：跨漂移容量优化 LLM 推理请求分发

A fixed dispatch rate for LLM inference becomes stale as serving fleets resize and request sizes change. Generative output length is unknown at admission, and shared provider pools can lose capacity. We present Cap-and-Track, a design for asynchronous inference across provider capacity and internal GPUs. Meta's traffic shaper implements Cap-and-Track for post and live-video understanding. Refusals signal the backend's current admission ceiling. Durable priority queues hold excess work outside execution, including refused requests for retry. Cap is a fail-fast admission check at the saturated resource that refuses work before a long wait. Using only those refusals, the Track dispatcher probes upward and backs off. It follows the ceiling without output-length prediction or a fleet-level rate to retune. In a concurrent same-traffic production A/B, the shaper ran per-replica Cap plus Track. It served a classifier on 43% fewer H100s than its queued, fixed-rate precursor, though lower-priority work waited longer at backfill peaks.
