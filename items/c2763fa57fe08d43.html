Kolmogorov-Arnold Networks vs. Multi-Layer Perceptrons: A Rigorous Statistical Benchmark on Clinical Tabular Data

arXiv (cs.LG) · 2026-11-02 · 论文PDF

Kolmogorov-Arnold Networks (KANs) have recently been proposed as a promising alternative to Multi-Layer Perceptrons (MLPs). However, empirical evaluations on structured medical datasets remain scarce, and commonly reported metrics like AUROC can yield overly optimistic performance estimates on imbalanced clinical cohorts. In this study, we present a standardized benchmark comparing eleven architectures - nine KAN variants (FastKAN, WavKAN, ReLUKAN, ChebyKAN, TaylorKAN, GramKAN, JacobiKAN, LegendreKAN, and TabKAN), a tuned StandardMLP, and a Random Forest baseline - across seven heterogeneous medical tabular datasets spanning oncology, cardiology, nephrology, neurology, endocrinology, and obstetrics. Our experimental protocol employs a 3x5 Repeated Stratified K-Fold cross-validation scheme with nested Bayesian hyperparameter optimization and evaluates discrimination via Area Under the Precision-Recall Curve (AUPRC), calibration through the Brier Score, and computational efficiency. Formal equivalence testing is conducted using the Bayesian Correlated t-Test with a Region of Practical Equivalence (ROPE) at +/- 0.01.
