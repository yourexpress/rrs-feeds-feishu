Stellarators offer a promising magnetic confinement approach for achieving controlled nuclear fusion. However, stellarator design remains challenging, as evaluating configuration performance requires computationally expensive, high-fidelity simulations. In particular, key performance metrics derived from guiding center simulations are costly to include directly in the design optimization loop, and are almost exclusively used as diagnostics. Machine learning has the potential to advance fusion research by enabling rapid generation and evaluation of new stellarator configurations. Effectively applying ML for fusion demands large-scale datasets that capture both design diversity and high-fidelity simulation outputs. In this work, we introduce the Benchmark of Stellarator Simulations (BoSS), a dataset of stellarator designs paired with guiding center simulation results and trajectories. The BoSS is the first large-scale dataset of guiding center simulations, containing simulation data for 180,000 distinct magnetohydrodynamic equilibria spanning a wide range of magnetic geometries and confinement properties.
