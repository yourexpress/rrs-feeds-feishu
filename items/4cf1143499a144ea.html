Network Contention State Aware Dynamic Optimizer for Accelerator Collective Communication Libraries

arXiv cs.DC · 2026-11-07 · 论文PDF

Collective Communication Libraries (CCL) are critical for large-scale distributed AI/ML training, yet their configurations are determined by static heuristics that do not account for real-time network conditions. Our empirical study shows that these heuristics perform near-optimally in contention-free environments, but degrade substantially under background traffic. To address this, we present a cooperative multi-agent run-time optimizer for accelerator collective communication. Our approach employs a two-agent architecture: (i) detector- a Long Short Term Memory(LSTM) recurring neural network fed classifier head with a sequential probability ratio test that distinguishes sustained contention from transient spikes; and (ii) selector- a configuration selector built on a Dueling Double DQN (D3QN) with Conservative Q-Learning that adapts configuration parameters in response. We implemented this dynamic tuner over NCCL, a widely used CCL developed by NVIDIA. When evaluated on Chameleon Cloud's isolated bare-metal cluster, this dynamic tuning approach reduces AllReduce latency by 36% over NCCL and 32% over a fully converged AutoCCL.
