Pudding：可重定向 PUD（Processing-Using-DRAM）编译器框架

Processing-Using-DRAM (PUD) architectures exploit the physics of DRAM arrays to perform Boolean operations directly in memory, eliminating the data movement bottleneck in memory-intensive workloads. Several PUD architectures have been recently proposed which differ in their primitive operation sets, row allocation models, and execution metrics. These architectural differences are today hard-coded in emerging PUD-aware compilers, making crossarchitecture reuse impossible. We present a retargetable, end-to-end compilation framework for PUD architectures. The central idea is to make the architecture description a first-class object in the compilation pipeline: retargeting to a new PUD architecture only requires supplying its description. Built upon the MLIR framework, our compiler operates at two coupled levels. At the tensor level, it lowers high-level programs through bit-slicing, mixed-precision assignment, and integer-linear-programming (ILP) based placement to reduce the number of PUD commands and inter-bank data movement.
