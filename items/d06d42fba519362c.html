Resource Locks and Fast Unlock for Shared Balances in Certificate-Based Payment Systems

arXiv (cs.DC) · 2026-10-30 · 论文PDF

Certificate-based payment systems such as FastPay settle a payment with one round of signatures from a quorum of validators and avoid global ordering. The client collects the signatures, and validators never talk to each other. We observe that this client-driven design is complete only as long as no lock is ever undone. Each account has a single writer, a conflict can only come from that writer's own misbehavior, and the account stays locked as the penalty. Stablecoin payments routinely need balances that several accounts debit: treasuries, merchant pools, allowances and subscriptions. There, honest accounts race, locks must be undone, and a quorum certificate held privately by a client can be silently overridden. We prove that no unlock rule is safe in this setting unless certificate formation is visible to validators. We present a hybrid protocol. Personal accounts keep the client-driven FastPay path unchanged. A shared resource is a balance with its own version number and a set of authorized accounts.
