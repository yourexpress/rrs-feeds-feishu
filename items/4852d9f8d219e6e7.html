Attention is a core building block of frontier models and often dominates compute-bound prefill at long context. FP8 matrix instructions offer twice the peak throughput of BF16, but they accelerate only matrix multiplication, and the softmax between the two matrix multiplications of attention runs on vector units that FP8 does not accelerate. On the AMD Instinct MI355X, the vendor's AITER kernels reach 39% of peak FP8 matrix throughput at 32K tokens, compared with 49% in BF16. Attention is also more numerically sensitive to quantization than feed-forward layers, and unlike NVIDIA's Hopper and Blackwell GPUs, the MI355X does not track the completion of matrix instructions in hardware, so overlapping the softmax with the matrix multiplications depends on instruction order.
