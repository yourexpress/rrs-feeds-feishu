An all-transistor physical-learning transformer can be trained to perform in-context learning

arXiv · 2026-10-08 · 论文PDF

全晶体管物理学习transformer经训练可执行in-context learning（亚阈值MOSFET注意力）

Most systems trained by equilibrium propagation or coupled learning are in the same class as multi-layer perceptrons: while they may be universal function approximators, they struggle to learn associative tasks which are easy for transformers. Equilibrium propagation relies on a bidirectional flow of information through the physical system between input and output. The reverse flow is broken by more complex nonlinear systems, such as those performing multiplication which is needed to express dot-product attention. Here we explicitly restore this bidirectional flow by introducing extra circuit elements for this purpose into a double or dyadic circuit. We then use these ideas to construct an attention mechanism exploiting the exponential current law of sub-threshold MOSFET devices. We show that in simulations this circuit can be trained to perform a simple in-context learning task, despite using realistic reverse components which transmit only an approximate gradient. For this training to succeed we need cross-entropy loss (not mean squared error).
