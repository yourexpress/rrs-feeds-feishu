- The original paper now has 3.9k citations since April 2024 and the open-source repo (opens in new tab) has over 36k GitHub stars. It has spawned a wide range of follow-on research and software.

GraphRAG Zero is designed to solve one of the fundamental challenges preventing AI agents from reaching their full potential — giving them the context they need to reason, make decisions, and act intelligently.

AI agents need more than text excerpts that merely match the words in a prompt. To answer complex queries and understand connections across different kinds of information, they need retrieval that captures the structure within the data. This becomes increasingly important as AI generates more information than people can review. GraphRAG provides that structure, while its latest version, GraphRAG Zero, makes retrieval fast and affordable enough to build and update continuously.
