Encoding, Decodability, and Use: A Five-Layer Framework for Benchmark Measurement Audits with Evidence from UAV Intent Recognition

arXiv cs.LG · 2026-11-17 · 论文PDF

基准测量审计的五层框架（UAV 意图识别）

Benchmark accuracy is routinely read as evidence that a model understands the construct the benchmark measures. We propose and instantiate on AeroIntent a five-layer framework that decomposes that reading into observation, representation, decoding, use, and behaviour. The framework separates encoding, decodability, and use as distinct levels. In the instantiation, the observation layer is unreliable on 7 of the 20 Drone programs and floors the direct visual correlate of radial velocity at a 0.26\% scale change. The representation layer exposes two task-designed RGB features with nominal signals. RAFT flow radial bias reaches $r=+0.257$ ($p=0.030$), and bounding-box optical-flow divergence reaches $r=+0.203$ ($p=0.029$). No multi-feature readout gives a fold-mean test correlation above the cluster minimum detectable effect (MDE), and the largest positive global out-of-sample correlation is $r=+0.080$. The decoding layer sets the MDE at $|r|=0.599$ at the multi-feature Bonferroni threshold ($α=0.00714$, power 0.80), and the observed $r=+0.080$ has power 0.00.
