DiffusionART: Efficient, Stable and Scalable Reinforcement of Diffusion Models

arXiv · 2026-10-08 · 论文PDF

高效、稳定、可扩展的扩散模型强化（DiffusionART）

Online reinforcement learning (RL) fine-tunes diffusion models with black-box rewards, but reverse-process methods such as Flow-GRPO are limited in efficiency by poor credit assignment, while forward-process methods such as DiffusionNFT are efficient yet unstable without carefully tuned exponential-moving-average (EMA) off-policy training. We show that NFT-style objectives contain a hidden forward--reverse consistency force that joint positive--negative training cancels only partially, and that EMA stabilizes training through restoration rather than off-policy rollouts. We introduce DiffusionART (Diffusion Reinforcement with Aggregation-First Reward Tilting), which aggregates each prompt's rewards into a shared reward-improving direction for reverse-process states, removing the consistency force by construction, and adds a trust-region-masked second update for drift-adaptive restoration without EMA tuning. It requires no differentiable reward model, remains effective with fewer samples per prompt, and supports pairwise reward models (DiffusionART-Pairwise).
