Specification-Grounded Adaptive Triage and Formal Invariant Exclusion Synthesis for Autonomous Silicon Coverage Closure

arXiv · 2026-11-19 · 论文PDF

ARGUS：规范约束的自适应分诊与形式不变式排除，面向自主芯片覆盖收敛的 LLM 流程

Closing the final 5%-15% of code and functional coverage remains a major schedule bottleneck in silicon tapeouts. Existing LLM-based coverage closure loops feed raw uncovered RTL lines or branches directly into iterative prompts, failing on industrial subsystems due to: (1) token and context explosion from querying thousands of correlated holes independently; (2) illegal stimulus that violates cross-signal invariants and yields dirty coverage; and (3) unsafe exclusion hallucination on structurally unreachable dead code. We present ARGUS (Autonomous Reachability & Guided Unified Stimulus), a specification-grounded, dual-regime coverage closure and formal exclusion synthesis engine. ARGUS mines microarchitectural specs and SystemVerilog Assertions into a formal Block Contract (C), then runs adaptive Macro-Clustering vs. Micro-Surgery triage gated by a per-module hole-density threshold (tau): dense modules collapse correlated holes into root-cause mode, FSM, and backpressure clusters, while sparse modules use AST and loop-domain reachability to separate reachable corners from provably unreachable invariants.
