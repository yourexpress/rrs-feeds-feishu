A Unified Control Framework for Gradient Inversion Defense in Federated Learning

arXiv · 2026-11-24 · 论文PDF

Gradient perturbations are widely recognized as an effective defense against gradient inversion attacks in federated learning, but they are equally known to impair optimization, creating a persistent tension between privacy and utility. In this paper, we show that existing gradient perturbation defenses, despite their different formulations, can be unified under a controlled dynamics framework, providing a cohesive understanding of their optimization behavior and enabling the derivation of universal convergence and safety conditions without relying on case-specific analyses. Leveraging this observation, we propose ``Near Orthogonal Gradient Projection (NODE)'', which injects perturbations only along directions orthogonal to the original gradient so that the defended gradient maintains a fixed cosine similarity with the true gradient. By preserving the first-order descent direction, NODE substantially reduces the optimization cost of strong perturbations while maintaining effective protection against gradient inversion attacks.
