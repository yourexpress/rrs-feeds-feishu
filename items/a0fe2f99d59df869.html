Lost in a Few Heads: Diagnosing and Repairing Block Selection in Sparse Attention

arXiv cs.LG · 2026-11-16 · 论文PDF

诊断修复稀疏注意力的块选择（oracle 校准）

Sparse attention models read a few blocks of keys for every query at a fixed budget and can lose much of their long-context retrieval accuracy at that budget. We ask where this loss is made. Using exact attention mass selection at the shipped budget as an oracle, we show that the selector under-ranks the needle rather than running out of budget. We find attention heads that miss the needle by running a calibration probe that teacher-forces the answer and reads two quantities in every head. The first is the needle attention that the selector forfeits, and the second is the share of the answer's log-probability that fixing the head's selection restores, integrated along the path from the shipped selector to full attention. Four checkpoints of the InfLLM-V2 family and NOSA-8B lose multi-key retrieval in the same three blind heads, which attend to the needle while a mean-pooled key summary dilutes its single decisive token. Full attention in those heads or a 4-bit scorer that keeps the shipped budget recovers most of the loss without training.
