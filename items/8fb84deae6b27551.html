Preserving Pretrained CFG Structure in RL for Diffusion Models with Conditional Contrast Regularization

arXiv · 2026-11-20 · 论文PDF

扩散模型 RL 中保留预训练 CFG 结构的条件对比正则化

Reinforcement Learning (RL) has become an important component in the post-training of diffusion models. In practice, however, standard RL often improves the target reward without reliably preserving holistic generation quality, leading to unstable reward-quality trade-offs that we view as a form of reward hacking. Since Classifier-Free Guidance (CFG) is known to improve visual clarity, prompt alignment, and perceptual quality in generation, we investigate whether its underlying structure can serve as a useful prior during RL. We present a unified geometric view that interprets both CFG and RL as correction vectors in a common prediction space, and identify the projection of the RL-induced correction onto the pretrained CFG correction as a useful diagnostic of quality-related structural preservation. Geometric analysis and rotation intervention further show that the pretrained CFG direction carries information useful for generation quality that reward-driven RL updates do not necessarily preserve.
