Conformal Expert Pools: Training-Free Probabilistic Time-Series Forecasting Beyond Seasonality

arXiv cs.LG · 2026-11-16 · 论文PDF

共形专家池：超越季节性的免训练概率时序预测

Conformal Seasonal Pools (CSP) showed that a forecaster with no training at all, built only from past values at the same point in the season and from past forecast errors, significantly outperformed DeepNPTS, Amazon's learned non-parametric forecaster. We propose Conformal Expert Pools (CEP), a training-free successor. CEP replaces the single seasonal-naive forecast by a pool of simple forecasters, builds its predictive distribution from their past forecast errors, and adapts both the pool and the errors to the series being forecast. No model is trained and no parameter is learned across series. On the CSP benchmark of 232 datasets and 24,502 forecast windows, CEP is better than CSP on 78% of datasets by CRPS, on 84% by MSIS and on 83% by Winkler score; on a typical dataset its CRPS is 14% lower and its MSIS 23% lower (both p < 1e-22, paired Wilcoxon test), and coverage of 95% intervals rises from 90.1% to 92.2%. CEP has a lower CRPS than DeepNPTS on 88% of datasets, than NPTS on 89% and than SeasonalNPTS on 83%.
