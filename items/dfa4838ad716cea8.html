Serve open models in production with Foundry Managed Compute

Microsoft · 2026-11-20 · 技术博客 / 每日精选

微软 Foundry Managed Compute 正式 GA：面向开源模型托管 GPU 推理，目录 150+ 模型，支持 NVIDIA A100/H100/H200 与 AMD MI300X、US/EU Data Zone、安全供应链与 speculative decoding，并预告 LoRA 适配器与 BYOW 自定义权重预览

We are excited to announce the general availability of Managed Compute in Microsoft Foundry. When we introduced Managed Compute in preview at Microsoft Build in June, we set out to make serving open models as approachable as calling a managed model API. You choose the model that fits your application; we provision the GPUs, maintain the containers, and operate the serving runtime.

Since Build, we’ve scaled serving capacity, improved prompt reuse, and expanded GPU and deployment options, including US and EU Data Zones. Customer feedback helped us refine the experience and give teams a clearer view of how their deployments are running. We’ve also strengthened the software supply chain and added more open models to the catalog. The result is a managed platform for serving open models on dedicated GPUs in the Foundry experience you already use.

Getting started with open models on Foundry Managed Compute

Sign into the Foundry portal, navigate to the model catalog and filter by “Managed Compute” under “Deployment options” to discover the 150+ open models available. Select a model, then choose a deployment template that fits your workload. The template defines the context length, serving runtime, and GPU configuration. Depending on the model, you can choose NVIDIA GPUs (A100, H100, or H200) or AMD MI300X GPUs. Start with one instance, review the hourly cost in the deployment wizard, and test a few prompts in the Playground once the deployment is ready.
