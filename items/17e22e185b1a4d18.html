FREE-LIVO: Physically Decoupled Yet Tightly Coupled LiDAR-Inertial-Visual Odometry

arXiv · 2026-11-06 · 论文PDF

LiDAR-Inertial-Visual Odometry (LIVO) integrates complementary LiDAR, camera, and IMU measurements to achieve robust state estimation. However, most existing methods assume a fixed relative pose and accurate time synchronization between sensors. Toward modular and heterogeneous robotic systems, this paper presents FREE-LIVO, a tightly coupled odometry method that requires neither a rigid LiDAR-camera rig nor LiDAR-camera time synchronization. The proposed method uses a pretrained image feature matcher to establish correspondences between a camera image and a dense LiDAR intensity image generated from a single LiDAR scan using a generative model. From the resulting 3D point-to-pixel correspondences, we construct reprojection factors that directly constrain LiDAR and camera poses at different acquisition times. These factors are jointly optimized in a single factor graph together with constraints from point cloud registration, visual feature tracking, and the IMU measurements associated with each sensor.
