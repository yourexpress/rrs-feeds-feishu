Adaptive Warm-State Retention for Serverless Cold-Start Mitigation: Treating Keep-Alive Time as an Online Control Parameter

arXiv · 2026-11-20 · 论文PDF

Serverless platforms are easy to operate but pay a recurring price in cold starts: when a function is called after sitting idle, its execution environment must be rebuilt before the request can run, and latency suffers. The usual fix keeps idle environments alive for a fixed window, but a single value rarely fits a changing workload: too high wastes idle capacity, too low causes avoidable cold starts. We argue the keep-alive window should not be a fixed deployment constant; instead we treat it as a per-function runtime parameter, adjusted online from what the system observes, kept separate from how many replicas to run. We build an external retention controller for OpenFaaS/faasd that needs no platform changes, driven by two simple agents: a reactive AIMD (Additive-Increase Multiplicative-Decrease) rule and a UCB1 multi-armed bandit.
