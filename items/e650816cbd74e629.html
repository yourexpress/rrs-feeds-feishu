Scaling Looped MoE Language Models under Matched Budgets: Information Flow and Parameter Allocation

arXiv (cs.LG) · 2026-11-25 · 论文PDF / 每日精选

匹配预算下扩展循环 MoE 语言模型：信息流与参数分配原则

Looped Transformers decouple computational depth from parameter count, and prior studies have demonstrated promising performance gains with increased computation. However, systematic design principles for configuring these models under strict iso-parameter and iso-FLOP constraints remain scarce. We contribute to this direction by improving information flow in looped models and systematically studying loop parameter configurations. Specifically, we refine this information flow via gated inner-loop state updates and a cross-stage residual connection. Building on this design, controlled studies identify a configuration combining a single-pass Core depth of 25%-35% of the total unrolled depth with reallocating saved parameters into extra routed experts per layer, improving performance at zero extra compute while preserving compatibility with existing training infrastructure. Evaluated after 1T pre-training tokens, our 69B MLA and 51B KDA Mixture-of-Experts models outperform budget-matched non-recurrent baselines by an average of 1.16 and 2.29 percentage points across four benchmarks, respectively.
