HLWalk: Modal-Split Random-Walk Thermal Analysis for Heterogeneous 2.5D/3D ICs

arXiv · 2026-11-23 · 论文PDF

Efficient thermal analysis is essential for accelerating the design of 2.5D/3D ICs. However, existing random-walk thermal solvers can suffer from boundary discretization errors and high computational costs when resolving fine-scale structures such as through-silicon vias (TSVs). To address these challenges, we propose HLWalk, an efficient random-walk thermal solver for heterogeneous 2.5D/3D ICs. We introduce local boundary integral reconstruction to eliminate discretization errors in local-time-based boundary treatments. To accelerate boundary preprocessing, we employ modal splitting: low-order modes are computed deterministically, while Monte Carlo sampling estimates only the residual. We further develop scale-aware random-walk transport to efficiently resolve fine-scale structures without requiring fine discretization throughout the domain. Experiments on 2.5D/3D IC benchmarks show that HLWalk achieves remarkable wall-clock speedup and better accuracy across settings.
