AgriManager: A Framework and Benchmark for LLM-RL Generalization in Agricultural Management

arXiv · 2026-11-21 · 论文PDF

Agricultural management is heterogeneous along two axes. The first is distributional shift within a fixed task interface: weather, crops, and prices vary across years and locations while sensors, actions, and objective stay fixed. The second is structural shift across interfaces: different farms expose different observed variables, available actions, and management objectives. We formalize the second axis as schema shift: variation in the policy-facing schema $Σ= (Σ_O, Σ_A, Σ_R)$ between training and deployment. We test whether LLM-RL generalizes across both axes with AgriManager, to our knowledge the first framework that trains LLM policies with RL across multiple process-based crop simulators, unifying DSSAT, WOFOST, and Cycles. We design a three-tier generalization ladder: Tier 1 within-schema distributional shift, Tier 2 single-axis schema shift along $Σ_O / Σ_A / Σ_R$, Tier 3 compositional schema shift across simulators, all scored on a frozen held-out test suite with three training seeds per method.
