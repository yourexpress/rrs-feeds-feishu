SpaceXAI releases a separate Grok iOS app for enterprise users（Grok for Intune）

面向 Microsoft Intune 托管组织的 Grok 独立企业应用上架 App Store。

Back in May, OpenAI released a dedicated version of their mobile app for enterprise. Now SpaceXAI is doing the same thing for Grok.

Grok for Intune now available

The new enterprise-specific app is called Grok for Intune. SpaceXAI says the new dedicated app is “the Microsoft Intune edition of Grok, the AI assistant by xAI — built for organizations that manage apps and data with Intune.”

“It supports Intune Mobile Application Management, honors your organization’s app protection policies, and signs in with your work email,” the company says.

SpaceXAI highlights these features with Grok for Intune:

AI assistant

- Ask anything and get fast answers, run deep research on the web, or work through hard problems in math, science, and coding.

- Upload documents and photos, or paste links, and have Grok analyze and explain the content.

Grok Imagine

- Generate striking images from simple text prompts, or upload images and have Grok edit them — remove objects, change the scenery, or apply any art style.

- Create short videos with sound from text prompts, or turn static photos into video.

Voice Mode

- Talk to Grok and get natural-sounding replies, hands-free.

- Live Camera: share what you see and let Grok reason about it in real time.

Real-time information

- With access to web and X data, Grok’s answers stay current on the latest news, trends, and developments for any topic.

Built for managed devices

- Deployed and configured by your IT team through Microsoft Intune.

- Honors your organization’s app protection policies, including save, share, and data-transfer restrictions.

- Work email sign-in. No in-app purchases.

Grok for Intune is now available on the App Store.
