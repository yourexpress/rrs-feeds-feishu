小米玄戒 O3 / O100 消费级综述

- The Xring O3 is a 3nm SoC manufactured by TSMC that introduces LPDDR6 memory and achieves record performance in AnTuTu.

- It debuts exclusively in the Chinese market, being integrated into the Xiaomi 18 Fold foldable phone and the Pad 9 Pro Max tablet.

- It is part of a technological independence strategy along with the O100 AI and D100 driving chips.

Xiaomi has decided to make a bold move in the hardware world, making it clear that it no longer wants to be a mere assembler of other companies' parts. With the arrival of the Xring O3 , the Chinese firm is entering the big leagues, designing its own electronic processors to avoid being dependent on the release schedules and prices of Qualcomm or MediaTek. It's a risky but ambitious move that seeks to give them that touch of exclusivity that until now only giants like Apple or Huawei possessed.

While this might seem like a game of numbers and benchmarks to us in the West, the reality is that we're witnessing a paradigm shift . Xiaomi isn't just aiming for faster devices; it wants to create an ecosystem where mobile phones, electric cars, and smart homes speak the same language thanks to custom-designed hardware. However, there's an important caveat: for now, it seems this entire display of power is confined to China , leaving global users wanting more.

The heart of the beast: Xring O3 specifications

This processor is no ordinary device. Manufactured using TSMC's 3-nanometer lithography , the Xring O3 is the direct successor to the Xring O1 released in 2025. Its most impressive feature is its CPU architecture, boasting 10 cores organized into three clusters: two C1-Ultra cores reaching up to 4,35 GHz, four C1-Premium cores at 3,68 GHz, and four C1-Pro cores at 3,15 GHz. This configuration is designed to deliver phenomenal multi-threaded performance , far surpassing its predecessor.
