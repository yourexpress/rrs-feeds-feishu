Selecting Stable Sampling Dynamics for Compositional Diffusion: A Feynman-Kac Weight Reduction

arXiv · 2026-11-22 · 论文PDF

Given $K$ separately trained diffusion models, how should one sample from a $β_k$-weighted product of their terminal laws without jointly retraining the experts? Existing Feynman--Kac constructions provide many weighted stochastic differential equations (SDEs) with the correct target marginal. The consequential question is how to select useful proposal dynamics among them. We study an explicit family of weighted diffusions that realizes the same product-of-intermediate-experts trajectory. A scalar parameter $λ$ controls how strongly the proposal drift follows the composed expert score, while an additional Langevin component can be varied independently. Writing $B=\sum_{k=1}^{K}β_k>0$, we identify the distinguished choice $λ=1/B$. At this value, the aggregate squared-score term in the instantaneous Feynman--Kac log-weight rate cancels exactly. For positive expert exponents, the remaining score-disagreement contribution is non-positive. We call this structural cancellation Feynman--Kac reduction.
