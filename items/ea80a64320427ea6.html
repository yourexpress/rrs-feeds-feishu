Improved (Minimum) Spanning Tree Construction in the HYBRID Model

arXiv · 2026-11-20 · 论文PDF

HYBRID 模型中改进的(最小)生成树构造

In the HYBRID$_0$ model, vertices exchange $O(\log n)$-bit messages in synchronous rounds, both along input edges (local communication) and directly to vertices whose IDs they know (global communication). The local capacity is the maximum number of messages a vertex may send per round to each input neighbor, and the global capacity is the maximum number of global messages it may send, and also receive, per round. The standard capacity is local capacity $O(1)$ and global capacity $O(\log n)$. A well-formed tree (WFT) is a virtual tree of maximum degree $O(1)$ and diameter $O(\log n)$. Known algorithms construct a spanning tree (ST) and a WFT either in $O(\log n)$ rounds with global capacity $O(\log^2 n)$ or with standard capacity in $O(\log^2 n)$ rounds. We give a randomized algorithm that constructs an ST and a WFT in every connected component of any graph in $O(\log n)$ rounds with high probability under standard capacity. Our approach is based on a $Δ$-pseudo-spanning representation ($Δ$-PSR): a virtual network of maximum degree $Δ$ with restoration information, such that any spanning tree of this network can be restored to a spanning tree of the input graph in $O(\log n)$ rounds…
