Learning to Defer under Expert Drift

The learning to defer (L2D) framework enables safety in machine learning and AI systems by allocating difficult or critical decisions to a human expert. However, most prior work on L2D assumes fixed expert reliability, overlooking temporal fluctuations in human performance due to factors such as fatigue and cognitive biases. Humans commonly exhibit such substantial, systematic performance shifts across a wide range of domains, from high-stakes settings such as radiology, aviation, and judicial decision-making, to everyday tasks such as crowdsourced image annotation. We propose Expert-Drift-Adapted Learning-to-Defer (EDA-L2D), a novel expert-aware L2D framework that explicitly models these temporal shifts by conditioning the deferral head on recent histories of expert outcomes via a sequence model. This history-aware approach enables adaptive deferral policies that anticipate reliability fluctuations and better allocate decisions between human and machine over time.
