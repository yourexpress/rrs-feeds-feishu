Spatio-Temporal Multi-Order Interaction for Video Recognition

arXiv · 2026-11-05 · 论文PDF

Many temporal approaches for video recognition rely on explicit cross-channel aggregation projections, which have quadratic complexity in the number of channels, introducing significant computational overhead. In addition, the spatio-temporal representation used for video recognition is biased either toward local order interactions (for CNNs) or toward global order interactions (for transformers). Starting from the multi-order spatial representation of MogaNet, our method introduces two complementary contributions to tackle these problems. First, for the temporal modelling, we introduce parallel multi-order processing with depthwise-only convolutions and implicit gating-based aggregation, which is linear in the number of channels, inducing a considerable computational reduction. Second, we propose a dual interaction between spatial and temporal dimensions, which allows a better spatio-temporal representation without increasing the computational cost.
