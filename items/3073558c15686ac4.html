CompCert4Lean: Porting a Verified C Compiler to Lean in Six Weeks with Parallel Coding Agents

arXiv cs.PL · 2026-11-16 · 论文PDF

CompCert4Lean：并行编码agent六周将验证C编译器移植到Lean

We present CompCert4Lean, a large-scale port of CompCert 3.17's verified compiler core from Rocq to Lean, completed in approximately six weeks by one expert directing parallel LLM agents. Our migration workflow partitions the development along its dependencies, audits translated definitions and theorem statements before large-scale proof reconstruction, and then reconstructs proofs in parallel against stable interfaces. The resulting compiler covers typed CompCert C through x86-64 assembly, discharges all 4,908 recorded proof obligations without project-specific axioms, and produces executable Linux binaries. The workflow enabled the expert to concentrate on organizing the work rather than supplying technical solutions. Of 1,071 human interactions, 88.8% concerned workflow and coordination, while only three supplied technical direction. Optimization campaigns further demonstrate the benefit of the Lean port for agent-driven verified development.
