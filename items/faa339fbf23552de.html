Well-Log Reconstruction with Pre-trained Geological Knowledge: A Knowledge-Conditioned Large-Language-Model Generative Agent

arXiv cs.LG · 2026-11-14 · 论文PDF

预训练地质知识条件LLM的测井重建

Missing well-log intervals make subsurface reconstruction ill-posed. Existing numerical regression models lack geological prior knowledge to constrain ambiguous inverse mappings. We propose GeoLog-Agent, a knowledge-conditioned generative agent using pre-trained large language models (LLMs) as a source of geological priors. A universal Geo-Harness translates LLM stratigraphic reasoning into formation-anchored petrophysical trajectories, feature-wise linear modulation, and conditional adversarial constraints. On the Norwegian North Sea benchmark, GeoLog-Agent achieves a mean $R^2$ of 0.9307 and a peak $R^2$ of 0.9713. It exceeds the nine-baseline average $R^2$ of 0.8387 by 0.0920, with a 30.97% average RMSE reduction, and exceeds the strongest baseline by 0.0274--0.0498 in $R^2$, with 16.25%--24.05% lower RMSE. Ablations confirm the effectiveness and synergy of geological prior reasoning, multi-scale dilated generation, and conditional adversarial modules. Transferring the coupling mechanism to six conventional deep learning models improves their $R^2$ by 0.0162--0.0789 with cross-well stability.
