Eliciting Research Taste in LLMs through Future Research Direction Choice

arXiv cs.LG · 2026-11-15 · 论文PDF

通过未来研究方向选择引出LLM研究品味

Research taste is increasingly discussed in the context of AI scientists, yet remains little studied or measured in LLMs. This judgment matters especially in open-ended research, where a direction must be chosen before a verifiable objective is available. We operationalize research taste through one task of the scientific workflow: choosing which future research direction to pursue given a research goal and current results. Rather than predicting downstream scientometric metrics or treating author choices as objectively correct, we evaluate models against human-authored future-work directions and report author alignment, stable divergence, and unstable choice as behavioral measures of research-direction preference. We construct an order-controlled evaluation of 80 pairwise comparisons from 19 ML papers published between December 2025 and February 2026 across four subdomains. Each query presents a research goal, hypotheses tested with key results, and two candidate future directions: one author-recommended direction and one plausible distractor sampled from a similar paper.
