GateTrim: Adaptive-Rank Quantization for Large Language Models

arXiv (cs.LG) · 2026-10-31 · 论文PDF

We introduce GateTrim, an adaptive-rank post-training quantization method for large language models that learns matrix-specific ranks through gate optimization and threshold-based rank selection. GateTrim standardizes the low-rank factors of a quantized low-rank--residual representation, learns a separate gate vector for each target weight matrix, and uses a shared threshold to automatically determine its retained rank. The resulting ranks can differ across matrices, and the compact quantized representations are subsequently reconstructed. We evaluate GateTrim under W4A8 on Qwen3-4B/8B/14B, Llama-3.2-3B-Instruct, and Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct. Across all five models, we observe a counterintuitive finding: increasing the threshold from $τ=0.3$ to $τ=0.7$ reduces the mean retained rank by 46.60%--67.89% while simultaneously lowering WikiText-2 perplexity by 0.59%--5.17%. Threshold 0.7 achieves the lowest mean retained rank and final perplexity among the three tested thresholds, 0.3, 0.5, and 0.7, and reduces measured target-export size by 1.50%--4.25% relative to 0.3.
