State-Augmented Learning for Post-Deployment Adaptation of Coexisting O-RAN Applications

arXiv (cs.LG) · 2026-11-01 · 论文PDF

Fixed control policies in Open RAN can require retraining when service requirements, traffic loads, or interacting applications change. We propose ATTUNE, a state-augmented learning method for an energy saving application that coexists with a learned mobility load balancing application under a service constraint. ATTUNE conditions the energy saving policy on a Lagrange multiplier and updates this scalar during operation using measured service degradation, while keeping both applications' network parameters fixed. To train a single policy across service requirements, ATTUNE samples target service levels uniformly and maps them to multipliers through an adaptive table. This procedure accounts for policy-dependent multiplier responses and improves energy savings compared with training over a widened fixed multiplier range. We characterize the induced distribution of service levels and the convergence and fluctuations of the online update. An exact finite-window identity establishes convergence of average measured degradation to the target when the projection corrections vanish on average.
