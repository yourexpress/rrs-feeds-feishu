Sparse PCA with Overlapping Supports: NP-Hardness and an FPTAS

arXiv cs.LG · 2026-11-11 · 论文PDF

In sparse principal component analysis (SPCA) with several components, each must be sparse and all must be pairwise orthogonal. When the supports of the components overlap, these two requirements interact. For a covariance matrix of fixed rank, polynomial-time algorithms with optimality or approximation guarantees are known for one component, and for several components with disjoint supports or with a common support. We study the general model, with individual sparsity constants and arbitrary support overlaps. We are given a rational $n\times r$ matrix $R$ and $d$ sparsity constants $s_i \le n$, and the goal is to find an $n\times d$ matrix $X$ with orthonormal columns that maximizes $\|R^\top X\|_F^2$, such that column $i$ has at most $s_i$ non-zero entries. Here $RR^\top$ plays the role of the covariance matrix and has rank at most $r$. We show that SPCA is NP-hard even when $r = d = 2$, but admits a fully polynomial-time approximation scheme (FPTAS) when $r$ and $d$ are fixed. The returned solution has exactly orthonormal columns, and its entries are real algebraic numbers in an exact representation.
