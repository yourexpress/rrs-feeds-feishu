AI4Plasma I: Data-Efficient Surrogate Modeling of Capacitively Coupled Plasma Fields

arXiv cs.LG · 2026-11-11 · 论文PDF

Reference capacitively coupled plasma (CCP) simulations provide spatially resolved plasma fields but are costly to generate across operating conditions. We compare the data efficiency of five surrogate architectures--Coordinate MLP, PCA-Net, U-Net, Fourier neural operator (FNO), and Transolver--for predicting RF-cycle-averaged electron density, ion density, electric potential, and electron temperature in 1D planar and 2D axisymmetric GEC CCP benchmarks. We assess the number of reference simulations needed to achieve 1% and 2% mean relative L2 error for parameter-interior and parameter-boundary test cases, and examine architecture-specific mechanisms. FNO achieves the lowest or joint-lowest reference-case requirement in seven of eight comparisons. In 1D, FNO reaches 1% interior error with 50 training cases, compared with 100 for Transolver and 200 for U-Net. In 2D, FNO and U-Net each reach 1% interior error with 100 cases; FNO and Transolver each reach 2% parameter-boundary error with 200 cases, compared with 400 for U-Net.
