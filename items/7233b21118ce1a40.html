Characterizing Serverless Workloads: A Large-Scale Analysis of Function-as-a-Service Platforms

arXiv (cs.DC) · 2026-11-03 · 论文PDF

大规模 serverless 负载特征分析：跨平台 FaaS 工作负载的冷启动/弹性伸缩/调度洞察

Serverless computing is a popular approach to application deployment without having to manage the underlying infrastructure. Its most common implementation is Function-as-a-Service (FaaS), in which applications are structured as fine-grained functions. FaaS providers face non-trivial tasks in the management of their platforms, including cold starts, auto-scaling, and scheduling. The design of optimized platforms requires an accurate understanding of realistic serverless workloads. However, existing research and platforms rely on insights from single, often outdated datasets. In this work, we conduct a large-scale, cross-platform comparative analysis of eight publicly available FaaS workload traces from major cloud providers (Alibaba, Azure, Huawei, IBM) and a scientific computing platform (Globus). We provide a comprehensive and up-to-date view of the serverless ecosystem by characterizing key dimensions such as invocation patterns, latency, cold start behavior, and resource utilization.
