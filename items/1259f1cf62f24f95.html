FairPGT: Partitioned Graph Transformers for Dual-Level Fair Graph Clustering

arXiv · 2026-11-17 · 论文PDF

FairPGT：分区图 Transformer 的双层公平图聚类

Clustering on attributed graphs can produce demographically biased clusters, particularly when communities align with protected groups. Graph transformers can amplify this bias through same-group attention, while global attention limits scalability. We propose FairPGT, a partitioned graph transformer for fair graph clustering. FairPGT restricts attention to METIS partitions and connects them through routing tokens, which divides the quadratic cost of attention by the number of partitions. A graph-aware self-training target guides clustering, with a provably bounded deviation from the standard DEC target. Fairness is imposed at two levels: a mutual-information term promotes demographic parity in cluster assignments, while a normalized cross-group attention ratio (nCGAR) guides attention toward group neutrality through self-distillation. We prove that the nCGAR correction moves attention toward neutrality and establish a condition preventing overshooting.
