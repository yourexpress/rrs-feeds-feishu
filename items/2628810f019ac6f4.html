Stacked Intelligent Metasurface-Based MultiModal Feature Fusion for Collaborative Inference in Edge Networks

arXiv · 2026-10-09 · 论文PDF

用堆叠智能超表面(SIM)作物理计算单元，同频谱内做多用户OTA特征融合的边缘协同推理

In navigating the challenges of computational and communication overhead within the 6G-era Collaborative Inference (CI), this paper explores the feasibility of employing Stacked Intelligent Metasurface (SIM) to perform CI, termed SIM-CI. The SIM functions as a physical computing unit, receiving multi-user Over-The-Air (OTA) aggregated features within the same spectrum. It thus executes the necessary spatial-frequency combining and inference in a single physical wave propagation.SIM-CI also incorporates a tri-heterogeneity robustness adapter to address the network heterogeneity, such as multi-modal data and user inference models, as well as a quality-aware contrastive approach that align features from different modalities and extraction levels into a unified feature space. Considering the heterogeneous and dynamic channels, we further integrate Channel State Information (CSI) as an additional input into the adapter design, enabling users' early perception of potential channel states.
