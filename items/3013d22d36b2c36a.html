Verifiable Logical-Physical Contracts for Refactored On-Die Power Control Circuitry

arXiv · 2026-11-23 · 论文PDF

Recent literature allows independent hardware experiments to occupy a single die for scalable and cost-efficient silicon prototyping. Within this framework, specialized hardware functions including low-power control mechanisms can be offered as reusable on-die microservices. However, this requires well-defined contracts connecting digital requests to physical behavior. We present a checkable logical-physical contract formulation including a physical contract model describing service completion between client and provider through formally verified digital guarantees and physically validated completion conditions. We introduce Lightswitch, a proof-of-concept on-die power control microservice. We assess post-layout designs in TSMC 28nm and demonstrate how checkable contracts provide formal guarantees.
