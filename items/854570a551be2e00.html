Beyond Overparameterization: Provable Learning of Input-Convex Multi-Layer Polynomial Networks with Active Queries

arXiv cs.LG · 2026-10-06 · 论文PDF

主动查询下输入凸多层多项式网络的可证明学习

The theoretical understanding of multi-layer neural networks is largely confined to overparameterized settings, which obscure parameter identifiability and incur high sample complexity. Neural tangent kernel (NTK) provides a general theory for wide networks, but does not offer efficient sample-complexity guarantees. Recent feature-learning results go beyond kernel methods for single-neuron, multi-index, and hierarchical targets. However, the analysis is often restricted to shallow or specific architectures and to the overparameterized regime. We break this paradigm to achieve parameter-level recovery of deep target networks, albeit by using active data queries. Specifically, we study $L$-layer polynomial networks with even degree-$k$ monomial activations and nonnegative higher-layer weights.
