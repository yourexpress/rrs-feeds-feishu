Efficient VLA Reinforcement Learning via Subchunk Rollouts in Latent World Models

arXiv · 2026-11-20 · 论文PDF

LS-MBPO：潜世界模型的高效 VLA RL

Model-based reinforcement learning (MBRL) can improve pretrained vision-language-action (VLA) policies through imagined experiences. However, existing VLA-MBRL methods primarily rely on large-scale image or video generation world models, incurring large computational overhead. Latent world models offer a more efficient alternative but face two challenges: compounding errors during recursive prediction and incompatibility with VLAs' visual input interfaces. In this work, we propose LS-MBPO, a lightweight residual MBRL framework that leverages latent world models to improve upon pretrained VLAs. LS-MBPO introduces two key techniques: (1) Hierarchical Forcing, which combines dense causal supervision with autoregressive feedback to mitigate compounding prediction errors; and (2) Latent Subchunk Rollouts, which leverage action chunks from a frozen VLA policy and a latent residual policy for successive subchunk refinement, bridging the gap between latent imagination and VLA policy execution.
