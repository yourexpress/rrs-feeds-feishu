Who Asks, Who Answers: Linear Attention Builds the Query in Hybrid Language Models

arXiv (cs.AI) · 2026-11-03 · 论文PDF / 每日精选

揭示混合语言模型中线性注意力通过 Encode-Address 电路构造 softmax 查询，跨架构移植可恢复检索准确率 0.92-0.94

Hybrid language models interleave linear-attention layers with a small number of softmax-attention layers. Prior work largely attributes in-context retrieval to softmax attention, treating linear attention primarily as a mechanism for compressing past context. We revisit this division of labor and show that linear attention plays a direct role in retrieval by constructing the query used by softmax attention. Studying Qwen3.5 (Gated DeltaNet) and Granite4.0 (Mamba-2), we identify an Encode--Address circuit. An early Encode block preserves token identity, while an Address block constructs the final-position representation from which a softmax \emph{reader} forms its attention query. Transplanting this representation between prompts with different queried keys redirects retrieval in 0.97--0.99 of cases. The Address block's recurrent state carries the retrieval target, and its computation transfers across architectures: replacing Granite's Address block with Qwen's restores retrieval accuracy to 0.92--0.94 without updating either model's weights.
