PromptPack: Training-Free Augmentation of Diffusion Editors for Intent-Aligned Spatial and Semantic Control

arXiv cs.LG · 2026-11-16 · 论文PDF

扩散编辑器的免训练意图对齐增强

Many image-editing tasks require confining changes to intended spatial regions while exactly preserving user-designated source content. Instruction-driven editing pipelines often leave such user-intent underspecified, while inpainting editors condition generation on explicitly defined edit support but not on the spatial assignment of individual edit concepts. PromptPack addresses both limitations by equipping diffusion editors with two explicit 'Edit Guides': an edit-role partition that labels each pixel as 'Keep,' 'Permit,' or 'Modify' to specify where edits may occur, and a regional-prompt assignment that maps spatial regions to text prompts to specify which concepts apply where. These guides may be manually specified or, for source-object edits, automatically derived from a text-grounded SAM 3 mask. Without retraining, PromptPack injects independently embedded regional prompts into the editor's fixed-length text sequence and uses the Edit Guides to spatially mask attention, gate flow updates, and restore protected source pixels.
