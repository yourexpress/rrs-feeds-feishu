Statistical and Privacy Guarantees for Deep M-Regression via Stochastic Gradient Langevin Dynamics

arXiv · 2026-11-06 · 论文PDF

In this article, we study deep nonparametric regression trained via stochastic gradient Langevin dynamics (SGLD), a variant of stochastic gradient descent that injects carefully calibrated Gaussian noise during optimization. Using a PAC-Bayesian analysis tailored to deep ReLU networks, we establish an excess-risk bound that simultaneously accounts for network approximation error, statistical complexity, and the algorithmic cost accumulated along the SGLD trajectory. Under a Bernstein condition and within a stable SGLD regime, the excess-risk bound contains a stochastic term whose coefficient is inversely proportional to the variance of injected noise, while the estimator retains the optimal rate for nonparametric regression under the Hölder smoothness condition up to logarithmic factors. This result provides an approximation-error-aware, rate-optimal statistical perspective on the widely observed generalization benefits of moderate SGLD noise. For per-sample clipped SGLD, we further establish an exact f-DP guarantee and the corresponding asymptotic $μ$-GDP guarantee for the proposed estimator.
