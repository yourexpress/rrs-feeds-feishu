TangentPDE: Learning Local Operator Responses with Sparse Finite-Difference Supervision

arXiv · 2026-11-18 · 论文PDF

TangentPDE：稀疏有限差分监督学习局部算子响应

Neural PDE operators are often optimized through state reconstruction, which largely constrains operator values at observed inputs but does not fully characterize their sensitivity to local perturbations. A more faithful approximation of operator behavior requires not only accurate state predictions, but also correct variations along local perturbation directions. We present TangentPDE, which uses finite differences to approximate directional derivatives and constructs task-relevant finite-step responses from the state outputs of a reference solver at sparse paired inputs. Its objective jointly constrains state fidelity, response-direction alignment, and response-magnitude calibration, thereby learning both operator values and their local variations. Because the response labels are constructed solely from state outputs at paired inputs, the method requires neither explicit derivative labels, linearized or adjoint PDE solves, nor full Jacobians, and remains applicable to nonlinear solvers without derivative interfaces.
