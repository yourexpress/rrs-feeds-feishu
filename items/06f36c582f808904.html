VINO: Revisiting 3D Representation Learning with Volume Transformers

arXiv · 2026-11-06 · 论文PDF

Self-supervised learning has given rise to vision foundation models that learn general-purpose image representations transferable across downstream tasks, yet representation learning for 3D scenes has not achieved comparable success. Motivated by this, we identify key limitations of current 3D representation learning approaches and revisit their core design choices. We first replace the commonly used hierarchical PTv3 backbone with the Volume Transformer, a ViT-like architecture for 3D scenes. We then redesign two core objectives of modern approaches built around student-teacher networks: matching representations across views and predicting masked content from visible context. For the first objective, we separate the roles of teacher and student: the teacher provides clean, consistent targets while the student learns invariance to stronger perturbations. For the second objective, we prevent the student from accessing the masked geometry and instead require it to infer missing representations from visible context only.
