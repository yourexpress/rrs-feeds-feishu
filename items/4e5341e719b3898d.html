Multi-Tier Edge Triage: Optimizing Latency for Safe Clinical Agents

arXiv · 2026-11-20 · 论文PDF

Multi-Tier Edge Triage：临床 agent 安全分流延迟优化

Deploying generative AI agents in enterprise clinical workflows requires enforcing multi-dimensional safety and compliance policies--preventing HIPAA Protected Health Information (PHI) exposure, maintaining clinical scope-of-practice boundaries, ensuring patient-appropriate reading comprehension, and preserving brand integrity--under interactive latency constraints. Synchronous outbound calls to cloud Large Language Model (LLM) judges add substantial network and decoding overhead onto the critical response path. We present Multi-Tier Edge Triage, a safety screening architecture that co-locates a compact Tier-1 sequence classifier on the clinical agent's host server, resolving clear queries in-process without a network round-trip and escalating only ambiguous queries to a Tier-2 cloud LLM judge. To evaluate Tier-1 encoders without length or unigram shortcuts, we construct four length-matched contrastive twin benchmarks seeded from MedSafetyBench, ASQ-PHI, and a synthetic four-mandate clinical assistant policy (Generic HealthCorp).
