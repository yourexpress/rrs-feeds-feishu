When Strong Reasoners Join the Pool: Small Union Premiums and Costly Runaway Generations in LLM Routing

arXiv (cs.LG) · 2026-11-03 · 论文PDF

强推理模型加入路由池：联合响应溢价实测（3.5pp）与代价高昂的失控生成

Routing benchmarks often judge a router against a hindsight oracle that counts a query as solved whenever any model's recorded answer is correct, but under stochastic decoding that oracle mixes two different objects: the clairvoyant ceiling of committing to one model per query and the premium of calling several models and keeping any correct answer. A companion study measured this premium at 3.5 percentage points (pp) for 11 small open models on MATH-500; how large it is once current reasoning models enter the pool was unknown. We measure it with a fixed-protocol, pre-committed generation on a single NVIDIA B300: all 500 MATH-500 problems, 30 seeded samples per problem, Llama-3.3-70B-Instruct and NVIDIA's Nemotron Nano 9B v2 and Nemotron Super 49B v1.5 (a NAS derivative of the same 70B) under vendor sampling, a 65,536-token cap, a pinned Math-Verify scorer, and an append-only attempt ledger, for 45,000 scored responses. The two reasoning models both reach 96.1% accuracy against 76.2% for the 70B baseline.
