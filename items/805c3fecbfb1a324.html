EPI-SST: Renewal-Structured State-Space Transformers for Epidemic Forecasting

arXiv cs.LG · 2026-11-16 · 论文PDF

更新结构状态空间Transformer用于流行病预测EPI-SST

Epidemic forecasting supports public health decisions ranging from capacity planning to intervention timing, for which both point accuracy and calibrated uncertainty are essential. Although neural models have advanced point forecasting, producing reliable probabilistic predictions over multiple future horizons remains challenging. Renewal models encode epidemic propagation, while neural forecasters learn flexible multi-horizon representations; integrating these strengths within a probabilistic future rollout remains underexplored. We propose EPI-SST (Epidemic State Space Transformer), a probabilistic forecaster centered on an evidence-driven renewal transition. At each horizon, neural evidence produces a bounded update to an internal multiplier and advances a finite-memory renewal state that generates the future mean trajectory. A separate heteroscedastic Gaussian head controls predictive scale using a validation-selected log-variance penalty. Across four epidemic surveillance benchmarks, EPI-SST achieves the lowest pooled RMSE on every dataset, reducing RMSE by up to 14.2 percent relative to the strongest baseline.
