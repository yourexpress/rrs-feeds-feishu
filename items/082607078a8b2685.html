Operation-aware Multimodal Clinical Decision Support System for ICU Discharge Risk Assessment

arXiv cs.LG · 2026-11-09 · 论文PDF

Unplanned 30-day intensive care unit (ICU) readmission is a critical benchmark for evaluating healthcare quality. Most predictive models assume readmission risk depends solely on a patient's individual pathophysiological state, ignoring hospital operational context and resource capacity. In practice, discharge decisions are dynamic trade-offs between patient stability and bed turnover pressures. To address this gap, we develop an Operation-aware Multimodal Clinical Decision Support System (CDSS) for ICU discharge risk assessment. The system combines multi-agent collaborative extraction with self-evolving retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) to eliminate template noise from progress notes, while constructing departmental workload indicators alongside admission flow features, continuous vital signs, and sparse medical codes (ICD/DRG). Addressing severe class imbalance (about 10.5% readmissions) and alarm fatigue, we implement data normalization, class reweighting, and decision-threshold calibration.
