HKD-ACPA: Heterogeneous Knowledge Distillation Adversarial Cyber-Physical Attacks in Power Grids

arXiv · 2026-11-22 · 论文PDF

Learning-based algorithms assist power-grid control centers in localizing transmission-line attacks, yet existing adversarial attacks typically require white-box access or repeated online queries. We study a practical zero-query setting in which an adversary coordinates physical, denial-of-service (DoS), and false data injection (FDI) attacks using only local measurements and historical detector outputs. To bridge the input-view asymmetry between local adversarial observations and DoS-masked control-center measurements, we propose Heterogeneous Knowledge Distillation Adversarial Cyber-Physical Attacks (HKD-ACPA). HKD-ACPA distills historical detector predictions into a heterogeneous graph surrogate ensemble and trains a residual calibration module to correct the view-induced logit discrepancy. Over this calibrated surrogate, we formulate sparse, magnitude-bounded FDI synthesis with a group-balanced objective that jointly induces missed detections and false alarms, and solve the resulting hybrid discrete--continuous problem via bi-level projected gradient-based proximal policy optimization (PGPPO).
