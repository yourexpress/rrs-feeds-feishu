Parallel Computing Systems for the Optimal Selection of Process Equipment

arXiv cs.DC · 2026-11-12 · 论文PDF

This monograph addresses the optimal selection of equipment for chemical-engineering systems of multiproduct batch plants, a large-scale NP-hard discrete optimization problem in which, for each processing stage, a unit of standard size must be chosen so that the total capital cost of the equipment is minimized while the system remains operable and produces the required products within a given time horizon. We present the mathematical model of system operation and analyze its computational properties. We then develop a sequential branch-and-bound algorithm and its parallel versions for shared-memory systems (OpenMP), distributed-memory clusters (MPI, master-worker), and graphics processing units (CUDA), including recursive and iterative tree traversal, shared-memory optimization, and branch-divergence reduction. To overcome the exponential growth of the run time, we propose a hybrid metaheuristic in which Ant Colony Optimization finds a feasible initial solution and Simulated Annealing improves it, and we implement it on GPUs with thousands of independent threads.
