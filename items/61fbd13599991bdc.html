The Alignment Tax Is Graded: Task-Selective Capability Loss Under Length-Normalized DPO with SFT Retention

arXiv · 2026-11-06 · 论文PDF

Setting. This is an empirical characterization study; we propose no new method and run no comparisons against alternative objectives. We fine-tune Qwen3.5-4B with sparse LoRA and length-normalized DPO plus an SFT retention term (the RPO objective) on structured financial-filing tasks, and track downstream capability as the implicit reward margin grows to $\sim 7$ $β$-scaled units (nf4 training; $\sim 3$ under fp16 measurement on the unquantized base). Question. Does margin growth degrade capability uniformly across tasks, or selectively? Findings. In a pre-registered study (8 seeds, 6 unseen at freeze) with artifact-aware scoring and TOST equivalence testing, the alignment tax is graded and task-selective: extraction is preserved ($Δ= -0.55$ pts, equivalent at $\pm5$ pts), json loses little ($Δ= -3.52$ pts, equivalence fails on a clustered item-level interval), and router degrades severely ($Δ= -30$ pts). Router's loss concentrates in a margin 1-3 damage window containing a transient format-break spike.
