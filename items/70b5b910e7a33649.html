Nonadiabatic dynamics through a conical intersection requires both static and dynamic correlation and, at every geometry, an excited-state gradient and interstate nonadiabatic coupling (NACME). Analytic multireference derivatives at this level have been confined to datacenter-scale hardware. We report device-resident \mbox{DMRG-QD-NEVPT2} with analytic gradients and NACMEs within the $8$\,GB of a consumer GPU: a density-matrix-renormalization-group (DMRG) active-space reference supplies the static correlation, and quasi-degenerate SC-NEVPT2 dresses it through a multi-state effective Hamiltonian that remains valid \emph{through} a conical intersection, where single-state perturbation theory develops a vanishing denominator and fails. Every gradient and NACME is one reverse-mode transpose of a single contraction graph: the DMRG sweep enters as a gauge-free differentiable node, and all two-electron work is AO-direct through Cholesky factors. The DMRG reference is exact within the active space ($\sim\!10^{-15}$\,Ha).
