Multimodal decentralized learning enables collaboration across heterogeneous edge devices without sharing raw data, but differing modality sets create coupled computation and communication bottlenecks. Multimodal agents support cross-modal alignment while training more model components and sharing bandwidth across multiple modality overlays. Under bandwidth limits, these agents can become hot spots: transfers miss aggregation windows, and their updates become stale or underrepresented, degrading worst-agent accuracy. Asynchrony removes global blocking but does not by itself coordinate local cross-modal interaction with timely peer-update exchange. We present MODAIC (Modality-Decoupled Asynchrony with Interleaved Coordination), a wait-free system that coordinates local modality progress, peer-to-peer update delivery, and aggregation under heterogeneous compute and bandwidth.
