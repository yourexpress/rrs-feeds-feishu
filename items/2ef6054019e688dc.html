Personalized federated learning must decide which clients should share representations. We introduce HDC-FL (Head-Derived Clustering for Personalized Federated Learning), which reads that decision off a signal shared-encoder training already produces: the client-specific classifi- er heads. At periodic refresh rounds the server projects the cached heads into a normalized low-rank descriptor space, groups the descriptors, builds one encoder prototype per group from the cached client encoders, and returns to each client a soft mixture of those prototypes paired with its own unchanged head. Client-specific heads are never averaged or distributed to oth- er clients; the shared-encoder trajectory is left intact; and no second optimization trajectory, additional local objective, or post-training fine-tuning stage is required.
