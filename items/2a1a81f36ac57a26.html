RATSIFT: Resolution-Adaptive Transformer for Solar Irradiance Forecasting and Temporal Downscaling

arXiv (cs.LG) · 2026-11-01 · 论文PDF

RATSIFT：分辨率自适应 Transformer 用于太阳辐照度预测与时序降尺度

Accurate forecasting and temporal downscaling of solar irradiance is critical for reliable renewable energy integration and grid management. Traditional methods often struggle to capture rapid irradiance variability. In this work, we develop a Transformer-based architecture for simultaneous forecasting and temporal downscaling. The model takes input data at arbitrary resolutions and generates outputs at any resolutions represented within the training data, leveraging both global and local contextual information. To improve training efficiency, a genetic algorithm-based optimization framework is implemented to identify suitable model hyperparameters, while reducing the computational cost of exhaustive grid search. The effectiveness of the proposed model is shown by analyzing real data in Dallas, Texas. The proposed model achieved root mean square error (RMSE) values of 28.785, 29.544, 30.157, 32.132~W/m\(^2\) for 15-, 30-, 60-minute, and irregularly spaced global input and producing forecast at 5-minutes resolution, respectively.
