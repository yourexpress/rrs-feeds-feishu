Scheduling in Cloud,Fog, and Edge Computing: A Comparative Analysis and Decision Framework for Technique Selection

云/雾/边计算调度的比较分析与技术选型决策框架

The increasing heterogeneity and dynamism of cloud, fog, and edge computing make scheduling a challenging problem involving diverse resource capabilities, workload characteristics, latency and energy constraints, and competing optimization objectives. Although numerous scheduling methods have been proposed, existing reviews often provide fragmented coverage of computing environments, scheduling granularities, or technique families, offering limited guidance on selecting an appropriate approach for specific requirements. This review presents a comparative analysis of task, job, and workflow scheduling across the cloud, fog, and edge paradigms, covering heuristic, metaheuristic, learning-based, and hybrid techniques. Studies published between 2020 and 2026 are analyzed according to scheduling granularity, computing environment, optimization objectives, constraints, performance metrics, and evaluation tools. The analysis highlights trade-offs among computational efficiency, optimization capability, adaptability, and implementation complexity across technique families.
