DT-MATCH: Defect- and Topology-Aware Chiplet Matching for Yield Optimization in Heterogeneous Integration

arXiv cs.AR · 2026-11-14 · 论文PDF

缺陷与拓扑感知的chiplet匹配，异构集成良率优化

Heterogeneous integration (HI) and advanced packaging enable chiplet-based system-on-chip (SoC) designs; however, they also introduce new yield challenges arising from manufacturing defects and complex die-to-die (D2D) interconnect constraints. In particular, lane-level defects, limited spares, and strict structural compatibility requirements impede the efficient assembly of functional systems from defective but repairable chiplets. We present DT-MATCH, a defect- and topology-aware chiplet matching framework that maximizes the number of functional SoCs under realistic assembly constraints. DT-MATCH provides two complementary approaches. The integer linear programming (ILP)-based approach is implemented using a batching strategy to approximate the exact formulation. The greedy randomized adaptive search procedure (GRASP)-based approach provides substantial acceleration, but it does not guarantee yield optimality. Both approaches use a preprocessing pipeline to reduce the problem size. Experimental results on industrial designs demonstrate that, as expected, the ILP-based approach achieves superior solution quality.
