Template Gap Reconstruction for Shell Output Simulation with Small Language Models

arXiv cs.LG · 2026-11-04 · 论文PDF

Small language models offer a compact basis for shell simulation without executing submitted commands, but full-output decoding requires them to predict recurring utility structure alongside content that varies across executions. We introduce template gap reconstruction (TGR), which supplies reusable output literals interspersed with sentinels to be interleaved with autoregressively generated gaps. A template selector chooses which template to be reconstructed, with the empty template retaining full-output decoding. We construct a template bank from 18.7 million outputs collected across heterogeneous execution environments and train a template selector and five reconstruction models initialized from various small language model checkpoints on a target environment excluded from bank construction. Evaluation demonstrates that TGR improves output edit similarity (O-ES) over full-output decoding (FOD) when the optimal template covers more than 25\% of the template, while performing similarly for lower-coverage outputs.
