HART: Hybrid Attention-RWKV Time series Foundation Model

arXiv · 2026-10-09 · 论文PDF

混合Attention-RWKV时序基础模型，定位RWKV-7长程预测的状态衰减机制

Non-autoregressive multi-step forecasting (NAF) enables time series foundation models to predict an entire future horizon in a single forward pass. Despite its strong sequence modeling capabilities, RWKV-7 exhibits long-horizon degradation in our NAF experiments. In this setup, future patches are represented as missing inputs, requiring the recurrent state to evolve across the prediction horizon without new observations. Our analysis of RWKV-7's generalized delta rule suggests a mechanism we term Placeholder Idling Decay (PID): repeated state transitions can attenuate historical information when placeholder-driven writes provide insufficient compensation. Layer-wise analysis reveals reduced state-injection norms in most layers, while transition-matrix norms remain similar between historical and prediction regions, consistent with PID. Motivated by this analysis, we propose HART, a Hybrid Attention-RWKV Time-series Foundation Model that alternates attention and RWKV blocks, combining direct access to historical tokens with recurrent temporal modeling.
