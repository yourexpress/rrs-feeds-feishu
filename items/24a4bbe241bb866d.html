Federated fine-tuning adapts large language models (LLMs) to decentralized client data, but its scalability in cross-device training is often limited by the high communication cost. Muon is an optimizer that improves optimization performance by orthogonalizing momentum for matrix-valued parameters. Existing federated Muon methods demonstrate the benefit of matrix-aware optimization in federated learning, but still require transmitting full layer-size updates and optimizer state. A natural way to reduce communication is to directly apply Muon to LoRA factors, but this changes the optimized object and weakens Muon's matrix-aware update geometry. We propose FedSubMuon, a communication-efficient federated Muon fine-tuning method that optimizes compact coefficient matrices within shared structured subspaces. This design keeps Muon on a single matrix-valued trainable object, while reducing the client upload to compact coefficient matrices. We further introduce FedSubMuon-GT, an accuracy-oriented extension that uses projected gradients to adapt tracked subspace bases toward task-relevant gradient directions.
