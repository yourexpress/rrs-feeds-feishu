Hybrid state-space and attention neural operators for nonlinear structural dynamics under environmental loading

arXiv · 2026-11-22 · 论文PDF

The computational burden of repeated nonlinear time-history analyses limits their application for structural assessment and design. We therefore propose DeepOJamba, a hybrid neural operator that combines selective state-space recurrence with full attention to predict the structural response history due to dynamic loading. Physical-parameter conditioning and predicted-state transfer between sequences enable a single trained operator to represent varying system conditions and generate response histories beyond its training sequence length. DeepOJamba is evaluated on a 15~MW monopile offshore wind turbine under wind and wave loading. The proposed operator is compared to pure attention, pure state-space, Fourier, and DeepONet operators and is found to give the lowest window error on every channel and the lowest closed-loop fatigue and ultimate-load errors of $9.4\%$ and $6.0\%$, against $14.6\%$ and $8.7\%$ for the next-best pure attention operator examined. Moreover, on sea states outside the training wave band, the error slightly increases by approximately $0.4$ percentage points.
