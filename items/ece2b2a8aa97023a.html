Where Is Rank-1 DiM Refusal Intervenable in Multimodal LM Derivatives?

arXiv cs.LG · 2026-11-15 · 论文PDF

秩1 DiM拒绝在多模态LM导数中何处可干预

Open-weight model hubs now ship not only chat checkpoints but a long tail of derivatives---fine-tunes, safety guards, vision--language models (VLMs), audio wrappers, and generative heads---yet residual-stream safety audits still often intervene at a single site, usually the text decoder $T$. We ask a localization question under one shared mean / rank-1 difference-in-means (DiM) extract$=$intervene protocol, with matched-norm random and empty-rate controls: does causal chat attack lift concentrate at $T$, or do perceptual encoders, projectors, and fusion paths ($E$/$P$/$F$) supply a comparable bypass? On the mixed-input settings we stress-test---HADES adversarial images paired with harmful text prompts, and spoken AdvBench via text-to-speech (TTS) or silence---text DiM at $T$ raises attack success rate (ASR) while the same mean DiM at $E$/$P$/$F$ does not; attacks track harmful text rather than image or audio content. Margin-rank and leverage probes on a Vision leaf show that this recipe is extremal at $T$ and near chance at $P$/$E$, which pressures a weak-extractor reading of those flat cells for the DiM family we study.
