We introduce a novel triple-overlap implementation of the pipelined preconditioned conjugate gradient (Pipe-PCG) and pipelined stabilized biconjugate gradient (Pipe-BiCGStab) solvers, methods widely used in scientific applications. By leveraging a collective communication library, our approach extends conventional computation-communication overlap to concurrently execute collective operations, irregular P2P communication, and compute kernels, enabling computation-communication-communication overlap across multiple GPUs. We evaluate the design on the MareNostrum 5 (H100) and Leonardo (A100) supercomputers, comparing against PETSc and the state-of-the-art two-stream NCCL implementation using SuiteSparse matrices and three massive instances scaled up to 512 GPUs. The three-stream design outperforms the two-stream baseline on every instance, with the margin widening from 2-6% at 4 GPUs to 18-47% at 64 GPUs. At 64 GPUs, the geometric-mean speedup over the two-stream baseline is 1.33x on MareNostrum 5 and 1.23x on Leonardo for Pipe-PCG, and reaches 1.30x for Pipe-BiCGStab.
