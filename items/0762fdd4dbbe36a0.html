State Space Attention: Learning Context-Dependent Memory Retention

arXiv (cs.LG) · 2026-11-25 · 论文PDF / 每日精选

State Space Attention：学习上下文相关的记忆保留以限制 KV 缓存

Standard attention's key-value cache grows linearly with context length. Bounding it raises the question of what a sequence model should retain for long-context reasoning. We argue this choice should be learned, and introduce State Space Attention (SSA), which learns to keep a bounded set of past representations and reads them with standard attention. A learned scorer rescores every chunk against newly arriving context and the rest of memory. A fixed-context Transformer is then the special case that always keeps the most recent tokens. We train the scorer and reader jointly, either moving gradually from a differentiable soft weighting of memories to hard retention, or with hard retention from the start. To produce signal for the scorer, we generate supervised samples during training, a technique we call recovery. We compare SSA with recurrent models, bounded-memory attention methods, and full-attention Transformers across retrieval, variable tracking, multi-hop reasoning, and language modeling.
