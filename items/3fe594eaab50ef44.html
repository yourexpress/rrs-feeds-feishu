U-Statistics-Powered LLM Reasoning

arXiv · 2026-11-24 · 论文PDF

Since its introduction by DeepSeek, group relative policy optimization (GRPO) has been widely adopted for training large reasoning models and agents, spurring a proliferation of its variants. Yet the group size, a crucial hyperparameter in these GRPO-type algorithms, remains largely chosen by trial and error. Exploiting the U-statistic structure of GRPO's policy gradient, we recast the selection of the group size as a classical statistical problem of variance ratio estimation. This problem is particularly challenging when the denominator of the ratio is small, rendering naive plug-in estimators unstable. To address this challenge, we propose a pessimistic ratio estimator that accounts for the denominator's estimation uncertainty. We characterise the statistical properties of this estimator across three sample-size regimes.
