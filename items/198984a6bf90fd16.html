FlowersBench: A FluidFlower-Inspired Dataset for Benchmarking Carbon Storage Surrogates Across Geological Structures

Machine learning surrogates are emerging as a computationally efficient tool for uncertainty quantification in geological carbon storage (GCS). Geological structure constitutes a major source of uncertainty; however, existing benchmark simulation datasets predominantly vary petrophysical and operational parameters within a fixed geological setting. Generalisation to unseen geological structures therefore remains largely unexplored. We introduce FlowersBench, a benchmark dataset of 5,000 two-dimensional, reservoir-scale CO$_2$ storage simulations across 244 structural geometries. The geometries are sampled from an in-house structural generator inspired by the FluidFlower experiments and Case B of the 11th Society of Petroleum Engineers Comparative Solution Project (SPE11B). Each simulation is run with Open Porous Media (OPM) Flow over a 50-year injection period and a 500-year post-injection monitoring period. Test geometries are withheld from training to assess generalisation to unseen structural geometries. Two additional subsets support evaluation under structural shift.
