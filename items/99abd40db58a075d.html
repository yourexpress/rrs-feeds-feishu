Replay for LLM Continual Learning with Multi-Teacher On-Policy Distillation

arXiv (cs.LG) · 2026-11-02 · 论文PDF

Multi-teacher on-policy distillation (MOPD) distills domain experts into one student but needs every expert at once. When domains arrive one at a time and earlier experts are gone, the previous-stage student can teach replayed prompts of earlier domains inside every stage. Some pipelines instead store every stage snapshot for a final multi-teacher recovery stage. On five domains with a 1.5B student and five public experts, this paper compares both with joint MOPD. It varies the replay budget and the replay signal. The signal is token-level distillation alone or with alignment of hidden states, attention and value relations. Sequential distillation without replay or recovery is 6.2 points below 208-step joint MOPD on a 14-benchmark average and 3.5 points below on the 11 benchmarks outside safety. Without snapshots, replay toward the previous student closes 3.8 of the 6.2 points when the earlier domains share the replay budget of a stage. With a fixed share of seven replay steps per earlier domain and stage, it closes 5.8 over two training runs.
