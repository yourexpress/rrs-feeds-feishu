1. Safety Overview

Today, we are releasing GPT-6.2 Astra and GPT-6.2 Sol, the most capable models we have deployed externally to date.

Both models meet the Critical threshold for Cybersecurity capabilities and the High threshold for Biological and Chemical capabilities under our Preparedness Framework [1]. GPT-6.2 Astra meets the High threshold for AI Self-Improvement capabilities, while GPT-6.2 Sol falls below it.

The most important things to know about the safety of this launch are as follows:

Our evaluations show that GPT-6.2 Astra and GPT-6.2 Sol are the most capable models we’ve released for cybersecurity tasks. Both were better than GPT-6 Astra and GPT-6.1 Sol at developing working exploits from known software vulnerabilities. In response, we tightened default restrictions on higher-risk cybersecurity assistance and require additional vetting for more permissive access.

GPT-6.2 Astra is our first externally released model to meet the High threshold for AI Self-Improvement. Before expanding GPT-6.2 Astra’s internal use in Codex, we evaluated its alignment and verified that the safeguards required for each stage were in place. We also improved isolation in our research environments, significantly expanded monitoring of frontier agents, and introduced Control Room, a central system that tracks model workloads across our research infrastructure and can stop them quickly when safety or security concerns arise.
