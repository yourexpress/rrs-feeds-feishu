Privileged On-Policy Distillation for Calibrated Alzheimer's Diagnosis With Missing Modalities

Alzheimer's disease is diagnosed from clinical, biospecimen and imaging evidence, but few patients undergo every test. Because these modalities are complementary, a missing one has many plausible values. Most existing methods leave it out or train on one estimate, so their classifier never sees its other values, and the estimate is never checked against the diagnosis. We address both gaps with on-policy distillation: the model fills in the missing modalities itself, and teachers with information available only in training grade these fills. Our method, Graded Rollout Imputation with Privileged teachers, draws several fills, one modality at a time. An imputation teacher that knows the diagnosis grades each step, bringing the outcome into the imputation as statistical practice recommends. A diagnosis teacher that sees all real modalities supplies a soft target. Trained on fills that vary, the model learns a confidence that reflects what the missing tests leave open, and it diagnoses from a single rollout.
