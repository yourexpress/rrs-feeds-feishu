Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) layers increase model capacity without proportionally increasing arithmetic, but their sparse expert computation is difficult to execute efficiently during autoregressive decode. Existing grouped and batched GEMMs are token-major: they construct expert-local token tiles and obtain parallelism from the token dimension. When few tokens reach each expert, this organization incurs tile padding and preprocessing, launches short-lived grids that underutilize memory bandwidth, and exposes quantization, activation, and reduction as separate stages. We present \textbf{MonoMoE}, a weight-major persistent megakernel for block-wise quantized MoE decode. MonoMoE places the complete decode-step token tile on the fine-grained tensor-core $N$ dimension and partitions CTAs over expert-weight tiles, eliminating expert-local token materialization and reducing padded arithmetic. A persistent grid fuses routing, top-$k$ selection, quantization, both expert projections, activation, and reduction in one launch; warp specialization and readiness flags overlap auxiliary work with the dominant expert-weight stream.
