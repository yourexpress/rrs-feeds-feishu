Drive Failure and Lifetime Simulation in the Backblaze Drive Statistics Corpus, 2013-2026

arXiv · 2026-11-20 · 论文PDF

Backblaze 硬盘故障与寿命模拟（2013-2026 语料）

Storage simulators estimate data-loss risk from assumptions about drive lifetimes and dependence between failures. We test these assumptions against Backblaze's daily records from April 2013 to June 2026: 504,624 data drives with usable power-on ages, 99 drive models, 2.0 million drive-years and 31,881 recorded failures, including removals of drives judged likely to fail. We test failure-count forecasts over nine successive years and forecast the drives already in service at each prediction date. Accounting for drive-model differences and attributes improves the score assigned to observed failure counts about 4.3 times as much as replacing a single Weibull with two competing Weibulls under the same drive-model treatment. Fleet failure risk falls early and rises at older ages, but the fixed bathtub benchmark predicts 2.4 times the observed failures. Across 169 well-observed drive-model years selected using earlier records, 95% prediction intervals cover 44% of observed counts without annual variation and 92% with fleet-wide changes and shared good or bad years for each drive model.
