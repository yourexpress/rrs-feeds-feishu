High-Performance Algorithms for Computing the V-BLAST Permutation

arXiv · 2026-11-24 · 论文PDF

We propose two novel and efficient algorithms for computing a column permutation called V-BLAST or an approximation of it. The V-BLAST column permutation is used in a QR factorization that is part of an integer/discrete least-squares solver. The overall solver is part of the receiver in advanced communication systems that transmit multiple symbols simultaneously. Two classes of efficient algorithms for computing the exact V-BLAST permutation have been proposed in the literature. The first, based on a sequence of Givens rotations, is unfortunately highly sequential. The other, based on a sequence of downdating operations on a pseudo-inverse, has a shorter critical path, but is unlikely to be numerically stable. The new algorithms that we propose are inspired by the similarity between V-BLAST and rank-revealing QR factorizations. Our first algorithm uses Householder reflections, is numerically stable, and has a short critical path. It is unable, however, to efficiently exploit cache memories.
