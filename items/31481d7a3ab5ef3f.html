Multicore Quantum Architecture for Scalable and Energy-Aware Green IoT Computing

arXiv cs.AR · 2026-11-08 · 论文PDF

多核量子架构用于绿色 IoT 计算的可扩展能耗对比

The rapid growth of the Internet of Things (IoT) is increasing the computational demand placed on edge and backend processing infrastructures, motivating scalable architectures that can reduce execution and communication overhead. This paper presents a communication-aware comparison of homogeneous multicore classical and quantum architectures for Green IoT using the Qcomm simulator. In addition, a mesh-based, homogeneous, quantum-classical multicore architecture is proposed to enable scalable, efficient execution of distributed quantum workloads. We evaluate a 4x4 mesh of 16 processing cores under regular, classical-bottleneck, and quantum-bottleneck conditions, and study the effects of traffic pattern and Light-to-Matter (LTM) port provisioning on performance. Results show that butterfly traffic yields the largest gains over uniform traffic in both execution time and qubit coherence, while performance benefits from additional LTM ports saturate beyond four ports.
