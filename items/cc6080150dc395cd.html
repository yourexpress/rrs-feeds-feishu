How Much Determinism Can Fine-Tuning Buy? Margin Regularisation Against Batch-Induced Token Flips

arXiv cs.LG · 2026-11-14 · 论文PDF

微调能买多少确定性：边界正则化对抗批次诱导token翻转

Greedy decoding at zero temperature is widely assumed to be deterministic, although the exact same prompt may yield different tokens depending on the number of other prompts sharing the batch for inference. The currently available solutions operate during inference, trading determinism for throughput. Can we train a model in such a way that fewer decoding steps would be susceptible? We characterize the perturbation empirically across six model families and three numerical precisions and observe two empirical facts about the measurement: the perturbation depends exactly on the representable precision (8.00x for bfloat16 vs float16, 8192.0x for float16 vs float32, in line with predictions based on mantissa bits) and the batch invariance happens accidentally at certain tensor shapes, allowing a practitioner to achieve zero nondeterminism merely by setting the sequence length. Then we apply hinge penalization of the model's top-1 / top-2 logit gap, scaled to twice the measured noise quantile.
