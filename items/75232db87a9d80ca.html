A Temporal Convolutional Network with Multi-Head Attention Model: A Surrogate for Parallel DEVS Semiconductor Fab Simulation

arXiv cs.LG · 2026-11-16 · 论文PDF

多注意力时序卷积网络作为并行DEVS半导体替代模型

Semiconductor factories process many kinds of wafers organized into lots of various sizes and configurations. Different kinds of machines, each with its own operations, process wafers for hours to days, and the wafers undergo many incremental fabrication steps (with re-entry) and flows spanning days to weeks across connected machines. Fast and accurate predictions of throughput ($TH$) and turnaround time ($TA$) dynamics for individual machines and their combinations are essential for factory operations. To use machine learning methods for this purpose, this paper presents a Temporal Convolutional Network with Multi-head Attention (TCNA) architecture. It uses simulated datasets from a benchmark Parallel DEVS (PDEVS) semiconductor fabrication factory model. The factory processes configurable wafer lots with uniform and sinusoidal wafer arrival profiles and may undergo preventive maintenance. The wafer lot size, arrival profile, and factory maintenance policy are used as static covariates. TCNA is applied to a selected set of simulated discrete-event throughput and turnaround time trajectories.
