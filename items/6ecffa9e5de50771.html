MediaTek's Dimensity 9600 Pro brings 2nm silicon, enhanced on-device AI, gaming, imaging and efficiency.

MediaTek Inc. has introduced the Dimensity 9600 Pro, its latest flagship smartphone SoC and the company’s first chip based on TSMC’s 2nm process. The processor combines an all-big-core CPU configuration with a dual-NPU architecture, upgraded GPU and support for LPDDR6 memory and UFS 5.0 storage.

According to MediaTek, the new process and architectural changes deliver higher compute performance while reducing power consumption. The company claims a 17% increase in single-core performance and a 15% increase in multi-core performance over the previous generation, alongside a 61% reduction in multi-core power consumption.

The CPU uses a 2+3+3 configuration comprising two C2-Ultra cores running at up to 4.55GHz, three C2-Pro cores at up to 4.35GHz and three additional C2-Pro cores at 3.1GHz.

“AI is central to the smartphone experience, and devices need greater performance, efficiency, and intelligence to keep pace with rapidly evolving user demands,” said JC Hsu, corporate senior vice president at MediaTek and general manager of the Wireless Communications Business Unit. “The Dimensity 9600 Pro meets these demands with a Native AI architecture and dual improvement in performance and power efficiency to deliver new agentic AI experiences.”

The Dimensity 9600 Pro incorporates two NPUs and MediaTek’s Agentic AI Engine for on-device AI processing. The second-generation Super Efficient NPU is designed for always-on workloads, with MediaTek claiming a 40% reduction in power consumption.

The NPU 1090 provides a 51% increase in LLM prefill performance and 55% higher token generation per watt, according to the company. It can also support on-device models with up to 30 billion parameters.
