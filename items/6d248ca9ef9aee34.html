CUFormer: Modality-Aware CT-Ultrasound Point Cloud Registration for Orthopedic Navigation

arXiv · 2026-11-06 · 论文PDF

Registering intraoperative Ultrasound (US) bone-surface point clouds to preoperative Computed Tomography (CT) models can support radiation-free orthopedic navigation. Differences in imaging physics produce distinct surface sampling patterns, complicating cross-modal feature matching. Bone strongly reflects ultrasound and casts an acoustic shadow, so US captures only the probe-facing bone surface and leaves most CT points unmatched. This partial coverage makes correspondences ambiguous on near-symmetric bones. We propose CUFormer, a modality-aware registration framework. We introduce a Modality-Aware Transformer (MAT) that encodes input modality through identity embeddings and updates features through modality-specific adapters within a shared geometric matcher. We model imaging visibility and use predicted reference-side visibility to weight coarse and fine correspondences. CUFormer achieves the lowest mean registration errors among evaluated methods on two CT-US datasets, UltraBones100k and UltraBonesHip, and the CT-RGB-D dataset SpineDepth.
