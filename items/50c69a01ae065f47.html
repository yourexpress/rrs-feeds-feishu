artist: A Python package for differentiable ray tracing and gradient-based optimization of solar tower power plant operation

arXiv cs.LG · 2026-11-16 · 论文PDF

可微光线追踪与梯度优化的太阳能塔式电站Python包artist

Solar tower power plants provide dispatchable, renewable energy by concentrating sunlight with thousands of mirrors, called heliostats, onto a central receiver. Efficient operation requires accurate modeling of the optical path from each heliostat to the receiver and compensation for manufacturing imperfections, kinematic misalignment, and material degradation that distort the flux distribution. Conventional ray tracers predict the flux from an assumed plant model but are limited to forward simulation. They cannot recover unknown plant parameters from measured flux nor directly optimize plant operation. We present artist, an open-source, FAIR4RS-compliant Python package implementing a fully differentiable digital twin of solar tower power plants. By making the optical pipeline, comprising heliostat surface representation, kinematics, blocking, and ray tracing, end to end differentiable, artist turns the digital twin from a passive forward model into an active physics-based tool for inverse modeling and data-driven optimization.
