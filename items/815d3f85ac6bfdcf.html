Simulate Before You Scale: High-Fidelity LLM Inference Planning Without GPUs

无需 GPU 的高保真 LLM 推理部署模拟规划，联合选择硬件布局/并行策略/调度/准入/路由。

Deploying large language model (LLM) inference at scale requires jointly selecting hardware layout, parallelism strategy, scheduling policy, admission control, and request routing to meet latency and throughput targets. These choices interact: a routing decision that improves cache locality can concentrate load, and an admission threshold that triggers only at saturation allows queues to grow deep enough to degrade critical-tier traffic. Evaluating candidates on GPU clusters is too expensive for iterative exploration, yet existing predictive tools either ignore batching and queueing dynamics or model only fragments of the pipeline. In this paper, we present BLIS, a deterministic, CPU-only discrete-event simulator that models the full inference serving pipeline (admission, routing, scheduling, prefill/decode execution, KV-cache management, and preemption). BLIS pairs analytical basis functions with learned scaling coefficients and overhead terms, fit once globally, to predict step latency without per-model or per-GPU profiling.
