KCensus: Synthesizing Latency-Optimal Consensus Fast Paths (Extended Version)

arXiv (cs.DC) · 2026-09-25 · 论文PDF

把 geo 复制共识快路径视为知识扩散机制并据此合成给定网络拓扑/时延目标下的最优快路径，跨 AWS 区域 KV 存储性能领先既有协议。

Strongly consistent geo-replication often relies on fast paths to reduce latency in the common case of no failures or contention. Existing fast-path schemes, however, are ad hoc and restrictive: each corresponds to a point in a broad design space shaped by network topology, workload, and latency objective, so no single scheme works best across settings. This paper looks at fast-path schemes from a new perspective, as mechanisms that spread knowledge about proposals. With this view, we identify a fundamental condition on the spread of knowledge for a fast-path scheme to work. We then introduce KCensus, a framework that turns this condition into an optimization problem, synthesizing new fast-path schemes that are optimal for a given setting. We use KCensus to build a geo-replicated key-value store and evaluate it across AWS regions. Our system outperforms competing protocols, with up to 16% lower average latency.
