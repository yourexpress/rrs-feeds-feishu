Coordinating Distributed AI Servers with Industrial Loads to Reduce Grid-Interconnection Needs

arXiv cs.DC · 2026-11-13 · 论文PDF

将分布式AI服务器与工厂工业负荷协同调度，可减少约60%额外电网互联需求

We show that co-locating distributed AI servers with industrial demand and coordinating AI workloads with production loads can substantially reduce the additional grid interconnection that AI computing requires. Grid interconnection is increasingly constraining the expansion of large AI data centers. Factories already have grid connections sized for their peak production loads, and we propose hosting AI servers during production valleys. We formulate a joint optimization that sizes CPU and GPU servers, schedules deadline-constrained tasks, and routes tasks among factories, and use it to compare three factory-site architectures with new large data centers serving the same demand. Applying this framework to 31 Chinese manufacturing industries, we find that AI demand and production loads often do not peak together. As a result, AI servers placed at factories and scheduled according to production peaks need about 60% less additional interconnection capacity than new large data centers, and 98% less when tasks are routed among a group's factories.
