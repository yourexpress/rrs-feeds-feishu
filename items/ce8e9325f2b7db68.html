Trading BRAM for LUTs: Hybrid Bit-Level Weight Storage for FPGA-Based Spiking Neural Networks

arXiv · 2026-11-24 · 论文PDF / 每日精选

FPGA SNN 混合位级权重存储：BRAM 换 LUT 的权衡

Spiking Neural Networks (SNNs) offer an attractive paradigm for energy-efficient AI inference, while their deployment on reconfigurable hardware enables application-specific acceleration without sacrificing model reprogrammability. However, realizing this potential on FPGAs is often severely constrained by the immense on-chip memory needed to store synaptic weights. Existing compression techniques reduce this storage requirement at the cost of weight precision or accuracy. This work presents a hybrid bit-level weight-storage scheme that trades BRAM capacity for FPGA logic resources while preserving the original weights unmodified. Selected weight bits are implemented using Look-Up Tables (LUTs), while the remaining bits are stored in BRAM and jointly reconstructed during inference. We integrate this approach into a spatial dataflow SNN architecture and introduce a practical bit-mapping heuristic that efficiently partitions weight bits between BRAM and LUT-based logic without modifying the weights. Experimental evaluation demonstrates more than 26% reduction in BRAM utilization with a modest increase in LUT and register usage, resulting in significant overall power savings.
