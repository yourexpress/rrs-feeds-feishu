Verifiable, Privacy-Preserving and Collaborative Multi-Objective Optimization with NSGA-II on Blockchain

arXiv cs.DC · 2026-11-08 · 论文PDF

Collaborative multi-objective optimization allows independent problem solvers to contribute their solutions to a shared problem. However, their validity cannot be reliably verified in open settings if the parties are reluctant to disclose their solutions. Therefore, this paper proposes a novel zk-NSGA-II protocol for verifiable, privacy-preserving and collaborative multi-objective optimization on blockchain. To the best of our knowledge, this is the first work that executes Non-Dominated Sorting Genetic Algorithm II (NSGA-II) on the RISC Zero Zero-Knowledge Virtual Machine (zkVM) where evolutionary generations are certified by succinct proofs that are verified on-chain. Problem solvers maintain their own independent populations as the set of solutions in the decision space and their corresponding objective vectors in the objective space. They contribute to a global on-chain Pareto front by aggregating their local off-chain Pareto fronts according to the rule of non-domination which admits a contribution if it is non-dominated with respect to the existing contributions in the global front.
