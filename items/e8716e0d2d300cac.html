MBUndo: Sound, Automatic, and Efficient Measurement-Based Uncomputation for Quantum Circuits

arXiv (cs.PL) · 2026-11-02 · 论文PDF

Quantum programs that use temporary values, known as \emph{ancilla} qubits, must explicitly \emph{uncompute} each ancilla back to zero to ensure that the program produces correct outputs. Conventional uncomputation uses unitary quantum gates, whereas leading quantum algorithms now leverage \emph{measurement-based uncomputation} (MBU), which replaces costly unitary $T$ gates with cheaper measurements and phase corrections. Current quantum programming frameworks can use measurement to uncompute specific gates such as Toffoli. No automatic transformation, however, accepts an arbitrary circuit whose ancillas are computed and uncomputed via the standard approach and converts the circuit to use MBU\@. Researchers instead apply MBU to individual circuit designs by hand, which limits algorithm development and compiler optimization. Such a transformation has not existed due to two fundamental unknowns: where MBU applies and which gates realize MBU for an arbitrary computation.
