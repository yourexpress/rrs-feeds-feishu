Imperfect Verifiers, Exact Targets: Inference-Time Scaling for Continuous Generation

Verifier-guided inference-time scaling uses additional computation to score intermediate states and steer generation toward better outputs. However, imperfect intermediate verifiers can distort sampling, and repeated refinement alone does not ensure recovery of the desired reward-tilted distribution. We introduce Continuous-State Verifier-Guided Backtracking (CS-VGB), an inference-time scaling method for sampling from reward-tilted distributions with continuous generative models through repeated denoising and renoising. With correctly paired denoising and renoising transitions, the stationary distribution of CS-VGB at the clean checkpoint equals the desired reward-tilted target, even when intermediate verifiers are inaccurate. Under stated regularity conditions, CS-VGB converges exponentially to the target despite verifier error, and the expected number of updates needed to reach a clean sample grows only linearly with the number of checkpoints.
