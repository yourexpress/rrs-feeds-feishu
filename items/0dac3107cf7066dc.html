In this paper, we focus on investigating the resource allocation in an integrated sensing and communication (ISAC) system assisted by a simultaneously transmitting and reflecting reconfigurable intelligent surface (STAR-RIS). Due to the coupled non-convex nature of the optimization problem, solving the above joint resource optimization problem while guaranteeing superior sensing performance is challenging, especially in dynamic environment. To conquer such challenge, prior iterative optimization algorithms have been presented. However, practical implementations of these prior algorithms face notable computational challenges, e.g., the reliance on accurate global channel information and the assumption of ideal phase shifts. In this context, we propose a model-driven deep learning framework for jointly optimizing the transmit beamforming and waveform in ISAC networks with low-overhead. In addition, we adopt the Cramer-Rao lower bound (CRLB) as the sensing performance metric, and the multi-user interference (MUI) energy as the communication performance metric for performance evaluation.
