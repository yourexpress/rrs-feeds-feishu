CLAIR: Consistent LLM Adaptation via Index-free Reprogramming

arXiv · 2026-11-21 · 论文PDF

Time-series forecasting with pretrained large language models (LLMs) uses contextualized patch representations, raising the question of how to align them. Matching patches at the same temporal position is natural, but self-attention allows each patch state to incorporate information from elsewhere in the input window, suggesting that useful correspondences can extend across positions. This flexibility, however, does not necessarily translate into consistent forecasts: similar patch states may play different roles when used in their original temporal order. To address both issues, we propose CLAIR (Consistent LLM Adaptation via Index-free Reprogramming), a teacher-guided framework that combines patch-level transport alignment (PTA) and forecast-level consistency (FC). A clean-view exponential moving average (EMA) teacher guides a mildly perturbed student, with PTA establishing soft, representation-dependent correspondences and FC encouraging agreement at corresponding future steps. The forecasting head retains the original patch order.
