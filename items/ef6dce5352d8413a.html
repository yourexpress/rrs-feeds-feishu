Not All Misalignment Is Alike: A Decomposed Benchmark for Emergent Misalignment

arXiv cs.LG · 2026-11-05 · 论文PDF

Emergent misalignment (EM), in which fine-tuning on a narrow task induces misaligned behaviour on unrelated prompts, has been demonstrated across a wide range of datasets, model families, and training setups. Yet, most studies continue to rely on the evaluation approach introduced in the original EM paper, producing rates that are sensitive to the small set of evaluation questions used, the choice of judge, and the thresholds applied. These evaluations can also conflate misalignment with capability loss and offer little insight into the specific ways misalignment manifests. Differences in datasets, models, and fine-tuning configurations further make findings difficult to compare across studies. We introduce EMERGE, a decomposed benchmark that tracks changes in eight distinct misaligned behaviours from base to fine-tuned models. It evaluates these behaviours on out-of-domain prompts against matched benign controls and tracks capability, coherence, and refusal to account for potential confounders.
