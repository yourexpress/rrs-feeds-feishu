Autoregressive generation over adaptive 3D octrees (Uni-3DAR) offers an intrinsic $O(N \log N)$ sparsity advantage over dense $O(N^2)$ equivariant diffusion and flow models on large drug-like molecules, yet lags in geometric equilibrium, closed-shell valence stability, and property fidelity due to Morton-seam coordinate jumps, leaf-heavy compute with root-count trapping, and target-shortcut collapse in internal critics. We introduce MolGPAO (Molecular Generative Pre-trained Autoregressive Octree), a standalone 3D octree architecture and single-output speculative tree-decoding engine using zero external surrogates, zero reinforcement learning, and zero inference-time MLIP relaxation.
