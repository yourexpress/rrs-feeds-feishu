EdgeAI systems are increasingly employing computer vision applications to enable intelligent, on-device decision-making in real-time. However, these deployments face highly dynamic operational conditions, with fluctuating constraints on latency, power availability, and memory resources. Deep Neural Networks (DNN), which follow fixed computational execution flows, lack the flexibility to adapt to such variability, resulting in inefficient and suboptimal performance in edge scenarios. This underscores the need for architectures that are not only efficient but also dynamically scalable at runtime. In this paper, we propose Elastoformer: A framework that transforms conventional neural networks (NN) into Elastic NN capable of real-time elastic inference. Unlike the conventional bag-of-models approach, which requires maintaining multiple independent models for different operating conditions, Elastoformer offers a single, modular solution that dynamically switches between multiple modes of operation at runtime, adapting efficiently to the changing computational budgets of edge devices without the overhead of managing separate models.
