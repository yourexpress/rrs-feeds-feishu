Adapting Qwen3 to Tajik from 0.6B to 8B: Lower Text Loss Did Not Mean Better Understanding

arXiv · 2026-11-06 · 论文PDF

Small LoRA adapters trained on Tajik news and books lowered Qwen3's Tajik text loss at every size we tried, from 0.6B to 8B, by 0.60 to 1.45 nats per token, but none of them improved Tajik reading comprehension where it can be measured. On Belebele the 1.7B and 8B adapters left accuracy unchanged and the 4B adapter lowered it by 3.7 points; at 0.6B the score rose by 4.2 points but sits near chance and coincides with a change in which answer letter the model prefers, so we do not read it as understanding. At 8B, continued pretraining on about 375 million tokens of raw Tajik text was followed by a Belebele gain of 9.9 points. After a further supervised fine-tuning stage, our main model, e3, is 11.4 points above Qwen3-8B on Belebele; its English MMLU changed by -1.2 points, and a loss larger than 3.3 points is excluded. The same heavy recipe applied to Qwen3-0.6B-Base brought its text loss close to that of the 8B model after books-only pretraining, while its multiple-choice answers stayed near chance and its English MMLU fell by 7.6 points.
