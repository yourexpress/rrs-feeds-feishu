Bilateral Log-Linear Attention

arXiv cs.LG · 2026-11-13 · 论文PDF

双边对数线性注意力

Sequence models represent past context at different resolutions, using token-level keys and values, fixed-size recurrent states, or hierarchies of compressed summaries. Full attention supports strong associative recall but requires quadratic work during training and prefill and a linearly growing key-value cache during inference. Linear attention and state-space models reduce these costs through fixed-size recurrent memory, but this compression can limit recall. Log-linear attention offers a middle ground by maintaining logarithmically many state matrices. However, its hierarchy favors finer resolution near the current query while compressing earlier context into coarser summaries. To complement this allocation of resolution, we introduce bilateral log-linear attention, which pairs the existing hierarchy with a second hierarchy anchored at the sequence origin. By combining their reads, the two branches provide finer access to both early and recent context while remaining causal.
