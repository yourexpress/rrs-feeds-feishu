AssameseLM-E2B: An Open-Weight Assamese Language Model for Efficient Consumer-Grade Deployment

arXiv · 2026-11-05 · 论文PDF

While Large Language Models (LLMs) excel in high-resource languages, their performance often degrades in low-resource languages due to tokenization inefficiencies, generative instability, and linguistic interference. This paper presents AssameseLM-E2B and its instruction-tuned variant, AssameseLM-E2B-it, a 2-billion-parameter model adapted specifically for low resource Assamese language. Built upon the Gemma-4 architecture, the model incorporates an expanded byte-level BPE vocabulary to reduce tokenization overhead by nearly 500% compared with multilingual 2B--3B baselines. We employ a parameter-efficient Continual Pre-Training (CPT) pipeline with Hybrid LoRA, followed by Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT) on a balanced, multi-domain dataset to enhance instruction-following and Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) capabilities. Evaluation demonstrates a Bits-per-Byte (BPB) score of 0.7488 on FLORES-200, alongside an overall RAG score of 96.7% and 100% refusal accuracy on adversarial unanswerable prompts.
