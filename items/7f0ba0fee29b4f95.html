Explaining Cross-Label Decision Structure in Multi-Output Tree Ensembles

arXiv cs.LG · 2026-11-16 · 论文PDF

多输出树集成跨标签决策结构的解释

A multi-output tree ensemble predicts several binary labels through decision paths that it largely shares across those labels, yet the attribution methods commonly used to explain such models treat each output in isolation. We propose multi-sink Decision Predicate Graphs (ms-DPGs), which extend decision predicate graphs with one sink per label: each leaf contributes to the sink of every label its in-bag positive count for that label. On this representation we define Shared Predicate Flow (SPF), the share of a label's decision mass that passes through conditions active for both labels of a pair, and a contracted view of the graph in which label-specific lifts indicate whether a shared condition raises the probability of both labels or of one at the expense of the other. Controlled experiments show that SPF captures shared decision conditions rather than mere feature overlap.
