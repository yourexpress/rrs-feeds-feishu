Multi-Order Passive Macromodeling from Layouts via Nested Port-Hamiltonian Learning

arXiv · 2026-11-21 · 论文PDF

Compact, passive state-space macromodels of interconnects and power-delivery networks are essential for stable transient signal- and power-integrity simulations. However, conventional flows require costly per-layout full-wave electromagnetic (EM) simulations, while both traditional fitting and prior learning surrogates rely on post-hoc fitting or passivity perturbation that degrades accuracy without guaranteeing convergence. In this work, we propose an end-to-end macromodeling framework that learns directly in the passive space, mapping physical layout graphs to multi-order port-Hamiltonian (pH) macromodels. Specifically, our network encodes each layout graph into unconstrained matrix blocks and assembles them via a complete, prefix-nested pH parameterization that guarantees continuous-frequency passivity and stability by construction, yielding valid $k$-state macromodels via direct state slicing in one forward pass. To train all orders without reference realizations, we supervise the network directly on S-parameters through a progressive order-growth curriculum.
