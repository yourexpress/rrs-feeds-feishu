Conditional Diffusion Models for Amortized Bayesian System Identification

arXiv cs.LG · 2026-11-16 · 论文PDF

摊销贝叶斯系统辨识的条件扩散模型

Generative diffusion models rapidly solve Bayesian inverse problems, such as image restoration and inpainting, with a single trained model and without retraining for each new observation or task. Bayesian system identification, which typically involves inferring the hidden states and parameters of a dynamical system from measurements, poses a closely related inverse problem. Yet it is often addressed with computationally heavy Monte Carlo methods that must be rerun for every new measurement record. Moreover, these methods are restricted to a single inference task, and their cost grows with the state dimension and the record length. To alleviate these restrictions, we interpret Bayesian system identification in hiddem Markov models (HMMs) as a general Bayesian inverse problem, taking advantage of conditional diffusion models. Specifically, a transformer-based diffusion model is trained once on short segments of simulated trajectories to approximate the joint posterior of states and parameters.
