Keeping Jerk in Check: Jointly Trained Perception-Gated Experts for Smooth Terrain Transitions

arXiv · 2026-11-06 · 论文PDF

Perceptive legged locomotion across discontinuous terrain requires shared locomotion knowledge, task-specific maneuvers, and smooth transitions between them. Existing approaches train specialized policies independently and either switch among them with a hierarchical selector or distill them into a monolithic policy. Both fix the constituent behaviors before they are composed, and both are assessed through aggregate course outcomes that leave behavior at terrain boundaries uncharacterized. We instead propose CTS-MoE, which formulates multi-terrain locomotion as multi-task reinforcement learning and jointly trains a perception-gated dense mixture-of-experts actor with task-specific critics: soft routing composes expert knowledge into continuously varying behavior, while separated critics isolate heterogeneous objectives so that specialization survives joint optimization. A concurrent teacher-student formulation transfers this composition from privileged terrain information to onboard depth, so that at deployment routing is inferred from perception alone, with no terrain label, selector, or distillation stage.
