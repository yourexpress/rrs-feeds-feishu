Resource-efficient artificial intelligence has become increasingly important for sustainable agriculture, where timely disease forecasting must be achieved under computational and energy constraints. Early estimation of rice blast can enhance food security by optimizing crop management practices while reducing excessive pesticide use. However, existing frameworks often suffer from high computational overhead and limited capability to model the complex spectral-temporal dependencies present in heterogeneous meteorological and remote sensing data. To address these challenges, this paper proposes a lightweight multimodal fusion framework comprising an attentive positional encoding-based Transformer and a convolutional neural network (CNN)-embedded gated recurrent unit (GRU) for efficient spectral-temporal representation learning. The attention-enhanced positional encoding assigns adaptive importance to sequential meteorological tokens, enabling effective modelling of temporal characteristics.
