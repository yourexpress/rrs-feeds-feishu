Denoising Is Stein: Moment Access and a Resource Hierarchy for Learning Hidden Structure in Diffusion Models

arXiv (cs.LG) · 2026-11-02 · 论文PDF

去噪即 Stein：扩散模型隐结构学习的矩访问与资源层级

We study score learning from denoising pairs when the data contain a hidden non-Gaussian component. If covariance reveals the component, its subspace can be recovered directly. If covariance is isotropic, the first distinguishing moment has order $k^\ast$, and the learner must extract that moment from clean points and their noisy views. Correlations in the denoising loss are Stein discrepancies against a baseline; for polynomial fields they determine exactly the corresponding moment differences. Averaging fresh noisy views conditional on a clean point gives an exact moment query on the clean distribution. In the covariance-matched regime, orientation-independent dictionaries satisfying an $L^4/L^2$ condition need order $d^{k^\ast-1}$ features, and the stated dot-product kernel ridge models need the same power in samples. Small-step online SGD on one unit has this exponent up to logarithms. A two-stage learner uses the curvature of a smoothed denoising loss to recover the hidden direction, then sharpens it and fits a scalar readout.
