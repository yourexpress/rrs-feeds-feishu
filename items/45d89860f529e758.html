Exact Shortest Paths and Maximum Flow in Planar Networks in the CONGEST Model

arXiv (cs.DC) · 2026-11-01 · 论文PDF

In the CONGEST model, exact single-source shortest paths (SSSP) with nonnegative weights and exact maximum $st$-flow in directed planar networks have both been solved in $\tilde O(D^2)$ rounds, where $D$ is the hop diameter, while the lower bound is $Ω(D)$. Given the approximate distance labels of a recent preprint (arXiv:2606.31192), which we use as a black box, we solve exact directed SSSP in $\tilde O(D)$ rounds. It rests on a lemma that answers queries from a virtual source with the labels, and on a corrected discretization of the reduction of exact to approximate distances (arXiv:2210.16351). All further bounds of the form $\tilde O(D)$ and $D\cdot n^{o(1)}$ use these labels. Through the reduction of arXiv:2303.00811, SSSP with negative weights, or the detection of a negative cycle, takes $D\cdot n^{o(1)}$ rounds with high probability. We weight the face-disjoint graph of arXiv:2411.11718 so that its distances are dual distances, which turns distance problems in the dual into problems in a planar network of hop diameter $O(D)$.
