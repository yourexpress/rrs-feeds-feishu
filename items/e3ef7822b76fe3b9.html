One-Shot Hierarchical Federated Clustering for Distributed Non-IID Biomedical Data Analysis

arXiv cs.LG · 2026-11-08 · 论文PDF

In multi-institutional biomedical data analysis, comprehensive disease profiles are often fragmented across different data silos. Furthermore, local data distributions typically exhibit varying levels of granularity or even complex nested structures. Although Hierarchical Clustering (HC) is a powerful tool for exploring such data topologies, its cumbersome recursive process incurs prohibitive computational overhead and renders it highly susceptible to privacy leakage. This paper, therefore, introduces an efficient one-shot hierarchical FC framework that performs client-end (institutions) distribution exploration and server-end distribution aggregation through one-way prototype-level communication from clients to the server. Although the designed one-shot prototype transmission guarantees minimal computational overhead and mitigates privacy risks, it inevitably incurs information loss. To counteract this, a multi-granular competitive learning mechanism is proposed on the server to fuse the clusterlets, effectively maximizing the retention and mining of global distribution topologies under limited, one-way communication constraints.
