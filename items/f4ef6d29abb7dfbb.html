Structure-Preserving Neural Approximation of Convex, Semiconvex, and Quasiconvex Functions

arXiv · 2026-11-18 · 论文PDF

凸/半凸/拟凸函数的结构保持神经逼近

Input convex neural networks provide a natural way to enforce convexity globally through architectural constraints. We study how this structure-preserving approximation framework extends to semiconvex, quasiconvex, and convex transformable functions. For $C^{1,1}$ convex functions on compact convex domains, we establish the sharp $W^{1,p}$ minimax rate $m^{-1/d}$ under max-affine approximation with $m$ affine pieces, and show that every max-affine function admits an exact bounded-width ICNN representation with complexity linear in $m$. The same $W^{1,p}$ approximation rate extends to semiconvex functions through quadratic lifting and to convex transformable functions through monotone composition. For general Lipschitz quasiconvex functions, we develop a quantitative finite-probe approximation theory based on monotone responses to affine projections and obtain a structure-preserving IqCNN with an $L^\infty$ approximation guarantee. We further show that these approximation results extend to ReLU-transferable smooth activations, including Softplus and ELU.
