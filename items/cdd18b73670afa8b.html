PAMS: Preconditioned Activation-Metric Subspace for Zeroth-Order LLM Fine-tuning

arXiv · 2026-11-21 · 论文PDF

Zeroth-order (ZO) optimization provides a computationally efficient approach to fine-tuning large language models (LLMs), but conventional methods typically rely on isotropic perturbations that ignore the anisotropic structure of neural-network parameter spaces. We introduce Preconditioned Activation-Metric Subspace (PAMS), a ZO optimization framework that uses forward-pass activation statistics to construct structured, layer-wise perturbation geometries without computing gradients or forming dense curvature matrices. PAMS tracks a low-rank approximation of the dominant activation subspace and its associated spectrum online, and uses the spectrum to softly reweight perturbation variance while preserving exploration in directions outside the retained subspace. A trace normalization separates the perturbation geometry from its overall scale, enabling consistent perturbation magnitudes across layers. We show theoretically that the resulting perturbation covariance induces right-side preconditioning of the gradient of the corresponding Gaussian-smoothed objective.
