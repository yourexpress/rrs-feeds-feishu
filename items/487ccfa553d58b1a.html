ParaBlock++: Communication-Computation Parallel Blockwise Hierarchical Federated Learning

arXiv (cs.LG) · 2026-10-31 · 论文PDF

In the emerging 6G paradigm, where AI-driven services operate over decentralized and resource-constrained infrastructures, the convergence of distributed learning algorithms remains a significant challenge. This paper introduces $\mathrm{ParaBlock}$++, a low Earth orbit (LEO) satellite-assisted hierarchical federated learning (HFL) framework, enabling resource-limited terrestrial clients to collaboratively engage in federated learning (FL) via ground-satellite links (GSLs) supported by LEO satellite megaconstellations. Within this architecture, LEO satellites serve multiple roles: as edge servers aggregating and broadcasting models, as clients accelerating training, and as high-throughput relays managing inter-satellite routing through inter-satellite links (ISLs). Departing from conventional synchronous Jacobian-type updates, $\mathrm{ParaBlock}$++ employs asynchronous BCD-type updates to enhance convergence speed. We provide theoretical convergence guarantees for convex, strongly convex, and non-convex objectives, and formulate optimization problems to determine optimal cluster resource allocation and model partitioning for minimizing global training latency.
