Stochastic Projections and Rank-Aware Learning Rates for Federated LoRA

arXiv cs.LG · 2026-11-09 · 论文PDF

Fine-tuning large-scale foundation models across resource-constrained, distributed clients presents significant computational and communication challenges. While merging Low-Rank Adaptation (LoRA) with Federated Learning (FL) enables rank-based flexibility, the interdependence of the adaptation matrices ($\mathbf{A}$ and $\mathbf{B}$) often complicates optimization. In this work, we theoretically demonstrate that LoRA fine-tuning with Gaussian initialization and a frozen $\mathbf{A}$ matrix acts as a stochastic approximation of full fine-tuning, where batch gradients are subject to a random, rank-dependent perturbation. Motivated by this insight, we propose freezing and periodically resampling $\mathbf{A}$ matrices at the client level. This strategy decouples the $\mathbf{A}$ and $\mathbf{B}$ gradients, diminishes LoRA low-rank constraints and reduces both computational and uplink communication overheads by 50\%, allowing for the reallocation of resources toward higher adaptation ranks. Furthermore, we show that in this frozen-$\mathbf{A}$ regime, the adaptation rank $r$ directly governs the signal-to-noise ratio (SNR) of the perturbed gradients.
