Scaling large language model-assisted line-of-therapy adjudication with human-in-the-loop evidence-calibrated selection

arXiv cs.LG · 2026-11-15 · 论文PDF

人机协同证据校准的LLM辅助治疗方案判定规模化

Large language models (LLMs) have enabled the ability to scale clinical adjudication tasks traditionally dependent on exhaustive manual review. Line-of-therapy (LoT) adjudication, a major bottleneck in oncology cohort construction, is a prime example. Line boundaries must be inferred from longitudinal treatment histories according to nuanced, study-specific definitions, at every regimen transition for every patient. LLMs can apply this adjudication at scale, but their reliability varies across patients. Treatment histories differ in complexity, and ambiguous or atypical patterns depend on expert clinical judgment. Reliable automation thus requires verified support for model assignments and the ability to identify cases that warrant manual review. Here, we present evidence-calibrated selection (ECS), a human-in-the-loop framework for scaling LLM-assisted LoT adjudication. ECS derives confidence from observed clinician adjudications, prioritizes patients with the least-supported assignments for follow-up review, and returns completed adjudications to the model as precedent.
