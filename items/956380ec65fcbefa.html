Graph Contrastive Learning (GCL) has achieved remarkable success in self-supervised representation learning by maximizing agreement between augmented views of a graph. Although augmented view generation is the central step in GCL, existing models rely on stochastic augmentation of the input graph, such as edge perturbation and node masking, which lack structural awareness and frequently destroy critical topology or introduce semantically inconsistent views. While recent adaptive methods improve upon random perturbations, they primarily operate on localized node or edge neighborhoods and fail to capture higher-order, macro-level structural organization crucial for graph classification. To address this limitation, we propose Structure-aware Compression for Graph Contrastive Learning (SAC-GCL), a novel framework that employs structural decomposition and graph compression as a principled view generation strategy.
