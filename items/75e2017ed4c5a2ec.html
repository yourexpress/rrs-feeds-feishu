OpenAI and ServiceNow expand partnership across enterprise workflows

ServiceNow will bring OpenAI models and agents into workflows from development to security, and expand ChatGPT Work and Codex to employees.

OpenAI and ServiceNow are expanding our partnership to bring more of our models and agents into ServiceNow products and connect ServiceNow workflows with ChatGPT and Codex. Together, we’re making it easier for businesses to build applications, resolve IT requests and address security issues using AI.

The expansion builds on our partnership announced in January, bringing OpenAI into more of the work ServiceNow does for its customers and within its own business.

A new ServiceNow integration will bring business context and workflow actions directly into ChatGPT, helping teams investigate issues and take the next step in the same conversation. An IT team member will be able to find similar incidents, assess the risk of a change and update a ServiceNow record without leaving ChatGPT.

Customers connect ChatGPT to their own ServiceNow instance and choose which capabilities to make available. ServiceNow’s Action Fabric applies existing business rules and permissions, so the actions people can take reflect their organization’s configuration and access.

ServiceNow is also previewing interactive experiences inside ChatGPT. In one example, an on-call engineer will be able to review linked monitoring alerts, recent changes and affected services, then assign the right team and work toward a fix within ChatGPT.

We also plan to develop new capabilities across three areas:

Application development: A planned Codex integration in ServiceNow’s AI Workflow Factory will help developers build applications using ServiceNow’s business context and platform capabilities. OpenAI models will also power Build Agent and Autonomous Engineer to support application development and modernization.
