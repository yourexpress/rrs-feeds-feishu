Mixture of Wide Experts: Sparse Routing for Interpretable Native Coordinates

arXiv (cs.LG) · 2026-08-26 · 论文PDF

宽专家混合：稀疏路由到固定分散输出坐标的可解释原生坐标，MoWE 稀疏前馈层

Language models often spread a concept across many internal dimensions, making their features difficult to interpret. Sparse autoencoders (SAEs) recover clearer features after training. We ask whether models can instead learn interpretable output coordinates directly. We introduce Mixture of Wide Experts (MoWE), a sparse feed-forward layer in which selected experts read the full representation but write to fixed, disjoint output coordinates. On a controlled task with known hidden causes, this design preserves predictive performance while localizing causes more clearly than dense and standard mixture-of-experts (MoE) models. At approximately matched compute, we train two 19-layer runs of each architecture for 10 billion tokens. MoWE achieves evaluation cross-entropy 2.417 versus 2.452 for dense, while standard MoE reaches 2.22. We measure native-coordinate readability by asking a language-model judge to describe each coordinate from passages where it is strongly active and test that description on new passages.
