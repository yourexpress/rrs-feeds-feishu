Neural Conditional Gaussian Model: Efficient Data Assimilation with Sequential Uncertainty Quantification for Nonlinear Dynamics

Data assimilation (DA) in partially observed nonlinear systems requires repeated estimation of both hidden states and their uncertainty. Conventional nonlinear filtering can be computationally expensive and often relies on an explicit dynamical model. In this paper, we develop the Neural Conditional Gaussian Model (Neural CGM), a data-driven dynamical model that maps unobserved physical states into a learned latent space, where the dynamics of the latent states follow a conditional Gaussian structure. This structure allows closed-form DA updates in the latent space, while nonlinear encoder-decoder mappings retain the flexibility needed to represent complex dynamics in physical space. A key feature of Neural CGM is its treatment of uncertainty in DA. The latent posterior mean and covariance are mapped to the physical variables through a Gaussian decoder that combines local moment propagation with learned nonlinear corrections. The resulting physical-space posterior statistics depend on the sequentially updated latent distribution rather than on a separate post-processing estimate.
