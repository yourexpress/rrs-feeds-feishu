MOSAIC: Memory-Organized Search with Anti-Imitation Crossover for Training-Free Microarchitecture Optimization

arXiv · 2026-11-24 · 论文PDF / 每日精选

免训练微架构优化：记忆组织搜索+反模仿交叉 MOSAIC

Large language models can generate cache-replacement policies. The central challenge is to retain performance on workloads excluded from the search. In our adaptations of published search methods, generated code resembles established policies, with cosine similarities of 0.78-0.84. The wider comparison also shows held-out harmonic-mean instructions-per-cycle (IPC) losses of 1.0-2.4%. We present MOSAIC, a search controller that improves policies without updating language-model weights. It stores concise lessons from previous evaluations rather than repeatedly copying high-scoring code. When improvement stops, it selects two high-scoring policies whose code embeddings differ and asks the model to combine their mechanisms. Novelty prompts and model rotation support this search; controlled memory edits test how the stored lessons affect later proposals. We evaluate cache replacement using intervention-level cross-validation and a wider comparison on ten memory-intensive SPEC2006 workloads. MOSAIC reaches a 51.1% peak hit rate on the primary search workload and a 47.9% held-out mean in the intervention study.
