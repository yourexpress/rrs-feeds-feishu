Spatio-temporal foundation models (STFMs) require large-scale training corpora, yet synchronized observations and spatial metadata are costly to acquire and curate, limiting the availability of suitable data. By contrast, time-series data are abundant and easier to collect. Since observations at each location form a time series, this motivates a natural question: can abundant time-series data enhance STFMs when spatio-temporal observations are scarce? To bridge this gap, we propose T2ST, a data-efficient training framework that augments limited spatio-temporal observations with selected auxiliary time series and transfers knowledge from a frozen time-series foundation model. Our key insight is that temporal pattern learning can benefit from abundant external time series, while spatial dependency learning remains grounded in real spatio-temporal observations. Guided by this insight, T2ST retrieves matched sequences to reinforce sparsely observed temporal patterns and selects reliable complementary sequences to broaden temporal coverage.
