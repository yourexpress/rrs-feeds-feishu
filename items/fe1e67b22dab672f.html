Nonreciprocal Spherical Attention and Retrieval: Sharp Coarsening Clocks and Certified Score Correction

arXiv (cs.LG) · 2026-11-01 · 论文PDF

We develop nonreciprocal spherical attention as a mathematical framework for coarsening and compressed nearest-neighbor retrieval. For two shrinking acute blocks whose centers have zero skew pairing and whose total mass ratio is subexponential in inverse temperature, we prove $\lim_{β\to\infty}β^{-1}\log T_β=1-\cosφ$, without restricting particle-count growth. An explicit three-body flow has two rigorously separated metastable exponents. For finite compression, mass centering yields a sharp $O(r^2)$ attention-output error bound at fixed temperature, with an explicit spherical-normalization correction. We formulate skew query-side score calibration as a strictly convex moment problem and derive a unique normal equation, residual-based optimization certificates, and bounded low-rank projections. A norm-aware margin objective calibrates native squared-distance rankings. Experiments use semantic retrieval data, one million visual descriptors with three codebook seeds, and a ten-million-vector FAISS IVF-PQ index with recomputed exact cosine ground truth.
