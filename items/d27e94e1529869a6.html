Causal Discovery Bench: Can LLMs Autonomously Conduct Effective Experiments?

arXiv cs.LG · 2026-10-13 · 论文PDF

As large language model (LLM) agents get actively adopted for autonomous research, they must not only reason over existing evidence, but also design informative experiments, allocate resources, analyze sequential observations, and decide when to stop and make conclusions. Yet these capabilities remain poorly measured. We introduce Causal-Discovery-Bench (this https URL), a verifiable sandbox with configurable structural equation models and resource constraints to evaluate sequential experimental design and causal discovery without domain-semantic cues. Across 3,802 episodes with 13 models from 5 vendors, we analyze agent behavior along 40 metrics in seven families. Agents' performance exhibits a four-level difficulty hierarchy spanning single-experiment design, sequential exploration of causal relationships, resource-aware experimentation balancing cost and statistical power, and autonomous resolution of conflicting evidence.
