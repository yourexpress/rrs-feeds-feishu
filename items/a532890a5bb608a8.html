Numerical Integration on Bounded Domains via Learned Reflected Diffusions

arXiv(cs.LG) · 2026-10-29 · 论文PDF

We study high-dimensional integration on bounded convex domains.

We study high-dimensional integration on bounded convex domains. Given a non-negative integrand $r$, the goal is to estimate $Z=\int_{D}r(x)\,\mathrm dx$ on $D\subset\mathbb R^d$. Standard Quasi-Monte Carlo does not adapt its evaluation points to the integrand, while effective importance sampling requires a proposal that captures its concentration. Learned diffusion samplers can supply such a proposal, but existing methods are formulated on $\mathbb R^d$ and do not respect the domain constraint $x\in D$. We instead build the proposal from reflected dynamics that stay in $D$ by construction. A reflected diffusion progressively transforms the target distribution toward the uniform distribution on $D$, and a neural score model is trained to reverse this process. A change-of-measure formula for reflected diffusions yields a training objective without additional boundary terms and an unbiased importance-sampling estimator of $Z$.
