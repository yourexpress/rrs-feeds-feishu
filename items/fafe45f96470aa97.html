Multimodal Fusion with Pre-trained Models for Psychotherapy Outcome Prediction from Emotional Dynamics

arXiv cs.LG · 2026-10-13 · 论文PDF

预训练多模态融合用于心理治疗结果预测

In-session emotional dynamics are associated with psychotherapy outcomes but difficult to measure at scale. Pretrained multimodal models provide automated access to facial, vocal, and textual signals, yet clinical datasets remain small. Our research question is how pretrained multimodal representations can best support psychotherapy outcome prediction under data scarcity. Three studies use 106 sessions from 88 patients and nine session-level outcome scales (HSCL-11, EPO-1, POMS). Study 1 asks whether deep learning outperforms classical machine learning: a two-stage deep pipeline, first predicting window-level valence/arousal and then session outcomes, transferred poorly (best single configuration, $r=.078$). Study 2 asks which representation and preprocessing choices work best: statistical pooling of frozen pretrained embeddings with regularized classical models achieved average $r=.406$ across nine scales under per-scale configuration selection, up to $r=.490$ for symptom distress, with complementary modalities and no significant benefit from manual affect annotations or therapist features.
