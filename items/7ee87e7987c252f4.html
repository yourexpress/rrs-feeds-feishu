LingSante-Pulse: Unified Physiological Pretraining through Random-Feature Prediction and Cross-Modal Alignment

arXiv · 2026-11-20 · 论文PDF

LingSante-Pulse：统一生理信号预训练框架

Clinical monitoring and wearable devices capture complementary physiological signals, but their partially overlapping modality coverage makes within-signal information and cross-modal relationships difficult to exploit jointly. We introduce LingSante-Pulse, a unified, general-purpose and flexible physiological representation framework that learns from these heterogeneous recordings. A shared, signal-conditioned encoder processes each physical channel independently and supports different numbers and combinations of signals for multimodal assessment and single-sensor inference. Pairwise cross-modal alignment, structured random-feature prediction (RFP) and masked waveform reconstruction jointly learn physiological relationships and preserve informative within-signal structure. Across 38 general physiological task configurations spanning PPG, ECG, EEG and EMG, the frozen encoder achieves the best dataset-balanced mean rank in each domain. In sleep assessment, multimodal staging on the HMC dataset and EEG-only staging on the Sleep-EDF dataset reach Macro-F1 scores of 0.7784 and 0.7941, respectively, while single-lead ECG apnea screening achieves an AUROC of 0.9183.
