Capturing Electrical Windows Through Reconfigurable Optical Networks: Transfer-aware Scheduling of Multi-Region AI Data Centers

arXiv · 2026-10-09 · 论文PDF

可重构光网络中面向迁移感知调度的电气窗口捕获

Artificial-intelligence data centers (AIDCs) can provide spatiotemporal load flexibility by migrating training workloads in response to regional electricity prices and renewable-energy (RE) availability. However, transfer-blind scheduling may overestimate migration benefits by overlooking transfer time or prevent training from resuming within renewable-rich or low-price windows when considering transfer time. This paper proposes a transfer-aware compute-electricity-network scheduling framework that coordinates training placement, checkpoint transmission, and optical circuit switching (OCS) to improve local RE utilization and reduce grid-purchase cost. A compact compute-electricity master problem generates candidate schedules using estimated transfer delays. The framework then schedules optical circuits, verifies checkpoint delivery under shared-link capacity constraints, and revises infeasible schedules using updated delay estimates. It integrates day-ahead planning, event-triggered intraday updates, and real-time dispatch.
