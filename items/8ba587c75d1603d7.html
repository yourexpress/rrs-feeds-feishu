Multiaccuracy as an Error Benchmark for Learning with Distribution Shift

arXiv cs.LG · 2026-11-15 · 论文PDF

多准确率作为分布漂移学习误差基准

Agnostic learning has a well-developed theory of computational and statistical complexity. For learning with distribution shift, even the choice of an appropriate error benchmark remains challenging. Testable learning with distribution shift (TDS) was introduced to develop computational foundations for this setting: using labeled training data and unlabeled test data, the learner must either produce a predictor with low test error or detect distribution shift. However, standard agnostic TDS guarantees allow a constant-factor amplification of the optimum error even when the training and test distributions coincide. They therefore do not directly recover the guarantee of classical agnostic learning. We introduce shift multiaccuracy, a stronger error benchmark inspired by multiaccuracy from algorithmic fairness. This yields a TDS formulation that becomes equivalent to multiaccuracy when there is no shift. Using a recent nontrivial reduction from agnostic learning to multiaccurate prediction from prior work, we obtain the first TDS framework that formally generalizes agnostic learning through an efficient reduction preserving the training marginal.
