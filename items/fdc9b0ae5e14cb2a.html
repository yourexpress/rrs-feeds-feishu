Benchmarking the State of AI for Crop Yield Forecasting: A Global Assessment Across Modeling Paradigms

arXiv cs.LG · 2026-11-14 · 论文PDF

AI作物产量预测的全球评估基准

Accurate crop yield forecasting is essential for food security, agricultural policy, and commodity markets. Although statistical, process-based, and machine learning approaches have all been proposed, their relative strengths remain poorly understood due to the lack of large-scale, standardized comparisons. We address this gap through a comprehensive benchmark of five modeling paradigms -- statistical baselines, process-based models, conventional feature-engineered machine learning, deep sequence models, and tabular foundation models -- using the CY-Bench benchmark spanning 63 country-crop datasets (>12,000 administrative regions) across six continents. Beyond conventional pooled accuracy metrics, we introduce a multi-dimensional evaluation framework that separately quantifies overall, spatial, temporal, and anomaly prediction skill. Across multiple crops and countries, feature-engineered machine learning and tabular foundation models achieve the strongest overall predictive performance.
