Mixed-Precision Computing for Scientific Discovery: Formats, Co-Design, and Responsible Approximation

arXiv / Emmanuel Agullo 等 · 2026-09-30 · 论文PDF

科学发现中的混合精度计算：格式、协同设计与负责任近似（跨类）

Reduced and mixed precision have moved from a niche optimization to a central design axis in scientific computing and engineering, driven by energy constraints, heterogeneous accelerators, and the convergence of simulation and machine learning. This paper organizes the landscape around seven coupled themes---number formats, floating-point emulation, emerging architectures, hardware/software co-design, relation to other approximations, software design, and precision as a multilevel resource ---and, for each theme, synthesizes the state of the art, future directions, and open questions. We emphasize \emph{energy per trusted solution} as the core objective, and we frame \say{recklessly responsible} computing as a pragmatic doctrine: exploit low precision aggressively, but with systematic detection, escalation, and certification pathways.
