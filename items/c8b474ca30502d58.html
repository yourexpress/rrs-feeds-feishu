PharmAgents2: Towards Autonomous End-to-End Drug Discovery

arXiv · 2026-11-06 · 论文PDF

Drug discovery requires continuous scientific decision-making across a vast research space. We introduce PharmAgents2, an autonomous system for end-to-end drug discovery that combines knowledge-enhanced decision-making with organized large-scale exploration. Paper2Skill extracts transferable scientific decision knowledge from public sources and, together with expert-developed skills, supports research decisions. Self-Scaling Discovery expands exploration through parallel agents inheriting research context and decision-making capabilities across targets, scientific hypotheses, and discovery strategies. We introduce PharmAgents-Bench v0.1, comprising 121 questions across nine decision scenarios and three end-to-end tasks assessed with expert-defined process rubrics. PharmAgents2 outperforms evaluated baselines in most decision categories and achieves the highest process scores on all three end-to-end tasks. In three real-world studies, autonomous investigations generated findings subsequently supported by human-executed experiments.
