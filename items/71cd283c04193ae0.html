CULypso: A Clustered Federated Unlearning paradigm for Vehicular Ad-Hoc Networks

arXiv cs.LG · 2026-11-17 · 论文PDF

车载自组网的聚类联邦遗忘范式

Unlearning has emerged as an essential security and privacy-preserving solution in Machine Learning, enabling networks to support their clients' rights for data erasure, as mandated by data protection regulations. Introducing such a non-trivial task to an edge, highly mobile and time-sensitive vehicular network is an even greater, largely unexplored challenge. This article argues that a hierarchical Federated Unlearning approach could be aptly integrated into vehicular ad-hoc networks (VANETs), leveraging topology and movement to reduce communication, resource and time costs, while effectively forgetting a client's influence on the global model updates. In order to achieve this, we propose CULypso, an approximate, surgical, hierarchical unlearning algorithm tailored for clustered VANETs. Inspired by the nymph in Greek mythology, Calypso, whose name translates to 'she who conceals the knowledge', CULypso, with the capital letters referencing \textbf{C}lustered \textbf{U}n\textbf{L}earning, aims to achieve exactly what its name suggests: conceal, erase or forget in our case, knowledge it contains.
