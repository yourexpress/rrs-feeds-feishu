Federated Transfer Learning for Medical Image Classification under Mixed Skew

arXiv (cs.LG) · 2026-11-03 · 论文PDF

混合偏斜下医学图像分类的联邦迁移学习

Deep learning in medical imaging is constrained by data scarcity and by privacy regulations that make centralizing patient data across institutions impractical. Federated Learning (FL) addresses these constraints by keeping data local, but its distributed nature exposes training to strong statistical heterogeneity. Multicentric medical data is particularly affected, exhibiting a mixed skew that combines feature, label, concept, and quantity shifts across centers. Personalized FL mitigates heterogeneity by tailoring a model to each client, yet most methods assume training from scratch, which is unrealistic given the high dimensionality of medical features relative to the limited data available. We propose the Personalized Aligner Federated Pretrained (PAFP) framework, a personalized FL approach built on a pre-trained backbone. PAFP inserts client-specific Feature Alignment modules before the fine-tuned blocks to realign hidden feature distributions toward a server-aggregated target.
