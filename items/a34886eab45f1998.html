Scalable fault vulnerability analysis for ReRAM-based process-in-memory CNN accelerators

arXiv · 2026-11-19 · 论文PDF / 每日精选

ReRAM 存内处理 CNN 加速器的可扩展故障脆弱性分析

Resistive random-access memory (ReRAM)-based processing-in-memory (PIM) accelerators offer a highly efficient platform for parallel matrix-vector multiplications (VMM) in neural networks, leveraging analog computation, high density, low leakage, and non-volatility. However, their susceptibility to permanent faults, such as stuck-at-high (\textit{SaH}) and stuck-at-low (\textit{SaL}) resistance states, permanently corrupts the convolutional neural networks (CNNs) weights mapped onto ReRAM cell conductance values, thereby degrading inference accuracy and leading to system unreliability in safety-critical applications. Fault injection is a commonly used technique to assess the vulnerability of neural networks by systematically injecting faults into individual parameters and measuring the resulting accuracy degradation. Although this method is accurate, it is prohibitively time-consuming and computationally expensive, especially for large-scale CNNs. In this paper, we propose a fast, scalable, and analytical framework to quantify the fault vulnerability of convolutional neural networks deployed on ReRAM-based PIM accelerators.
