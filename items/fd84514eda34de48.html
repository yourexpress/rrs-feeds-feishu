Tight Asymptotic Bounds on the Maximum Number of Linear Regions of Piecewise-Linear Kolmogorov--Arnold networks

arXiv · 2026-11-06 · 论文PDF

Schoots et al.~\citep{schoots2025relating} (AISTATS 2025) established a generally loose upper bound on the number of linear regions of continuous piecewise-linear (CPWL) Kolmogorov--Arnold networks (KANs), without providing a lower bound. We consider a $T$-layer network with width vector $\mathbf n=(n_0,\ldots,n_T)$, input dimension $n_0=d$, output width $n_T=1$, and at most $k\geq2$ affine segments per edge. Our matching upper and lower bounds determine the tight growth order $Θ_{\mathbf n}(k^{\sum_{\ell=1}^{T}ρ_{\ell-1}})$ in $k$, where $ρ_{\ell-1}:=\min_{0\leq s\leq\ell-1}n_s$. When every hidden width is at least $d$, the tight order simplifies to $Θ_{\mathbf n}(k^{dT})$. The upper bound of \citep{schoots2025relating} is $k^{\sum_{\ell=1}^{T}n_{\ell-1}n_\ell}$. Our upper bound improves it by a factor of $Θ_{\mathbf n}\!\left(k^{\sum_{\ell=1}^{T}n_{\ell-1}n_\ell-\sum_{\ell=1}^{T}ρ_{\ell-1}} \right)$, with strict asymptotic improvement whenever a hidden layer has width greater than one.
