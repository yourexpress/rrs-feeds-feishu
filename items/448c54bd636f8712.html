Edge Accuracy Is Not Enough: Why Dynamics-Learned Structure Fails to Transfer to Inverse Problems

arXiv cs.LG · 2026-10-09 · 论文PDF

动力学预测学到的图结构迁移到逆问题系统性失效的实证与理论分析

A natural strategy for inverse problems with scarce labelled data is to transfer relational structure learned from abundant forward-simulation data. We show this strategy fails systematically, even when it satisfies the standard theoretical justification for why structure should help. We prove that approximate structure provides estimation-error benefits whenever the edge error satisfies $Δ< n^2 - kn$, reducing sample complexity from $O(n^2)$ to $O(kn+Δ)$.
