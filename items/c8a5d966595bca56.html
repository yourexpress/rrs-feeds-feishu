IP-GNO: An Inductive Physics-Informed Graph Neural Operator for Automated Distribution System State Estimation

arXiv · 2026-11-23 · 论文PDF

Distribution System State Estimation (DSSE) is a crucial tool for real-time monitoring, operation, and control of the new-age distribution systems (DSs) due to intensified voltage fluctuations and operational uncertainties introduced by volatile power injection at the nodes. Existing GNN-based approaches generally exhibit limited generalization across networks with different sizes and topologies, making zero-shot cross-grid transfer to unseen DSs a significant challenge. To overcome the aforementioned challenges, this article proposes an Inductive Physics-Informed Graph Neural Operator (IP-GNO) for scalable and transferable DSSE. The proposed framework frames complex bus voltage estimation as an operator-learning problem inspired by the Neumann series expansion of the inverse bus admittance matrix. An admittance-parameterized kernel, incorporating a self-admittance gate in the zeroth-order term and a persistent source-injection skip connection across network layers, captures the underlying electrical dependencies.
