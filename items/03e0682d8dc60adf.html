Pathology foundation models are typically benchmarked on classification performance at full label availability and on in-distribution test sets, while calibration, behavior at low label budgets, and robustness under cross-institutional shift are seldom evaluated jointly. We introduce PRISM, a tile-level benchmark that measures the reliability of pathology foundation models along four axes: model choice, label fraction, calibration, and out-of-distribution transfer. PRISM evaluates eight foundation models (CLIP, PLIP, CONCH, UNI, Virchow2, GigaPath, H-Optimus-0, Midnight) on six tile-level datasets at six label fractions (1% to 100%) with three seeds (864 linear probe runs), extended with a five-hospital Camelyon17 study of 20 directed transfer pairs. Four phenomena emerge. (i) Under full supervision, AUROC rank predicts post-temperature calibration rank consistently across datasets (Spearman rho = 0.52, I^2 = 0%), whereas at 1% labels the relationship becomes dataset-specific (rho = -0.24, I^2 = 67%).
