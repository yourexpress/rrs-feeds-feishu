ArtCrafter: Feed-Forward Generation of Articulated 3D Object with Analytic Joint Derivation

arXiv · 2026-11-06 · 论文PDF

Articulated 3D assets are essential for robotic simulation and training, yet their generation from casual images poses a key challenge: recovering part geometry and kinematics that remain mutually consistent. We present ArtCrafter, a novel feed-forward generative model for controllable generation of articulated 3D objects from a pair of images, one depicting the object in the rest state and the other in the open state. At the core of ArtCrafter is a DiT-based generative model that jointly produces part-level meshes for both articulation states in a single forward pass. Inspired by Slot Attention, its denoising process operates on a $1+2N$ slot layout comprising one shared base slot and $N$ paired rest/open slots, encouraging each pair to bind to the same kinematic part. To ensure geometrically consistent and disjoint part generation, we introduce Triplet Attention to enforce cross-state geometric consistency between paired rest/open slots anchored through the shared base slot, and a repulsion loss $\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{rep}}$ on decoded part point clouds to prevent inter-part penetration throughout denoising.
