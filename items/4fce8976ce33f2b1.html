Symbolic Synthesis with Token-Level LLM Guidance

arXiv cs.PL · 2026-11-08 · 论文PDF

We propose a synergistic combination of classical program synthesis techniques and contemporary large language models (LLMs) to solve the problem of recursive program synthesis from first-order logic specifications. Our approach leverages the strengths of both paradigms: we use LLMs as a source of statistical bias to guide the search for a correct program. At the same time we use techniques from classical synthesis to short-circuit on semantically incorrect prefixes and to avoid sampling semantically equivalent responses. The statistical bias is derived from the next-token distribution of an LLM, which is highly context-dependent. We operate with the understanding that (1) there is some class of synthesis instances that can be discharged by small models and symbolic tools, and (2) every other instance can be decomposed into these instances. Therefore, our intention is to expand the reach of small, open-weight LLMs and symbolic synthesis techniques, not to compete with proprietary frontier models on every possible instance.
