STMemoLLM: A Spatial-Temporal Memory-Augmented LLM for Robust Traffic Forecasting

arXiv · 2026-11-23 · 论文PDF

Traffic forecasting is essential for intelligent transportation systems. However, traditional deep-learning models often rely heavily on high-quality observations. Recent LLM-based approaches incorporate pretrained knowledge to improve forecasting, but they remain sensitive to input perturbations and struggle to preserve long-term patterns. To address this, we propose STMemoLLM, a spatial-temporal memory-augmented LLM for robust traffic forecasting. STMemoLLM introduces memory prototypes to compress inputs into compact summary tokens while extracting reliable dynamics from residual signals, achieving a balance between stability and variability. Specifically, a memory-enhanced tokenizer encodes prototypes and sensor characteristics into attention biases to guide adaptive compression of correlated sensors into semantic summary tokens. A memory-calibrated router further evaluates contextual deviation, compression deficiency, and prototype alignment to identify reliable residual signals, which complement the summaries while suppressing noise.
