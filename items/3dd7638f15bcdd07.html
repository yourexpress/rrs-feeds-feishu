The Critic Can Hurt: On the Safety of Critics in LLM-Guided AutoML Search

arXiv cs.LG · 2026-11-14 · 论文PDF

LLM引导AutoML搜索中critic的安全性研究

LLM critics increasingly steer iterative search in agentic AutoML, prompt optimization, and self-refinement--evaluated for whether they help, but rarely for whether they can hurt. We isolate the critic's marginal effect through a four-arm audit and characterize the loop's tolerance through controlled fault injection. We show a naive 8B critic drives search below the no-critic random-search floor on 7 of 8 tabular datasets, losing up to 0.245 AUC in the worst case; the magnitude varies strongly with dataset, frontier, and budget. When: stronger critics attenuate the loss on the evaluated suite, without establishing a universal capability threshold. Why: controlled fault injection shows that non-directional errors are nearly costless under best-so-far search, whereas directional errors consume finite search opportunities; an idealized effective-sample calculation yields a necessary condition, $q(m-1) > β$, not a deployment certificate. How: a simple observable validation gate and a learned detector can reduce realized loss, but neither is guaranteed to preserve the full-budget no-critic baseline.
