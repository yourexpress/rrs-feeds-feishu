When Can a Rewiring Control Test LLM Weight Readers? C2 and an Eligibility Protocol

arXiv · 2026-11-22 · 论文PDF

Large language models can predict the outputs of small neural networks from raw weights, but success could reflect recognition of weight-space regularities (fingerprints) rather than computation, and function-preserving controls cannot tell these apart. We propose C2, a counterfactual that permutes the columns of a network's final linear readout: every other parameter, and the readout's singular values, norms and entry multiset, are unchanged, and the function can change in an exactly recomputable way; we verify that each evaluated permutation does. C2 separates fingerprinting from computation only under three conditions, which we state as an eligibility protocol: a strategy restricted to C2-invariant summaries must already predict the target; C2 must change a property specific to the rewiring; and the reader's identity must be attested. No natural setting we tested is eligible. On tiny MLPs a frontier reader route followed the rewired function on all 24 networks, but invariant baselines were near chance and its backend could not be attested.
