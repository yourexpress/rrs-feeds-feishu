What Does Weight-Space Programming Add to Diffusion Editing? Program Construction versus Score-Guided Refinement

arXiv cs.LG · 2026-11-17 · 论文PDF

权重空间编程给扩散编辑带来什么

Weight-space programming (WSP) lets a language model construct executable weight updates for another network. We study diffusion concept editing through an interface in which programs define training contexts, preservation constraints and update directions, while a shared numerical fitter selects their coefficients. We compare generated programs with published editors, a shared-panel hand-written baseline and budget-matched search over conventional edits on 24 TIMED test edits for Stable Diffusion 1.4. The preregistered rewrite pipeline shows no advantage over the strong baseline. In an exploratory comparison, one-shot Astra programs improve fresh-scene VLM joint success, which requires subject presence together with whole-image target matching, by +10.0 points over the shared-panel baseline, while CLIP relative match shows no detectable change. These programs organise adjustable directions around protected text-state subspaces. Probing and rewriting the Astra programs then raises CLIP by +6.2 points while joint success changes by -9.7.
