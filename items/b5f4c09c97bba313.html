GhostPrefix: Synchronization-Free Universal Prefix Attacks for ASR Manipulation

arXiv (cs.LG) · 2026-10-30 · 论文PDF

We present GhostPrefix, the first synchronization-free universal adversarial prefix attack against automatic speech recognition (ASR) systems. Unlike prior audio attacks, which are typically crafted per utterance, require synchronization with the victim waveform, or target narrow objectives, GhostPrefix learns a short, reusable audio segment prepended to arbitrary live speech to exert real-time control over ASR outputs without knowledge of the future utterance or temporal alignment. A key challenge is maintaining stable adversarial effects across unknown, evolving speech contexts. GhostPrefix combines token-level losses with a novel representation-level objective for stable, input-agnostic effects. GhostPrefix supports three attack modes: (i) prompted prefix attacks for semantic steering, (ii) targeted prefix attacks for transcript hijacking, and (iii) untargeted prefix attacks for transcription degradation. To remain perceptually inconspicuous, we regularize prefixes toward benign environmental-sound distributions and improve robustness against over-the-air distortions.
