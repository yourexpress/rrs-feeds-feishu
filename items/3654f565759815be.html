STORMGUIDE-FL: Stochastic Remote Guidance for Efficient Mining of Federated Streaming Data

arXiv cs.LG · 2026-11-16 · 论文PDF

联邦流式数据高效挖掘的随机远程引导STORMGUIDE-FL

Federated Learning enables collaborative model training across distributed edge devices ("clients") while preserving data privacy. However, real-world deployments involve clients that observe continuous data streams from evolving environments while operating under strict resource limitations. Existing solutions still face two major challenges: local concept drift, where each client's data distribution shifts over time, and global client drift, arising from asynchronous engagement and imbalanced contributions across heterogeneous training participants, formally described as dual client heterogeneity. We propose STORMGUIDE-FL, a federated optimization and data-mining framework that mitigates both types of drift using a server-guided pipeline for data acquisition and local training. For data acquisition, a lightweight client-side sampling strategy incrementally selects informative and diverse instances from non-stationary, non-IID local streams under bounded memory, compute, and labeling budget.
