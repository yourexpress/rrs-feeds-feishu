CleanRoute: Reconciling DRC Compliance, Post-Layout Performance, and Runtime in Analog Routing

arXiv cs.AR · 2026-11-15 · 论文PDF

协调DRC合规、布线后性能与运行时的模拟布线CleanRoute

Automated analog routing remains a long-standing challenge because design-rule checking (DRC) compliance, post-layout performance preservation, and runtime reduction are difficult to achieve together. Existing analog routers have limited coverage of joined-metal design rules, lack control over topology-induced mismatch, and face a trade-off between DRC violations and runtime on uniform routing grids. In this paper, we introduce CleanRoute, an analog router that reconciles these three targets. Hierarchical rule enforcement assigns DRC checks and repairs to stages where the required geometry is complete, covering rules both between separate metal and where metal joins. IR-drop isolation constructs supply trees from current-mirror relationships and device currents to reduce topology-induced mismatch that geometric matching cannot eliminate and voltage drops along high-current paths. A flush-line grid adds net-specific lines aligned with device-metal edges to a coarse routing grid, providing DRC-compliant connections without uniform refinement.
