Photoplethysmography (PPG) enables low-cost and non-invasive heart rate (HR) monitoring but remains sensitive to waveform distortion, physiological variability, and measurement interference. Temporal waveform characteristics and frequency-domain periodicity provide complementary information for HR estimation; however, effectively exploring and integrating these representations within a compact model remains challenging. This paper proposes TF-IG, a parameter-efficient interaction-gated time-frequency learning framework for PPG-based HR estimation. TF-IG employs a dual-branch architecture in which a temporal branch captures pulse morphology via dilated depthwise-separable convolutions, while a frequency branch extracts cardiac periodicity information from bounded log-spectrogram representations. To combine the two domains, we introduce an interaction-gated fusion module that explicitly models feature agreement and discrepancy, while also learning feature-wise weights to balance temporal and spectral information.
