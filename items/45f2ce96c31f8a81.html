Irregular multivariate time series forecasting (IMTSF) requires capturing temporal dynamics and cross-variable dependencies from sparse, asynchronous observations. Existing methods often rely on learned temporal encoders, coupling historical representation construction with model optimisation and repeatedly processing the same observations. We propose CTLite, a lightweight forecasting framework that decouples historical summarisation from learned prediction. Specifically, a parameter-free continuous-time moment encoder summarises local levels and trends at multiple temporal scales while suppressing poorly supported slope estimates. A compact readout combines damped local extrapolation with a low-rank cross-variable correction that accounts for observation freshness, target availability, and forecast horizon. Since the historical summaries are independent of trainable parameters and forecast queries, they can be reused across optimisation steps and repeated predictions for an unchanged history without altering predictions or parameter gradients.
