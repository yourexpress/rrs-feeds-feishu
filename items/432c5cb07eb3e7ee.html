PRISM: Preference-based Reward and Uncertainty-Integrated Synthesis Minimization for RTL PPA Optimization

arXiv cs.AR · 2026-11-08 · 论文PDF

This paper introduces a budget-aware, multi-objective framework (called PRISM) for RTL optimization that improves power, performance, and area (PPA) while sharply limiting expensive commercial synthesis tool calls. Starting from a synthesizable baseline and functional testbench, the method iterates through LLM-driven rewrite generation, fast compile/simulation equivalence filtering, low-cost proxy feature extraction, and selective commercial synthesis tool (Synopsys Design Compiler (DC)) evaluation. To prioritize which candidates deserve high-quality synthesis, we train a preference-based reward model using pairwise ranking supervision derived from commercial synthesis tool outcomes, and combine ensemble uncertainty with a diversity-aware acquisition rule to avoid early bias and redundant evaluations. A key advantage is that PRISM maintains a best-tradeoff set G of non-dominated designs throughout the run: G preserves the best solutions found so far, is fed back into generation and refinement, and enables consistent, cumulative improvements rather than repeatedly restarting from the baseline.
