Local and Sparse Kernel Operator Learning for Incompressible Flows: An Operator Lagrange Formulation

arXiv · 2026-10-21 · 论文PDF

We present a local and sparse kernel method for learning the solution operators of incompressible flows.

We present a local and sparse kernel method for learning the solution operators of incompressible flows. It builds on our earlier property-preserving kernel method, whose global output reconstruction and dense operator regression scale poorly. Our method replaces the global output reconstruction by local divergence-free interpolants with divergence-free polynomials of any degree, solved in parallel on the GPU. These interpolants are divergence free, periodic on periodic domains, and, with a multiscale kernel, approach Kolmogorov statistics under refinement. A compactly supported kernel, positive semidefinite in any input dimension, makes the operator regression sparse. Its support radius, and hence the sparsity, is fitted to data, and optional affine polynomials in the input capture global trends. Operator Lagrange weights join the two. They cost one sparse solve per new input and act on the training fields before the output reconstruction, so their local coefficients are never formed. We show that, unlike direct evaluation, Lagrange evaluation does not amplify solve errors by the cancellation among spatial coefficients.
