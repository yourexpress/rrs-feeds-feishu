IDPrior: Decoupling Identity Generation from Facial Image Synthesis

arXiv · 2026-11-06 · 论文PDF

Large-scale identity-labeled face datasets are essential for training modern face recognition models, yet collecting and annotating such data is costly and raises privacy and ethical concerns. Existing synthetic face generation methods typically derive identities from predefined class labels, unconditional image generation, or predefined latent sampling strategies, without explicitly modeling the underlying distribution of facial identities. In this paper, we propose IDPrior, a two-stage framework that decouples identity generation from image synthesis. We first learn a diffusion prior in the embedding space of a pretrained face recognition model to model the distribution of facial identities and generate novel identity embeddings. These embeddings are then used to condition a pretrained identity-conditioned diffusion model (DM), enabling the synthesis of multiple images of the same identity.
