Efficient and Scalable Multi-View Masked Autoencoder for Heterogeneous Graph Self-Supervised Learning

arXiv (cs.LG) · 2026-10-31 · 论文PDF

Recent years have witnessed the flourishing of self-supervised learning on heterogeneous graphs, e.g., masked autoencoder-based methods. However, existing methods suffer from three issues, including the prohibitive computational cost of constructing meta-path-induced subgraphs, the limitations of uniform random masking, and the suboptimal objective of reconstructing potentially noisy or uninformative original node features. To address these issues, we propose the Multi-View Masked Auto Encoder (MVMAE), a novel, efficient, and scalable framework for heterogeneous graph self-supervised learning. First, MVMAE introduces a novel multi-view mask-then-reconstruct strategy. Instead of masking individual nodes or features, MVMAE randomly masks entire meta-path views and guides the model to reconstruct their representations from remaining visible views. This approach shifts the learning objective to a high-level and semantic task, providing a more robust and informative supervisory signal that is effective even when initial node features are absent or flawed.
