Towards Sustainable Federated Learning via Carbon-Aware Client Selection

arXiv cs.DC · 2026-11-06 · 论文PDF

The Information/Communication Technology (ICT) industry is severely challenging the global environment due to the sharply rising carbon emissions generated by machine learning (ML) workloads. Federated Learning (FL) is one of the prominent distributed ML systems used in applications with billions of client devices, such as Google Gboard and Apple Siri. Nearly half of the total carbon footprint in FL originates from client-side computation during local training. However, existing client selection strategies in FL primarily focus on minimizing energy consumption rather than carbon emissions, despite the fact that carbon impact depends not only on computational load but also on the underlying carbon intensity of the energy source. In this paper, we address this gap by proposing a carbon-aware fair client selection strategy (CA-FCS) designed to minimize the carbon emissions associated with model training on client devices.
