Coding agents spend substantial computation generating actions and processing long interaction histories. Poor action choices can steer agents into low-value states, wasting computation and increasing API costs. Sampling and verifying alternative actions can improve the agent's decisions, but applying this procedure at every step adds substantial overhead. We introduce SWE-Verifier, a lightweight generative model that selectively triggers candidate sampling and verification. It scores the coding agent's proposed action to determine when additional candidate sampling is needed, then ranks the resulting actions before execution. We train SWE-Verifier from Qwen3.5-4B using preference data that groups contrasting actions with their resulting environment observations and rubric-guided teacher scores. Using the mini-swe-agent harness, SWE-Verifier improves resolution from 75.47% to 77.20% on MiniMax-M2.7 at 1.09 times cost and generalizes to DeepSeek-V4-Flash without training on its actions, improving resolution from 72.60% to 74.27% at 1.13 times cost.
