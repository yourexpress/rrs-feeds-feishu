Measuring Layer-wise Intrinsic Dimensionality of FFNs in LLMs via PCA

arXiv · 2026-11-24 · 论文PDF / 每日精选

LLM FFN 逐层本征维度 PCA 测量

Modern large language models (LLMs) keep the feed-forward network (FFN) intermediate width identical across every layer, a convention inherited from the original Transformer. Yet mechanistic work suggests layers at different depths do qualitatively different work, raising a natural question: are their capacity demands also non-uniform, and if so, can a fixed parameter budget be allocated more wisely than uniformly? To probe this, we apply principal component analysis (PCA) to the input of each FFN down-projection and measure, for every layer, its intrinsic dimensionality as the smallest number of principal components needed to reconstruct the activation up to a fixed cosine similarity. Scanning 58 open-weight LLMs spanning 14 families and 70M to 72B parameters, we find a recurring middle-heavy profile in which middle layers carry higher intrinsic dimensionality than edge layers; the profile is robust to the calibration corpus, even across English and Chinese, and is largely preserved through post-training fine-tuning.
