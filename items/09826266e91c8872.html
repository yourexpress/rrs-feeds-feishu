Atomic MathReasoner: Instance-Level Composition of Atomic Reasoning Skills for Mathematical LLMs

arXiv · 2026-11-18 · 论文PDF

Atomic MathReasoner：数学 LLM 原子推理技能的实例级组合

Most existing approaches enhance mathematical reasoning in large language models (LLMs) as a monolithic capability, despite the fact that different mathematical problems require different reasoning skills. Recent work has explored a more fine-grained perspective by decomposing mathematical reasoning into atomic skills and developing specialized modules for individual capabilities. However, how to effectively utilize these atomic abilities remains underexplored. To address this challenge, we propose Atomic MathReasoner (AMR), an instance-level framework for composing atomic reasoning skills. AMR first trains a library of capability-specific LoRA experts and performs gradient-free optimization over held-out validation subsets to discover a diverse set of low-loss composition prototypes. It evaluates the preference of each prototype using the token-level negative log-likelihood of reference solutions, filters ambiguous assignments through a confidence margin, and distills the resulting pseudo-labels into a lightweight router.
