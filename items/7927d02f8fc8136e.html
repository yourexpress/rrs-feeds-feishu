The rapid proliferation of distributed energy resources (DERs) aggregated by virtual power plants (VPPs) elevates cybersecurity risk by expanding the attack surface. A coordinated cyberattack has the potential to disrupt distribution-system, bulk-system, and market operations. Existing cyberphysical defense strategies largely assume access to grid network models and operating conditions, which may not be available to third-party aggregators. This paper proposes a novel hybrid, perturbation-based cyber-physical defense for VPP-operated DERs that blends game-theoretic planning with model-free reinforcement learning (RL) under a Bayesian belief-update framework. Topology agnosticism is defined from the VPP information and implementation perspective: the VPP does not require access to the feeder topology, line parameters, switching configuration, or full system state.
