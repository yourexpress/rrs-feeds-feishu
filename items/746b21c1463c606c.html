Plasma experiments observe coupled physical states through sparse, heterogeneous, and incomplete diagnostics. We present a proof of concept toward a multimodal foundation model for plasma state reconstruction during magnetic reconnection, motivated by the Facility for Laboratory Reconnection Experiments (FLARE). Using 250 synthetic VPIC simulations at fixed reference electron beta $β_{e0}=0.2$, with 225 training runs and 25 disjoint validation runs, we train a 5.5M-parameter residual 3D U-Net to reconstruct three magnetic-field components and electron density from incomplete observations. The model combines spatial-only pooling, which preserves all 24 temporal frames, with global spatiotemporal self-attention and rotary positional encoding. Independently masking magnetic and density modalities across random, grid, block, and temporal patterns enables sparse sensing, cross-modal completion, density super-resolution, and temporal completion with a single set of weights and no task-specific retraining.
