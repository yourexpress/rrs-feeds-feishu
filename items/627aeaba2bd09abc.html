Language Model Activations Inhabit Privileged Error-Correcting Basins

arXiv（Goodfire 等） · 2026-10-07 · 论文PDF / 每日精选

可解释性新发现：语言模型激活空间存在特权纠错吸引盆，盆地感知的自适应转向显著改进跨语言 steering

Language models exhibit remarkable robustness, continuing to produce coherent text even when their activations are perturbed by interventions like linear steering. We hypothesize that this robustness is a result of passive dynamics, i.e., constraining mechanisms in the forward pass that funnel activations toward "good" regions that produce coherent outputs. To investigate these hypothesized error-correcting mechanisms, we probe the geometry of language model activation space by observing the action of model layers on low-dimensional curves. In doing so, we discover that model activations occur within a cluster of distinct attracting basins, which differentiate natural activations geometrically from distributionally similar synthetic activations. Applying this lens to language model steering, we observe feature-specific basins along semantic steering directions, and find that steering moves activations between these basins.
