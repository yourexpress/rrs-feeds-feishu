PFedHASNet: A Personalized Federated Hybrid Analog-Spike Network for Multi-Subject Motor Imagery EEG Decoding

arXiv · 2026-11-24 · 论文PDF

Motor imagery electroencephalography (MI-EEG) data are often collected and stored separately across subjects or institutions, and EEG acquisition and annotation remain costly. Training models solely on individual subject data limits the use of information from other subjects, whereas centralizing raw EEG recordings raises privacy and data governance concerns. Federated learning (FL) enables collaborative model training across distributed clients without sharing raw EEG data, providing a practical approach to leveraging information from multiple subjects. Accordingly, we propose PFedHASNet, a personalized federated hybrid analog-spike network for MI-EEG decoding. The shared encoder integrates continuous-valued temporal features, leaky integrate-and-fire (LIF) spike responses, and channel-wise log-variance statistics through learnable fusion weights. Client-private residual adapters and classifiers perform subject-specific feature refinement and personalized prediction, respectively.
