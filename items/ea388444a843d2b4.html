Latent reasoning enables LLMs to operate in continuous embedding space, yet existing methods suffer from deterministic inference that precludes exploration of diverse reasoning paths. While recent approaches inject stochasticity to restore exploration, we identify two fundamental limitations: \textit{uniform stochasticity} across all steps is suboptimal, and unconstrained perturbations disrupt reasoning coherence. We propose Dynamic Stochasticity Control for Latent Reasoning (DLaR), a framework with two complementary mechanisms that operate at different temporal granularities within the GRPO reinforcement learning framework. At the token level, Adaptive Stochasticity Modulation (ASM) dynamically adjusts perturbation intensity at each latent step based on a confidence-entropy fusion signal, increasing exploration when the model is uncertain and preserving stability when confident. At the training level, Training-Aware Stochasticity Tuning (TAST) progressively decays the base stochasticity level following a curriculum schedule.
