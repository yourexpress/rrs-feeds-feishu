Improving fine-grained expressivity of graph neural networks

arXiv cs.LG · 2026-11-15 · 论文PDF

提升图神经网络细粒度表达能力

This thesis investigates the expressive power of neural networks for structured data, focusing on graph neural networks and symmetry-aware architectures. We present four main contributions. First, we characterize the generic expressive power of geometric message-passing neural networks on sparse graphs and introduce EGenNet, an architecture achieving the corresponding guarantees. Second, we use representation theory to characterize linear layers equivariant to multiple permutations, including wreath-product symmetries. Third, we study permutation-invariant set representations that preserve containment, establish dimensional bounds and impossibility results, and introduce MASNet. Finally, we propose FSW-GNN, a message-passing architecture combining strong expressivity and bi-Lipschitz stability guarantees with strong empirical performance on long-range graph-learning tasks. Together, these results provide a broader understanding of how neural architectures can preserve the geometric, algebraic, order-theoretic, and metric structure of their inputs.
