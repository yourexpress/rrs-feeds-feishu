MXFP4 for LLM Inference: Complementary Post-Training Quantization Techniques and Their Interactions

arXiv · 2026-11-24 · 论文PDF / 每日精选

MXFP4 LLM 推理：互补 PTQ 技术及其交互

Recent advances in low-precision representation have introduced several microscaling numerical formats for LLM quantization. By sharing a scale factor across groups of 16 or 32 elements, these formats enable aggressive low-precision quantization with minimal accuracy degradation, particularly when combined with modern PTQ algorithms. Their accuracy depends on how the numerical format interacts with rotation, PTQ, and scale selection. We study the composability of PTQ configurations across six LLMs and three microscaling formats: MXFP6, MXFP4, and NVFP4, systematically varying Hadamard rotation granularity, PTQ algorithm, and scale-selection strategy. Our central finding is that effective quantization recipes are format dependent: both MXFP4 and NVFP4 support accurate 4-bit inference, but techniques that benefit one do not necessarily transfer to the other. MXFP4 benefits substantially from rotation and error-correcting PTQ, whereas NVFP4 gains less from rotation, consistent with its finer-grained scales capturing more within-block variation.
