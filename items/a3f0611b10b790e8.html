Pareto DNN Verification: Fast for Most Queries

arXiv · 2026-11-06 · 论文PDF

Formal verification of deep neural networks (DNNs) faces a fundamental scalability challenge: verifying robustness is NP-hard and state-of-the-art tools often time out on modern architectures. We propose PAreto VErification, or PAVE: by augmenting DNNs with early exits (EEs) and verifying exit-by-exit, we solve a large fraction of robustness queries---roughly 80% in our evaluation---in a small fraction of the time required for direct verification. The key insight is that many inputs in a robustness neighborhood exit at an early layer. Verifying this early-exit behavior is often a tractable problem,allowing verification to terminate long before the full network is analyzed. EEs can be added without modifying the base network, and incur at most a 0.3% accuracy change across our tested architectures. We formalize the novel robustness property for EE networks, prove it is Fixed-Parameter Tractable under a trace stability condition (which commonly holds in practice), and present a sound and complete verification algorithm.
