LINK: Lightweight and Inductive Node-to-Node Propagation Rule Learning for Traffic Kriging

arXiv cs.LG · 2026-11-16 · 论文PDF

轻量归纳节点传播规则学习用于交通kriging

Spatio-temporal traffic kriging aims to reconstruct traffic time series at unobserved locations with no historical traffic observations by leveraging spatial and temporal dependencies across the road network. Existing Graph Neural Network (GNN)-based approaches primarily rely on neighborhood aggregation to capture spatial dependencies, thereby modeling traffic interactions implicitly and making it difficult to accurately recover traffic magnitudes at locations without historical observations. To address this limitation, we propose LINK, a Lightweight and Inductive Node-to-node propagation rule learning paradigm for traffic Kriging. LINK introduces three key designs: 1) a propagation-centric traffic kriging paradigm that explicitly learns a shared node-to-node transition rule; 2) a context-aware Spectral Propagation Network that performs traffic-state propagation in both the spectral and time domains; and 3) TraffiKrig, a realistic traffic kriging benchmark that preserves heterogeneous road-segment characteristics and road-specific metadata while enabling systematic comparison of representative kriging methods under a unified metadata-enhanced setting.
