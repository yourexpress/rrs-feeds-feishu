Beyond Service Discovery: Coverage Awareness in LLM-Driven Service Composition

arXiv cs.DC · 2026-11-15 · 论文PDF

Large language models increasingly support the automated discovery and composition of services. In federated data ecosystems, this creates new opportunities for reusing existing data products and transformation services when constructing service-based data exchange modeled as data-sharing pipelines. However, finding relevant reusable assets does not imply that the available assets are sufficient to satisfy a consumer request. In this paper, we distinguish reuse discovery, the ability to identify relevant published assets, from reuse coverage, the extent to which the available assets support the transformations required by a request. We further consider coverage awareness, the ability of a composition system to recognize insufficient coverage. We investigate this distinction using PipeFinder, an LLM-driven framework for composing data-sharing pipelines, and evaluate 99 consumer requests against a fixed catalogue of data products and transformation services. Our experiments show that relevant data products can be discovered reliably, while successful composition remains considerably more difficult.
