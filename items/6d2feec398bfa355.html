Coexponential Exploration in the Clef Programming Language

arXiv · 2026-10-09 · 论文PDF

Clef编程语言中的上指数探索

Clef was created as a concurrent programming language whose compiler tracks state ownership, waiting computations and request dependencies. We develop a coexponential account of stateful services and their client pools within this design. Delimited continuations retain the computation and resources needed to complete each interaction. These structures give our compiler premises for verifying service composition and preserving it through native lowering to multiple hardware targets. We connect finite actor state transitions to the initial, service and final rules of a coexponential server. A separate operational account relates actor dispatch to single-use continuation frames. We prove state-ownership and reply preservation for that translation, give a finite communication-summary analysis, and circular-wait exclusion from the conditions needed. A further result identifies when changing service order preserves both state and replies. Our Program Semantic Graph retains these facts through compilation.
