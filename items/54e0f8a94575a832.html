SCARF: A Scene-Adaptive Depth-Guided G-3DGS Encoder Accelerator with Semantic Reuse and Fused Dataflow

arXiv (cs.AR) · 2026-11-02 · 论文PDF

Depth-guided, cost-volume-based Generalizable 3D Gaussian Splatting (G-3DGS) encoders reconstruct scenes as sets of Gaussian primitives directly from multi-view images without requiring per-scene optimization, thereby facilitating feed-forward novel-view synthesis on edge computing platforms. However, their shared encoder architecture presents two primary bottlenecks. First, the per-pixel depth search disregards semantic similarity within the 2D feature space, causing depth search operations to account for over 65% of memory traffic due to redundant exhaustive candidate scans. Second, the sequential pipeline of depth prediction followed by Gaussian generation neglects local 3D geometric continuity, resulting in more than 52% of generated Gaussians being redundant prior to instantiation. In this paper, we introduce SCARF, a dataflow-co-designed accelerator that aligns each bottleneck with a corresponding scene-level regularity.
