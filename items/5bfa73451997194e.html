NVIDIA and MediaTek Unveil Co-Developed Chips for AI Edge-to-Cloud Workflows

NVIDIA and MediaTek announced a major expansion of their joint chip development, integrating NVLink Fusion and custom silicon to power AI workloads spanning hyperscale data centers, personal AI supercomputers, and autonomous vehicles.

Global semiconductor leaders NVIDIA and MediaTek have announced a major expansion of their strategic partnership, introducing co-developed system-on-chip (SoC) architectures tailored for edge-to-cloud artificial intelligence integration. Unveiled during an executive technology briefing, the new hardware series fuses MediaTek’s low-power mobile processor architecture with NVIDIA’s high-performance Tensor Core GPU capabilities into a single silicon footprint. Designed specifically to run parameter-heavy generative AI models directly on localized hardware devices, the chips eliminate latency bottlenecks associated with continuous cloud server requests while maintaining high computational efficiency. Industry engineers highlight that the unified architecture allows smartphone platforms, autonomous automotive units, and internet-of-things (IoT) systems to execute complex multimodal AI tasks natively on-device. Corporate leadership from both chipmakers noted that the collaboration will standardize edge-computing developer frameworks, lowering energy overhead for enterprise deployments across global mobile and automotive ecosystems. Hardware analysts view the release as a pivotal milestone that accelerates real-time on-device AI processing without relying solely on remote data center infrastructure.
