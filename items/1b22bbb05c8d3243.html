Investigating the Impact of CI-Source Sensitivity in Carbon-Aware Resource Management Systems

arXiv cs.DC · 2026-11-16 · 论文PDF

碳感知资源管理系统中CI源敏感性的影响调查

The sustainable growth of data centres increasingly depends on Resource-Management Systems (RMSs) that aim to reduce carbon emissions of computing workloads without degrading performance. This need is intensified by the rise of Artificial Intelligence (AI) workloads, which demand exponentially growing amounts of compute and energy across geographically distributed infrastructure. Recent carbon-aware RMSs exploit temporal and spatial variation in grid Carbon Intensity (CI) to shift workloads towards lower-carbon periods or locations. In practice, CI estimates and forecasts can be inaccurate and uncertain, and CI providers' methods differ substantially. Such discrepancies may have a significant impact on RMS placement decision-making, raising concerns about the fragility of existing solutions. Thus, in this work, we ask how discrepancies between plausible CI estimates propagate into resource-management decisions and operational outcomes. To answer this question, we harmonise and characterise CI traces from Wattnet and ElectricityMaps across five Central European zones.
