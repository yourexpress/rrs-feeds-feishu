OverclusterDRG: a Deterministic, LP-Free Heuristic for Robust $k$-Center

arXiv cs.LG · 2026-11-13 · 论文PDF

确定性无LP的鲁棒k-center启发式OverclusterDRG

The $k$-center problem is a fundamental formulation for metric clustering. However, its min-max objective is highly sensitive to outliers, motivating the Robust $k$-Center (R$k$C) formulation which permits excluding up to $z$ points as outliers. Existing algorithms for R$k$C suffer from scalability issues: LP rounding relies on fractional relaxations that scale poorly, known combinatorial approximation algorithms are computationally expensive due to radius guessing, and scalable randomized heuristics require exponential trial multipliers to guarantee success. We present OverclusterDRG, a deterministic, LP-free heuristic for R$k$C. The algorithm leverages the theoretical insight of farthest-point oversampling -- adding exactly $z$ extra candidates to the center set to absorb outliers -- followed by a forward-greedy initialization and a $1$-swap local search. Evaluations across real-world datasets show that OverclusterDRG attains a mean approximation ratio of 1.147 against fractional LP lower bounds on the UCI Adult dataset, outperforming state-of-the-art baselines in both clustering quality and speed.
