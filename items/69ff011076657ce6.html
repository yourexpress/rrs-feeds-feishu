Auditing firewall logs against requirements mapped to controls and frameworks must retain overlapping obligations, preserve uncertainty from missing attributes and count distinct affected records. Direct, bitmap and relational methods can express this audit; a correlation implementation must preserve distinctions that ordinary match counts and rule-hit totals discard. We present an exact ternary correlation algorithm that compiles categorical conjunctions into one bounded-integer matrix product. A dominating false-predicate penalty separates known mismatches from missing evidence, while per-record hierarchy unions prevent duplicate counting. We prove their composition and handle high-cardinality endpoints through exact address classes and versioned labels. Evaluation compares eight CPU/GPU implementations, includes public traffic and 133 published source-address ACLs with generated observations, and measures hierarchy and policy-rebuild costs. On an NVIDIA L4, matched fused execution reduces latency by 8.8 to 11.2 percent relative to two-product correlation across five tested shapes.
