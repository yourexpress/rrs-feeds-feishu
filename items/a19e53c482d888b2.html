CastWorld: Benchmarking Context-Aware Agentic Time Series Forecasting Beyond Predictive Accuracy

arXiv cs.LG · 2026-11-11 · 论文PDF

Agentic time series forecasting extends conventional approaches beyond one-shot numerical prediction toward autonomous, context-aware interaction. However, existing benchmarks offer limited integrated support for diverse contextual information, forecasting-specific adaptive interactions, and systematic evaluation of forecasting agents. In this paper, we introduce CastWorld, an agentic benchmark for context-aware time series forecasting. First, its context-rich dataset suite spans 8 domains and 25 real-world datasets, covering external variables, events, and historical cases under a unified context specification. Second, a lightweight, modular forecasting-native harness provides 49 core tools and a state-adaptive loop that tracks forecast versions and enables iterative evidence gathering and forecast revision. Intermediate forecasts can expose information gaps, while newly acquired evidence guides subsequent revisions and final version selection. Third, model-level evaluation assesses forecast quality, process execution, behavioral trustworthiness, and resource efficiency through decision trajectories and forecast-version histories.
