Projected Penalty-Gradient Tracking for Communication-Efficient Byzantine-Resilient Federated Learning

arXiv cs.LG · 2026-11-17 · 论文PDF

拜占庭鲁棒联邦学习的投影惩罚梯度跟踪

We investigate communication-efficient federated learning over heterogeneous datasets in the presence of Byzantine adversaries capable of uploading arbitrary information. Penalty-based methods bound the influence of each worker through the bounded gradient of a penalty function; however, the bound is lost once the uploads are compressed, since a Byzantine worker can accumulate an unbounded state at the server. To retain the Byzantine resilience under compressed uploads, we propose a projected penalty-gradient tracking framework, where each honest worker uploads a compressed residual of its penalty gradient and the server projects each reconstructed state onto the known penalty-gradient ball. The framework accommodates any contractive compressor and yields two algorithms: fault-resilient proximal gradient with consistent sparsification (FRPG-CS), whose shared mask is protected by freshly drawn reserve rows, and lazily aggregated FRPG (LAG-FRPG), whose uploads are event-triggered.
