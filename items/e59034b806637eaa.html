Continuous-Time Set-Transformers for Asynchronous WiFi-IMU Indoor Localization

arXiv cs.LG · 2026-11-09 · 论文PDF

Indoor localization commonly combines WiFi received signal strength indicator (RSSI) fingerprints with inertial measurements. However, these sensor streams are sampled asynchronously at different rates; therefore, observations rarely align in time and may be missing or stale. Most existing methods resample the data onto a shared clock and process each modality using a separate branch. We instead present a continuous-time transformer that represents observations as an unordered set of timestamped tokens. Each token encodes its measurement, modality, and elapsed time since acquisition. A single attention block jointly fuses information across modalities and time, avoiding both resampling and modality-specific branches while remaining invariant to token order. During training, we randomly mask entire modalities and individual observations, enabling the model to operate under missing or outdated inputs. We validate the modality-specific encoders on their respective benchmarks and evaluate the fused model on both simulated and real datasets, including a crosssession dataset collected on unseen days.
