Causal Optimal-Transport Attention: Positional Priors and Length Extrapolation

arXiv (cs.LG) · 2026-11-01 · 论文PDF

Causal masking obstructs the column balancing used by optimal-transport (OT) attention. We study a prefix-consistent unbalanced operator and characterize the positional prior it induces. Under bounded scores, for any fixed balancing strength $ρ<1$, its fixed point dilutes attention: every fixed window receives a vanishing share as the context grows. For constant scores, we derive an exact scale-invariant prior. We then classify positional encodings by whether their distant attention mass remains bounded away from one. Discrete window-mass bounds quantify the finite-length dilution of summable log-distance priors near exponent one. Preregistered experiments with 25M- and 100M-parameter language models establish improved loss extrapolation over softmax; exploratory comparisons favor an explicit distance bias at a fraction of the cost. CABLE outperforms RoPE with evaluation-time YaRN on every seed at three training lengths. Changing only the bias beyond the training length moves long-context loss in the predicted direction on all 30 tested checkpoints.
