Evidence from a Multi-Regime Causal Discovery Benchmark: UCMT Algorithms, Comparative Performance, and Controlled Ablation Studies

We present an internal, pre-registered evaluation of the UCMT causal discovery algorithm family alongside established alternatives across heterogeneous benchmark regimes, complemented by a separately pre-registered, controlled study of UCMT v2 Global components. The contribution is both empirical and methodological: a family-stratified comparison of three actively developed algorithm configurations and targeted evidence about which internal stages affect their outcomes. In the main benchmark, UCMT v2 Global has higher directed F1 than NOTEARS and DAGMA in selected real, semi-synthetic and Gaussian-network comparisons at the paired-run level, while trailing leading permutation-search methods in other settings.
