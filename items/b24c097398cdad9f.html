Advection-diffusion plays an essential role in transport phenomena such as atmospheric and cloud dynamics. Improved forecasting of cloud motion, in turn, enhances solar power production forecasts and facilitates the integration of additional photovoltaic (PV) sources into the power grid. However, both classical numerical models and emerging deep learning approaches face challenges when applied to spatially irregular sensor networks such as those formed by weather stations. In this work, we adopt a graph signal processing perspective and model advection-diffusion particles as signals on a graph to capture the dynamics of these processes. We introduce a Physics-Informed Graph Neural Network (PING) for forecasting future advection-diffusion particle concentrations on irregular grids. The model incorporates a discretized advection-diffusion equation as a soft constraint in the loss function to guide learning toward physically consistent solutions. This model is on par with state of the art models on synthetic and real transport phenomena datasets, including sea surface temperature and cloud index datasets.
