Imputing Missing Near-Surface Air Temperature using Graph Attention Networks with Distance-Aware Edge Features

arXiv cs.LG · 2026-11-11 · 论文PDF

We introduce a graph attention network for reconstructing near-surface air temperature fields from incomplete observations. The model represents each grid cell as a node connected to its $k$ nearest neighbors, with attention weights that depend on node features, distance, and land-ocean transitions. This graph representation avoids the polar distortions associated with treating the globe as a flat latitude-longitude image, as most deep learning reconstruction methods do. This is particularly relevant for historical temperature records, which extend back to the mid-nineteenth century but remain incomplete, with the largest gaps early in the record and at high latitudes. We train our model on CMIP6 CESM2 historical simulations masked with the time-varying HadCRUT5 observational coverage from 1850 to 2014. We then compare our approach with ordinary kriging, GraphEM, and the LaMa inpainting network on a held-out realization. The graph model achieves the lowest spatial RMSE (0.997 K), 10.1% lower than LaMa and approximately 31% lower than kriging and GraphEM.
