InfuseLoop: Cross-Trajectory Latent Injection for Scalable Parallel Looped Transformers

arXiv cs.LG · 2026-11-17 · 论文PDF

并行循环 Transformer 的跨轨迹潜注入扩展深度

Parallel Looped Transformers (PLTs) use otherwise idle decoding compute by overlapping recurrent iterations. However, vanilla PLTs scale poorly with depth: they exhibit training-loss spikes, and their quality gains shrink as more loops are added. The benefit of additional loops depends on the information available to each iteration, so we examine whether the existing computational dependencies limit effective depth scaling. We find that only the preceding in-trajectory state is passed across loop boundaries, even though completed cross-trajectory representations are already available. Based on this finding, we propose InfuseLoop, a cross-trajectory latent injection mechanism. It aggregates causally available hidden states from later prediction trajectories through lightweight attention residuals. This design preserves token-parallel training and concurrent loop execution, and requires only $O(Rd)$ additional hidden-state storage. Experiments show that InfuseLoop eliminates training-loss spikes and improves validation-loss scaling up to eight loops.
