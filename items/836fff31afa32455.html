One of the remarkable abilities of Transformer-based neural networks is in-context learning. This motivates us to think about "in-context" control. Can we control a system knowing only (1) that it belongs to a particular family of systems and (2) some data on system evolution (provided as the context trajectory) but no specific parameter information? Specifically, we investigate the ability of Transformer-models to perform "in-context" control of unstable, nonlinear dynamical systems, where context consists only of the unforced system dynamics. We leverage a multi-head, GPT-2-style architecture, and we evaluate performance for two classical nonlinear systems -- the cartpole system and the two-link acrobot system, where the system parameters are unknown to the Transformer. We consider a setting where trajectories from an expert controller are available at training time but not at inference time. Here, we identify training techniques that are essential to train Transformers to perform low-level control.
