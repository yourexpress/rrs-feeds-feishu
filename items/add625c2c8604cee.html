WingBench: Benchmarking How AI Agents Search for Better Designs

arXiv · 2026-11-20 · 论文PDF

Design search often uses cheap learned predictions to choose which candidates deserve expensive tests, but these tests can fail, especially on the unusual designs that optimization favors. We introduce WingBench, a multifidelity benchmark in which optimizers and AI agents design transonic wings with computational fluid dynamics (CFD) inside the search. They can query a learned aerodynamic surrogate and spend a fixed budget of sequential CFD evaluations, each returning a score or a failure type. WingBench grades the design each method recommends, reporting separately whether it receives a valid score and how good that score is. Using WingBench, we contribute a study of five baseline optimizers and a comparison of three AI agent methods, which produced 6,629 CFD evaluation records. Feedback helps baseline optimizers deliver valid designs when it changes their later proposals, but the differential-evolution variant does so with lower aerodynamic quality. An open-ended research agent finds designs of similar quality to the strongest baselines over 32 CFD queries, with different prior information and unmatched total computation.
