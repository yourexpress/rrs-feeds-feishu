NEMESIS2.0: LLM-Aided Netlist-Driven Performance Equation Modeling for Accelerated Analog Circuit Sizing

arXiv cs.AR · 2026-11-13 · 论文PDF

LLM辅助网表驱动性能方程建模，加速模拟电路尺寸优化

This work presents NEMESIS2.0, an open-source LLM-aided framework that automatically derives equation-based performance models for analog circuits. The resulting equations provide fast, accurate surrogates for SPICE evaluation. To validate the method, equation-based models are developed for seven circuit test cases in a commercial 65nm CMOS technology, covering 15 metrics on five operational transconductance amplifier topologies, and six metrics on two dynamic comparator topologies. When tested on 1000 unseen variants of these seven designs, each corresponding to a different set of independently sampled transistor sizes, the accuracy is found to be within 5% of SPICE. To demonstrate use cases for NEMESIS2.0, the generated models are used as performance evaluators in multiple transistor sizers based on different population-based stochastic search optimizers. For each of the seven test cases, transistor sizing is performed for 100 different sets of performance specifications, with final SPICE simulation confirming that 83.3% of the resulting designs meet all specifications after a single sizing run.
