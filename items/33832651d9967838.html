DSADiff: Data Structure-Aware Diffusion for Crystal Structure Prediction via Mitigating Complexity- and Imbalance-Induced Biases

arXiv cs.LG · 2026-11-17 · 论文PDF

数据结构感知的晶体结构预测扩散

Crystal Structure Prediction (CSP) is an important step in accelerating the generation and discovery of new materials. In recent years, diffusion-based CSP methods have made significant progress. However, existing models still suffer from generation bias when facing diverse materials spaces, while systematic approaches for tracing the sources of such performance variations remain limited. To address this issue, we propose LAS (Layered Analysis Strategy), a mechanism analysis framework that identifies crystal complexity and elemental composition imbalance as two key factors affecting generation difficulty and further reveals their relationships with model generation performance. Building on these findings, we further propose DSADiff (Data Structure-Aware Diffusion), a data structure-aware diffusion model that transforms the key factors identified by LAS into a unified generation difficulty representation and uses it to jointly drive feature modeling and training strategy optimization. This enables the model to adapt its representation and generation capability to samples with different data structures and difficulty levels, thereby mitigating learning bias across samples.
