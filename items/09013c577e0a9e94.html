Learning Lane-Structured Conditioning Fields for Online HD Mapping

arXiv · 2026-11-06 · 论文PDF

High-definition maps provide critical structural information for downstream autonomous driving tasks. Recent methods increasingly rely on external priors to improve prediction accuracy and robustness. Yet, existing prior representations expose a trade-off: dense rasters are easy to integrate but ignore certain map geometric properties, while sparse vectors preserve geometric structure but require dedicated encoders and are difficult to plug into existing architectures. Moreover, external priors can be inaccurate, costly to acquire, or difficult to maintain, motivating a structural representation that can provide geometric guidance while remaining learnable and broadly applicable. We address this limitation with the Lane Field, a dense BEV-aligned geometric representation that encodes local lane structure at each spatial location and can be either predicted from onboard observations or constructed from external map priors.
