Scalable Bayesian Optimization for Efficient Exploration of Moire Patterns

arXiv cs.LG · 2026-11-09 · 论文PDF

Large materials design spaces often contain rare high-performing configurations that are difficult to identify through trial-and-error exploration or purely representative machine-learning (ML) sampling. In this work, we present FruBO, a flexible Bayesian Optimization framework for accelerating the discovery of optimal configurations in computationally demanding materials design problems. As a case study, we investigate multilayer Moiré patterns generated by the superposition of six rotated hexagonal lattices, where the objective is to identify the rotational conformation that maximizes pore-size uniformity, quantified using the Jain fairness index. The resulting benchmark contains 600,000 unique configurations and exhibits a highly skewed objective distribution, making the global optimum difficult to locate by random sampling. We compare random exploration, Gaussian Process-based Bayesian Optimization, and Bayesian Optimization using a lightweight CatBoost surrogate with committee-based uncertainty estimation.
