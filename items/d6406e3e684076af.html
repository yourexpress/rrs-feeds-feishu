Injecting skills into a frozen language model currently costs a million parameters and a reinforcement-learning pipeline. We introduce \method{}, a System-1 decision operator trained by behavior cloning that lowers this cost by roughly two orders of magnitude. The default operator uses 330K parameters to match a 1.33M-parameter operator trained with reinforcement learning, exceeds or achieve comparable performance, while collapsing 3,685-token deliberation into a 6-token decision with no loss in accuracy. A rank-4 variant with 23K parameters, 1/58 of the strongest published skill operator, suffices for SearchQA and near-suffices for LiveMath, where higher rank still helps; the same recipe transfers across five tasks and three backbones, with out-of-distribution gains persisting on LiveMath problems released months after training.
