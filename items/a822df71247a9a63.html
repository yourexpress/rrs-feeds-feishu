ReCALM: A Residual-enhanced Crowdsourced Analytic Learning Mechanism for Distributed Continual Service Construction

arXiv cs.LG · 2026-11-14 · 论文PDF

残差增强众包分析学习机制用于分布式持续服务构建

Crowdsourcing has emerged as a promising paradigm for constructing AI services by harnessing the collective capabilities of distributed workers. As consumers' demands continually evolve, service models must acquire new knowledge while retaining previously learned information. However, due to the inherited reliance on gradients for backpropagation, existing approaches typically suffer from catastrophic forgetting during continual model updates on heterogeneous data. Recent studies have introduced analytic learning, a gradient-free paradigm that achieves invariance through recursive closed-form solutions. Nevertheless, existing analytic methods are typically constrained by single-layer linear networks, which tend to suffer from underfitting due to their inability to perform deep representation learning. To address this issue, we propose ReCALM, a Residual-enhanced Crowdsourced Analytic Learning Mechanism that achieves deep nonlinear invariance for distributed continual service construction.
