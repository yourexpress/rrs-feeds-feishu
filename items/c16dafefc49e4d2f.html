Turbo-GMM: FlashAttention-Inspired GPU-Accelerated Gaussian Mixture Model

arXiv (cs.LG) · 2026-11-02 · 论文PDF

Expectation-maximization (EM) for Gaussian mixture models (GMMs) requires scoring $N$ data points against $K$ mixture components. Prior fused GPU implementations, notably Flash-GMM, eliminate materialization of the $N \times K$ responsibility matrix but traverse the component dimension twice to compute normalization terms and accumulate sufficient statistics. We introduce Turbo-GMM, a single-sweep fused EM kernel that completes responsibility normalization and sufficient-statistic accumulation within one traversal of the component dimension. By retaining the complete component axis on chip, Turbo-GMM avoids the second score computation required by the released two-sweep Flash-GMM implementation. Its applicability is limited by register pressure, occupancy, and the component and feature dimensions, making it particularly suitable for large datasets with relatively small numbers of components. For larger configurations, Turbo-GMM employs a two-pass fallback following the established fused design.
