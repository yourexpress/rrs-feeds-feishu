NV-Tesseract-AD: Curriculum-Enhanced Conditional Diffusion for Multivariate Time-Series Anomaly Detection

arXiv · 2026-10-08 · 论文PDF

课程增强条件扩散做多变量时序异常检测

We propose NV-Tesseract-AD, a curriculum learning-enhanced conditional diffusion framework for multivariate time-series anomaly detection. Our central hypothesis is that reconstruction difficulty can be explicitly structured along two complementary axes, masking severity and diffusion noise level, and that training from easier to harder tasks along both axes simultaneously stabilizes optimization and improves residual discriminability. We integrate a Transformer-based diffusion backbone with a three-stage dual curriculum over masking ratios and diffusion timesteps, retaining anomalous samples throughout training and relying on structured corruption to drive self-supervised representation learning. We evaluate NV-Tesseract-AD under two regimes: in-distribution training on the TSB-UAD-M corpus and strict zero-shot transfer to ten EasyTSAD benchmark families without per-dataset tuning. The dual-axis curriculum produces masking-invariant reconstruction, confirmed by a near-zero loss gap between complementary masking strategies within the first training stage, and yields an approximately 3.7-fold improvement in F1 and a 56 percent reduction in MSE relative to a non-curriculum baseline. …
