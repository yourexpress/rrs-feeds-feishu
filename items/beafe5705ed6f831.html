Mechanistic Failure Modes of Weight Pruning in Transformer Language Models

arXiv(cs.LG) · 2026-10-27 · 论文PDF / 每日精选

GPT-2 上剪枝的机制性失效模式实证：幅值剪枝使 IOI 电路归零或孤立，输出行为保留不等于内部计算保留

Pruned language models are judged almost entirely by accuracy and perplexity, which assumes that preserved output behaviour implies preserved internal computation. We test this assumption with a factorial pruning study on GPT-2, crossing two importance criteria (magnitude, random), two component types (attention heads, MLP neurons) and three sparsity levels (3%, 50%, 70%). Using the fully characterised indirect object identification (IOI) circuit as ground truth, we measure circuit integrity with activation patching, path patching and direct logit attribution. We find two qualitatively distinct failure modes. Magnitude head pruning >= zeroes the circuit: output-facing heads are removed, and the model falls to near-chance. Magnitude neuron pruning at 30% inverts it: all 26 circuit heads remain intact, but Name Mover contributions flip sign, and the model systematically prefers the wrong name (17.7% accuracy). Perplexity ranks it as less damaged than random head pruning (74.6 vs 140.1). A replication of the Greater-Than circuit shows that the critical component type depends on the circuit's architecture.
