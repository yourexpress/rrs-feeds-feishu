Stability of Parallel Transport Convolution under Riemannian Metric Perturbations

arXiv · 2026-11-17 · 论文PDF

黎曼度量扰动下 parallel transport 卷积的稳定性

Geometric convolutions on Riemannian manifolds depend on the geometry of the underlying domain, making stability under metric perturbations a fundamental question in geometric deep learning. This paper studies this question for parallel transport convolution through methods from differential geometry and analysis. On a fixed compact manifold, we establish that, for a continuous input signal and a continuous reference kernel supported in a sufficiently small normal neighborhood, the convolution output depends continuously in $L^2$ on the Riemannian metric in the $C^2$-topology, under the specified localization and transport-path conventions. The analysis develops perturbation estimates for geodesics, parallel transport, Jacobi fields, and exponential maps, and uses these estimates to compare the transported kernels. A central difficulty is the cut locus, which obstructs uniform comparison of the transport construction over the entire manifold. We address this difficulty by combining uniform estimates away from the cut locus with integral bounds on neighborhoods of arbitrarily small volume, while also controlling the change in the Riemannian volume measure.
