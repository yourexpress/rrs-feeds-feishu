IMPACT: Constraint-Aware Search for Imperfectly Factorized Accelerator Mappings

The efficiency of Deep Neural Network (DNN) accelerators depends on how each tensor operation is mapped onto the hardware. A mapping specifies how the computation is tiled across the memory hierarchy, ordered, and parallelized across the processing elements. These choices form a combinatorially large search space, where efficient mappings are hard to find. Most violate hardware constraints, and most remaining mappings underutilize the hardware. Existing mappers either search only part of this space to keep the search tractable, excluding the most efficient mappings, or sample the full space randomly, so most mappings they evaluate are infeasible. We present IMPACT, a mapper that searches the full space. IMPACT pairs a genetic algorithm with a constraint-aware repair operator that converts each infeasible mapping into a feasible one instead of discarding it. On a flexible accelerator, IMPACT outperforms state-of-the-art mappers in EDP, latency, and energy on every DeepBench workload.
