Weights & Caches: LLMs Can Program Tabular Foundation Models

arXiv cs.LG · 2026-11-15 · 论文PDF

权重与缓存：LLM可编程表格基础模型

Neural networks are typically programmed through optimization, rather than by explicitly specifying their computations. Recent work on weight-space programming suggests that large language models (LLMs) can instead reason about and manipulate neural-network weights directly. In this work, we show that LLMs can directly program statistical computations in tabular foundation models (TFMs), whose parameters enable inference across different datasets. We investigate three complementary settings using GPT-6 Astra, without gradient-based optimization. First, Astra directly programs nanoTabPFN from zero-initialized weights, achieving a mean ROC AUC of 0.849 across 20 TabArena datasets, compared to 0.876 for a pretrained baseline. Second, Astra diagnoses and repairs a signed-product regression failure in pretrained TabPFN-3.5, restoring near-perfect predictions on held-out synthetic datasets while preserving performance on TabArena-Lite, with only a 0.08% average relative increase in error.
