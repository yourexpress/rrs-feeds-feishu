Wearable inertial measurement unit (IMU) systems for construction worker human activity recognition (HAR) routinely report aggregate accuracies above 95 %, rarely under genuine subject-independent (leave-one-subject-out (LOSO)) evaluation, masking the per-individual variance that determines real-world deployment performance. Using the VTT-ConIoT benchmark, we conduct, to our knowledge, the first systematic configuration sensitivity analysis of this dataset: six classifiers across five sensor placements and three window sizes under a single LOSO protocol, a personalised-calibration pilot, and a validated comparison of tabular GAN-based and standard time-series augmentation.
