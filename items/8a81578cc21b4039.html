SlotFill: Repairing Lossy SoC Communication Traces for Post-Silicon Validation

arXiv · 2026-11-22 · 论文PDF

Post-silicon validation relies on traces of messages exchanged by SoC components, but trace buffers and monitors drop messages, often leaving no mark of what was lost. We present SlotFill, which puts each deleted message back in the right gap, with the right type and order. SlotFill gives every gap between the remaining messages a fixed number of slots, and a Transformer-based model fills each slot with a message or leaves it empty when nothing was deleted there, so the input never reveals how much was lost. The model also uses monitor fields (timestamps, transaction IDs, addresses) from surviving messages. On whole-program gem5 traces of 4- and 8-core systems (2.07 billion messages), SlotFill restores up to 97% of deleted messages exactly at 15% loss and up to 87% at 50% loss, and 76-83% at 15% loss when timestamps are coarse.
