LEGOSpec: A Composable and Efficient Framework for Speculative Draft Design and Training

arXiv · 2026-11-24 · 论文PDF / 每日精选

LEGOSpec：投机草稿设计与训练的可组合高效框架

Speculative decoding accelerates large language model (LLM) generation using a lightweight draft. Existing methods evaluate complete recipes under different training configurations, leaving component effects, interactions, and composition benefits unclear. Therefore, we present LEGOSpec, a composable framework for speculative draft design and training. LEGOSpec decomposes draft models into Context, Backbone, Proposal, and Objective components that share a unified training infrastructure. Using LEGOSpec, we systematically vary components and evaluate selected combinations under matched training conditions to isolate their effects and interactions. These experiments reveal three findings: (1) Different workloads favor different backbones, depending on context length and memory budget, with within-block dependency modeling particularly important for long contexts. (2) Proposal design must balance acceptance and latency. (3) The training objective should match token distributions locally and optimize acceptance length across the block. These findings guide workload-aware configurations and DFlux, a balanced general-purpose draft combining a convolution-equipped SWA--GQA hybrid backbone…
