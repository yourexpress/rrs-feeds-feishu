Federated learning (FL) enables collaborative model training without data centralization but struggles with incentive alignment, accountability, and robustness under heterogeneous non-IID data. Existing decentralized FL (DeFL) systems leverage blockchains for coordination yet face high on-chain overhead, unverifiable self-reports, and weak fairness guarantees. We present the FLAI Protocol, a trusted execution environment (TEE)-backed DeFL framework that integrates enclave-validated aggregation, sparse Peer Evaluation Graph (sPEG) scoring, and on-chain settlement. sPEG incorporates peer-based quality, class coverage, promptness, effort, and reputation into composite contribution scores. The protocol formalizes contribution evaluation and tokenized incentive distribution, ensuring verifiable fairness and resilience against free-riding and collusion. We evaluate FLAI on image, text, and healthcare datasets and demonstrate parity with FedAvg in utility while improving fairness and accountability.
