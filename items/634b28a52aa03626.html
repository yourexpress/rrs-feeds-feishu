Native Block-Quantized Matrix Multiplication and Explicit K/V Residency with Dual-View Access for LLM Inference on RISC-V Vector Processors

LLM 推理原生块量化矩阵乘与显式 K/V 驻留（双视图访问）

Some key execution states in large language model inference persist across multiple instructions and are difficult to express and exploit within RVV semantics centered on individual operations. We define specialization boundaries according to data-state lifetimes and propose Quantized Block Streams (QBS) and Attention/KV Context (AKV) while preserving RVV programmability. QBS organizes native-block format processing, block traversal, integer computation, and ordered FP32 accumulation into a unified execution process, whereas AKV maintains local K/V residency and supplies operand views aligned with the computation direction while leaving Attention arithmetic on RVV. Cycle-accurate RTL evaluation shows that QBS achieves geometric-mean speedups of 35.68x and 51.34x for projection Compute and Matmul, respectively, while AKV achieves a 1.45x geometric-mean speedup over Tiled RVV for Decode Attention. Compared with the baseline, the complete design increases total area by 12.65%; on the evaluated projection workloads, throughput per area and energy efficiency improve by 46.63x and 19.78x, respectively.
