When Zero Is Competitive: A Descriptive Pilot of Transformer and Gate-Graph Surrogates on Random-Angle Quantum Circuits

arXiv cs.LG · 2026-11-15 · 论文PDF

随机角度量子电路上Transformer与门图代理的零竞争试点

We examine supervised classical prediction of all one-qubit Z and two-qubit ZZ expectation values on noiseless, layered 4-10-qubit RX/RY/RZ-CNOT circuits. In a 63-cell stratified workload with disjoint circuit-template tests, we evaluate one transformer encoder and gate-dependency graph neural networks against literal-zero and training-derived constant predictors. On the Arm K training labels, 44.89% of scalar targets have absolute magnitude below 0.05. The held-out-template zero predictor has equal-instance mean absolute error 0.193399, and none of the evaluated transformer checkpoints or final graph-network endpoints achieves a lower score. A separate product-state positive control achieves MAE 0.007755 versus 0.514811 for zero, but does not explain the failure on the entangled workload. The difference between zero and a training-fitted mean constant under training MSE is only 0.14%, a descriptive point estimate without uncertainty analysis. The transformer's 815,233-parameter source identity is locally verified; detailed packed-input semantics have not been independently audited.
