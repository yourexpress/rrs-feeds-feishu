Estimating Outpatient Visit Prices from Hospital Price Transparency Data using Large Language Models

arXiv cs.LG · 2026-11-15 · 论文PDF

用LLM从医院价格透明度数据估算门诊价格

Objective: Hospital care prices in the United States have increased substantially, yet patients lack effective tools to access and compare costs. We aimed to develop and evaluate an LLM-based approach for improving access to and use of hospital price transparency (PT) data. Materials and Methods: To estimate visit prices, we used a retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) framework to analyze PT files from three major hospitals in Maryland and the surrounding region. We compared BM25, OpenAI embedding models, and a GPT-BM25 hybrid against a no-RAG baseline for cost estimation of individual procedures and procedure groups, then evaluated the best approach for symptom-based visit cost estimation using synthetic simulations. Results: Across hospitals, the GPT-BM25 retriever achieved the best performance, with average price MAPE of 19.44% for individual procedures and 4.90% for groups, compared with 147.98% and 82.82%, respectively, for the no-RAG baseline. In simulations, our framework correctly identified approximately 50% of reference procedures and reduced MAPE relative to the LLM-only baseline at two of the three hospitals, while achieving comparable performance at the third.
