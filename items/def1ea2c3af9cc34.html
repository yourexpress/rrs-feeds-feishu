Behavior Graphs: Unsupervised Graph Structures for Interpreting Robot Deployment Data

arXiv · 2026-11-05 · 论文PDF

Data flywheels promise continuous improvement via insights from deployment data. Understanding how the deployed model succeeds and fails can steer subsequent data collection towards patching failures and reinforcing successes. However, robot deployments create large volumes of high-dimensional rollout data, which is challenging to understand and confidently ground data collection decisions in. To make deployment data easier to analyze and act on, we propose behavior graphs, which compress policy rollouts into a single interpretable, directed graph. We construct the graphs by learning to identify similar behavior modes across rollouts, connecting the behaviors temporally, and finally linking them to outcomes. In a user study on three real-world tasks, where participants analyzed rollouts to choose a data collection strategy, 16 of 18 preferred behavior graphs to rollout videos, reaching comparable conclusions with 40.3% lower perceived temporal demand and 37.6% lower effort.
