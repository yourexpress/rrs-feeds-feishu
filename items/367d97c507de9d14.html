During independent home study sessions, students frequently sustain improper sitting postures, accumulate excessive sedentary hours, and experience productivity loss from off-task digital distractions. Furthermore, learners often lack real-time pedagogical scaffolding for demanding self-study curricula. To mitigate these ergonomic and instructional challenges, this paper presents an affordable, privacy-preserving cyber-physical embedded system for domestic study spaces. Engineered on a Raspberry Pi 3 Model B (1 GB RAM) paired with a UVC optical camera and a USB condenser microphone, the system executes on-device computer vision utilizing MediaPipe Pose and OpenCV to monitor upper-body alignment, sagittal head inclination, and coronal shoulder asymmetry in real time without persisting raw video streams. A deterministic finite state machine (FSM) quantifies active study durations, enforces health-compliant rest periods, and detects illicit smartphone usage. Simultaneously, an integrated multimodal AI tutoring agent parses voice and text queries via an encrypted API gateway, synthesizing Socratic conceptual hints rather than direct solutions.
