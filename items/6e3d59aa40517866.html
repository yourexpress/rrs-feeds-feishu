Physically Admissible Generative Flow Matching for Sparse Flow Reconstruction

arXiv cs.LG · 2026-11-17 · 论文PDF

稀疏流重建的物理可允许生成流匹配

Reconstructing full fluid velocity fields from sparse, localized observations is a severely ill-posed inverse problem: most of the spatial domain remains unobserved, discrete conservation laws must hold strictly, and predictive uncertainty must be reliable. Existing generative models typically enforce physical constraints only at the terminal output or via soft penalties, leaving intermediate generative trajectories unconstrained. We introduce Feasible Subspace Consistency (FSC), a framework that preserves physical admissibility as an invariant of continuous generative dynamics: tangent-space matching confines the entire ODE trajectory to the discrete conservation manifold, while mass-orthonormal coordinates preserve the kinetic-energy metric. Sparse reconstruction then reduces to amortized posterior transport in the latent space of a frozen FSC prior, ensuring every posterior sample inherits incompressibility and boundary conditions without per-sample projection loops.
