Multifidelity Operator Inference for Non-intrusive Model Reduction under Data Scarcity

arXiv cs.LG · 2026-11-10 · 论文PDF

Many-query analyses, in which a computational model is repeatedly evaluated, play an important role in uncertainty quantification and optimization for engineering design. However, many-query analyses can be prohibitively expensive when considering high-fidelity numerical models, especially those governed by partial differential equations (PDEs). Surrogate modeling replaces costly simulations with cheap approximations, enabling practical uncertainty quantification and design workflows. Machine learning (ML) approaches to surrogate modeling have gained significant traction in the development of surrogate models due to their non-intrusive nature; they do not require access to the underlying simulation code. The Operator Inference (OpInf) approach, in particular, fits reduced operators to state and corresponding time derivative data. However, high-fidelity training data is scarce due to limited computational budgets, and OpInf models trained on such scarce data often exhibit high variance, a critical liability in high-risk engineering design.
