Distributed Linear Bandits for Freshness-Aware Preprocessing in MEC-Enabled Digital Twin Networks

arXiv (cs.DC) · 2026-11-03 · 论文PDF

MEC 数字孪生网络中新鲜度感知预处理的分布式线性 bandit

Digital twins (DTs) require timely updates from Internet of Things (IoT) devices to accurately represent the state of the corresponding physical systems, but transmitting large data volumes can compromise freshness. Moreover, different task classes have distinct computational demands and freshness requirements, while stochastic task generation and sizes complicate resource allocation. In this paper, we propose a freshness-aware framework in which multi-access edge computing (MEC) servers preprocess IoT data, reducing transmission volume and DT-server processing load. The framework establishes mutual support between DT and MEC servers. The DTs determine feasible CPU-allocation strategies guaranteeing class-specific age-of-information (AoI) requirements under specified workload, delay, and update-generation bounds. MEC servers collaboratively identify the feasible strategy minimizing expected freshness-normalized preprocessing delay. We formulate CPU-strategy selection as a fixed-confidence distributed best-arm identification problem in linear bandits, with MEC servers as agents and CPU allocations across task classes as arms.
