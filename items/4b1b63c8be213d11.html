Accurate and dynamic maps of agricultural LULC are important for agricultural water resource management, particularly in areas where water scarcity is prevalent, such as Morocco. Here, we evaluate the utility of AlphaEarth Foundation Model (AEF) embeddings for agricultural LULC and irrigation-type mapping in Morocco. AEF embeddings were compared to single-date and time-series remote-sensing features using three machine-learning classifiers: XGBoost, CatBoost, and TabPFN. We also evaluated whether AEF embeddings can: (a) provide an efficient and transferable alternative to conventional direct use of Earth observation products and (b) reconstruct biophysical information. Results showed that multi-temporal feature time series achieved the highest performance, with accuracies of 0.93 for agricultural LULC and 0.87 for irrigation-type mapping when using TabPFN. However, AEF embeddings yielded comparable results, achieving accuracies of 0.91 for agricultural LULC and 0.85 for irrigation-type mapping, while reducing the need for extensive data processing and manual feature engineering typically required for satellite products.
