Clips2Story: Training-Free Video Storyboarding and Editing Using Multimodal Retrieval-Embedded Generation

arXiv · 2026-11-05 · 论文PDF

Video editors often work with large collections of fragmented footage and must select and organize relevant clips into a coherent story. However, many existing video editing and summarization methods assume a temporally ordered source video and aim to preserve its original narrative and temporal structure. In this paper, we introduce Clips2Story, a training-free, LLM-based pipeline that enables users to create keyword-specified short videos from a pool of video clips. Given a pool of clips and a user-specified keyword, we first extract text-based multimodal descriptors for each clip and retrieve the clips most relevant to the keyword. We then provide the textual descriptors of these candidate clips to an LLM, which selects and arranges them into an editable storyboard with connective narrations. Finally, we assemble the selected clips and retrieve accompanying visuals for the narrations to compose the output video. To evaluate the proposed pipeline, we construct a synthetic evaluation dataset from 100 long-form videos across five genres by segmenting each video into short clips using a scene-change detection model and shuffling the resulting clips to remove their temporal order.
