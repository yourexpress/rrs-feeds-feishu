Corralling AI Risks with Grassroots Contracts

arXiv · 2026-11-05 · 论文PDF

AI's inflection point is a fountain of alarming risks. Digital platforms in particular are vulnerable to three major AI risks, as those in control of global platforms cede control to AI, of the algorithms, of membership and of content: (1) AI control of global platforms, social and economic, used by the majority of humanity, resulting in the majority of humanity becoming vulnerable to AI control. (2) Unaccountable AI agents operating anonymously on social and economic digital platforms, side-by-side with humans, possibly masquerading as humans, serving unknown masters with unknown goals, operating under unknown restraints without accountability or culpability. (3) Unattributed deepfakes generated by anonymous AI, with people unable to distinguish them from genuine content and with their producers evading accountability and culpability. Here, we propose to corral these AI risks with grassroots contracts, a digital incarnation of a two-centuries old notion of Pierre-Joseph Proudhon.
