DLS: Dual-Logic Graph Neural Networks with Chaos-Fuzzy Residuals for Robust Link Prediction

arXiv cs.LG · 2026-11-17 · 论文PDF

混沌模糊残差的双逻辑图神经网络链接预测

Link prediction is a fundamental task in graph mining, critical for applications ranging from biological interaction discovery to recommender systems. While Message Passing Neural Networks (MPNNs) have achieved state-of-the-art results, they are fundamentally limited by the inadvertent suppression of high-frequency signals, as the recursive aggregation that stabilizes structural learning inherently leads to over-smoothing, rendering node representations indistinguishable in deep layers. This limitation is particularly severe in heterophilous graphs, where structural proximity does not imply feature similarity. In this paper, we propose DLS (Dual-Logic SAGE), a novel inductive framework designed to break the over-smoothing barrier via a "controlled instability" mechanism. Unlike conventional methods relying on static structural heuristics, DLS introduces a dynamic residual pipeline. First, a Chaotic Injection Module utilizes the Logistic Map to diversify the feature manifold, exploiting sensitivity to initial conditions to separate topologically similar but semantically distinct nodes.
