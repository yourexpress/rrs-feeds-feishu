Exploration of Reinforcement Learning in Generative Recommendation Models

arXiv cs.LG · 2026-11-15 · 论文PDF

生成式推荐模型中强化学习探索

Traditional cascaded recommender systems balance accuracy and efficiency over large item spaces, but misaligned stage-wise objectives hinder end-to-end optimization. Generative recommendation unifies these stages in an autoregressive framework, providing a natural interface for reinforcement learning (RL) to optimize the entire generation process. However, directly applying RL often leads to unstable optimization and degraded recommendation quality. We revisit Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO) and other ratio-based objectives through a unified logit-gradient view. We identify three issues: (i) clipped surrogates create dead-gradient regions, under-utilizing samples; (ii) negative-advantage updates can become effectively unbounded as importance ratios grow, allowing a small subset of negatives to dominate optimization; and (iii) ratio-based control lacks sensitivity to absolute policy confidence, so updates on high-confidence cases may fail to vanish.
