Sustainable Federated Fine-Tuning with LoRA: Environmental Impact and Performance Footprint

arXiv cs.LG · 2026-11-12 · 论文PDF

Federated Learning (FL) with Parameter-Efficient Fine-Tuning (PEFT) enables efficient adaptation of Large Language Models (LLMs), yet its energy and carbon costs across hardware remain poorly understood. We evaluated federated Low-Rank Adaptation (LoRA) fine-tuning on NVIDIA A40, RTX 6000 Ada, and A100 GPUs using a $2^2$ full factorial design with 135M/360M models and 10/100 local steps. CodeCarbon tracked energy and estimated CO$_2$-equivalent emissions. Descriptive allocation of variation assigned 84.8-91.2% of energy and emissions variation to local steps within these ranges. On A40 and RTX 6000 Ada, configurations with fewer steps achieved comparable accuracy with roughly 6-8-fold lower energy consumption and higher accuracy per kWh. These findings support smaller local budgets for energy-efficient federated fine-tuning within the evaluated setting.
