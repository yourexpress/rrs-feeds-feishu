Matching Architecture to Workload: Comparing RISC-V Parallel Accelerators for Energy-Efficient Embedded Computing

面向能效嵌入式计算的 RISC-V 并行加速器架构-负载匹配对比

Embedded computing demands high performance and energy efficiency across workloads with widely different computational characteristics. Choosing the most suitable execution model therefore requires a quantitative understanding of how each model maps to different workload classes. In this paper, we compare three execution models for energy-efficient embedded computing: scalar multicore, stream ing multicore, and vector processing. We use three open-source RISC-V accelerators as representative implementations: the PULP Cluster, the Snitch Cluster, and Spatz, respectively. Their open-source hardware and software stacks enable a controlled comparison based on implementations in the same 12 nm technology and a common benchmark suite. Our results reveal that no single execution model performs best across all workload classes. Spatz delivers the greatest benefits for regular and highly vectorizable compute-intensive applications, achieving up to 4.29x higher performance and 5.17x greater energy efficiency than the other architectures.
