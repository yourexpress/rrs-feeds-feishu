A Transferable Pretrained 3D Model for Impedance Inversion with Sparse-Well Prior Conditioning

arXiv cs.LG · 2026-11-11 · 论文PDF

Deep-learning-based seismic impedance inversion commonly requires retraining for each target survey, which may generalize poorly beyond the conditioning locations when well control is extremely sparse. We propose a transferable three-dimensional inversion framework that separates offline learning from target-survey deployment. A masked autoencoder first learns seismic structural representations from unlabeled multi-survey data, after which the frozen encoder is incorporated into an inversion network trained on synthetic geological models with sparse-well-derived low-frequency priors. During deployment, the complete inversion network remains fixed; the available conditioning well is used only to construct the low-frequency prior and align the global impedance range. Experiments on the Overthrust model show that the proposed method achieves the lowest full-volume NRMSE and reduces the P95 trace-wise NRMSE from 0.1310 to 0.1074 relative to the best target-trained baseline, indicating fewer localized large errors under different well locations.
