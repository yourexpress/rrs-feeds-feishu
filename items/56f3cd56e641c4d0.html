Subspace Learning with Outlier Exposure for Out-of-Distribution Detection

arXiv · 2026-11-06 · 论文PDF

Out-of-Distribution (OoD) detection is pivotal for reliable deployments of Deep Neural Networks (DNNs), aiming to distinguish anomalous samples from the In-Distribution (InD) data in training. By introducing outliers as surrogate OoD data to finetune DNNs, Outlier Exposure (OE) has greatly advanced OoD detection in recent years. Existing OE-based research remains predominantly logit-centric, either by generating diverse outliers or by regularizing them with objectives operating at the prediction-output level. Nonetheless, these approaches leave much of the rich information in feature spaces untapped. In this work, we propose an OE-based detection framework, named RecOE, that learns appropriate feature subspaces from InD data and auxiliary outliers, such that the InD-OoD discrepancy can be effectively characterized through the learned feature subspaces. Specifically, the feature subspaces are optimized to capture the dominant variations of InD and outliers by minimizing reconstruction errors therein.
