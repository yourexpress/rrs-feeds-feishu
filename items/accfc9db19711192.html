RecoGEM: A Serving System for Production-Scale Generative Recommendation

arXiv cs.DC · 2026-11-09 · 论文PDF

Generative recommendation (GR) is moving industrial recommender systems (RecSys) from scoring candidate items to autoregressively generating item identifiers. This shift promises higher recommendation quality, but makes serving substantially harder: each request executes a short sequence of dependent decoding steps that explore a large item space and select promising candidates on the latency-critical path. GR not only creates structural mismatches with conventional RecSys pipelines, but also challenges the architectural and optimization assumptions of LLM serving stacks, making direct adoption of existing systems difficult. We present RecoGEM, our production GR serving system. RecoGEM uses a runtime--engine decoupling architecture to move GR-specific serving responsibilities, such as candidate-state transitions and live artifact updates, to a dedicated runtime. Its execution engine exploits GR's short token sequences, large candidate populations, and predictable step shapes to optimize self-attention, candidate selection, and execution planning.
