Byzantine-Robust Muon: Resilient Momentum Orthogonalization for Distributed Learning

arXiv cs.LG · 2026-11-09 · 论文PDF

Optimization methods, including the Muon optimizer based on the Linear Minimization Oracle (LMO) and momentum orthogonalization, have shown strong empirical performance in large-scale and distributed learning, yet their robustness to adversarial behavior and data heterogeneity is not well understood. We study these methods within a unified first-order trust-region framework and analyze Muon-based updates with robust aggregation for Byzantine-robust distributed and federated learning. Our analysis does not rely on the i.i.d. assumption and introduces a non-Euclidean notion of heterogeneity consistent with the trust-region geometry. Under this framework, we establish convergence guarantees for both general non-convex and star-convex objectives. Our results provide a theoretical characterization of Byzantine-robust Muon-based optimization, with an additive heterogeneity error floor consistent with known impossibility results, under explicit aggregation assumptions. Under an explicit additive oracle-error condition, the guarantees also cover inexact orthogonalization, with the non-convex Byzantine terms in the bound's numerator unchanged but a smaller progress denominator.
