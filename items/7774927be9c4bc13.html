Local Distributed Computation under Crash Failures

arXiv (cs.DC) · 2026-11-03 · 论文PDF

We study deterministic algorithms for locally checkable graph problems in the synchronous LOCAL model with crash failures, where a crashing node may send messages to only some neighbors. We introduce consistent neighborhoods: partial radius-$R$ views that agree locally across surviving edges and are complete without crashes. For $R=T+\ell+1$, these views allow local simulation of $T$-round fault-free algorithms that remain correct on subgraphs, satisfying constraints wherever the radius-$\ell$ checking neighborhood contains no crash. On graphs with at most $n$ nodes and maximum degree at most $Δ\geq3$, we construct these views in $O(\min\{n,(Δ-1)^R,RΔf\})$ rounds when a crash bound $f\geq1$ is globally known; the first two bounds require no knowledge of $f$. For $Δ=2$ or girth greater than $2R$, including forests, $O(R)$ rounds suffice. Our upper bounds are essentially tight in suitable parameter regimes. One crash can force $Ω(n)$ rounds even for $R=3$ on diameter-two graphs. For each fixed $Δ\geq3$ and sufficiently large $R$, we prove a $(Δ-1)^{R-O(\sqrt R)}$ lower bound even with crashes restricted to round beginnings.
