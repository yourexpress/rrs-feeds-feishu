Normalizing Flows (NFs) have shown strong results in generative modeling, recently reaching state-of-the-art image generation performance. However, their repetitive, sequential operations limit the computational and memory efficiency achievable with GPUs and TPUs. We propose M3D-FLOW, a novel monolithic 3D-stackable (M3D) DRAM-based processing-near-memory (PNM) architecture that eliminates memory bottlenecks using a Cu-Cu hybrid-bonded logic die integrated with an M3D DRAM die. The M3D DRAM provides dense bank integration and a tiered organization with variable access latencies that M3D-FLOW exploits for the efficient processing of different NF workloads. To maximize compute density while minimizing area, energy, and thermal overhead, M3D-FLOW employs hybrid binary--stochastic floating-point multipliers, performing mantissa multiplication in the stochastic domain and exponent operations in the binary domain.
