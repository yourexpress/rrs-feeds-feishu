InformedXRD: Reproducible Benchmarks and Physics-Informed Evaluation for Powder Diffraction Symmetry Classification

arXiv (cs.LG) · 2026-11-01 · 论文PDF

Machine learning for powder diffraction (PXRD) symmetry classification is being trained at scale, yet the field lacks a common evaluation standard: papers report results on different subsets of experimental archives, with different target taxonomies, preprocessing, and metrics, and published models are rarely released in a form that permits rescoring. We present InformedXRD, an evaluation framework with three parts: (i) a machine-readable mapping from 230 space groups to 99 extinction groups, the finest classes powder diffraction can identify from systematic absences alone; (ii) a benchmark-construction method whose inclusion rules are specified as code and reproduce from a frozen snapshot, instantiated as RRUFF-473, RRUFF-325, and, as a small example, opXRD-98; (iii) a reporting protocol with prior-only frequency baselines, a label-permutation test, a hierarchy-aware error metric on the condensed translationengleiche subgroup graph, and stratified evaluation. We build on the mapping, RRUFF benchmarks, subgroup graph, and four ViT checkpoints released in our earlier work (Baggett et al., 2026).
