Predicting live birth before fresh embryo transfer could support shared decision-making in IVF/ICSI, but routine clinical data are small, heterogeneous, and contain pathway-dependent variables prone to outcome leakage. We aimed to construct a curated single-centre IVF/ICSI dataset and develop a prediction model suited to small tabular clinical data. \textit{Materials and Methods:} We assembled 616 fresh embryo-transfer cycles (251 live birth, 365 non-live birth) with clinical, laboratory, and embryological predictors, and embryo morphology encoded with a novel differential weighting of the inner cell mass and trophectoderm. After KNN imputation, robust scaling, and selection of 50 features, we developed a Hybrid Attention Network (HAN), a learnable feature-attention gate followed by a compact multilayer perceptron, and a HAN + logistic-regression (LR) ensemble with a validation-tuned weight.
