Network-Guided Automated Synthesis of MAC Units for Neural Network Inference

网络引导的 MAC 单元自动综合用于神经网络推理

In neural inference accelerators, multiply-accumulate (MAC) datapaths account for the majority of circuit area and critical-path delay, where operator-level numerical formats simultaneously dictate hardware overhead and quantization error. Yet no straightforward mapping exists between hardware cost and end-to-end network accuracy: logic synthesis transforms datapaths in a highly nonlinear manner, while numerical errors propagate and couple across layers. To address this disconnect, we present NAMSI, an automated network-aware MAC synthesis framework tailored for neural inference. NAMSI characterizes heterogeneous data formats using a format-distribution representation (FD) and leverages topology-aware accuracy and hardware-cost modeling (TaH), calibrated with actual post-synthesis Area-Delay Product (ADP) measurements, to jointly estimate coupled full-network accuracy and circuit cost. Furthermore, reversible lattice search (RLS) is introduced to tractably navigate the exponentially scaling design space.
