Split-KV: Efficient Context Parallelism for Training Diffusion Drafters

arXiv · 2026-11-05 · 论文PDF

Diffusion drafters accelerate speculative decoding by denoising multiple draft tokens in parallel. Their training uses anchor sampling to construct compact draft queries and performs cross-attention with a block-sparse mask over a much longer context. We empirically show that acceptance length remains stable as context scales with a fixed query budget, while increasing the query budget yields diminishing quality gains, jointly establishing a practical regime with queries far shorter than the context. No existing context parallelism (CP) method jointly exploits this query--context asymmetry and meets the nonlinear, sample-dependent load-balancing requirement created by anchor sampling. We present Split-KV, an exact CP method that keeps key/value shards local and limits per-layer attention communication to query-sized tensors, making this communication independent of context length. Its global query view further enables a cost-aware longest-processing-time-first (LPT) strategy that balances context key/value chunks by their realized attention cost.
