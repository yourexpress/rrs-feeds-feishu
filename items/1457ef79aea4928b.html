FILO: A Unified Fisher-Geometric Framework for Low-Rank Adaptation

arXiv · 2026-11-06 · 论文PDF

Low-Rank Adaptation (LoRA) reparameterizes a weight matrix $W$ as a product of two low-rank factors, but the Jacobian $J_G$ of the generator mapping the factors to the weight matrix is rank-deficient, so the factor-space preconditioner $J_G^* F_t J_G$ induced by any $W$-space preconditioner $F_t$ is singular, and consequently the standard chain rule cannot be uniquely inverted to map a preconditioned $W$-space direction back to a factor-space update. Therefore, we introduce FILO, a unified Fisher-geometric framework for low-rank adaptation that enables factor updates without directly inverting this singular operator and casts existing LoRA optimizers by their factor-space inversion strategy and $W$-space preconditioner $F_t$. Existing methods occupy four families along these axes: factor-space adaptive updates, block-diagonal surrogates for $J_G^* J_G$, Frobenius-residual pseudoinverse methods, and Riemannian manifold methods. Within this design space, a gradient-statistics-aware $F_t$ paired with a closed-form factor-space solve at $O((m+n)r)$ memory remains underexplored.
