Federated LLM unlearning addresses targeted knowledge removal when forget and retain data remain distributed across private clients and are inaccessible to the server. The resulting locally trained adapters encode conflicting objectives, making their aggregation fundamentally different from conventional federated optimization. A key failure mode is magnitude explosion, whereby unnormalized cross-client aggregation increases the merged update magnitude and degrades retained utility under stronger unlearning. We propose FIRM (Fisher-Informed Retention Merging), a single-round framework for stable and retention-aware server-side aggregation. FIRM uses client-local Fisher statistics as a unified sensitivity signal to construct a Fisher-normalized base merge and impose explicit control over retain-sensitive parameters. Its second-order retention projection admits a unique minimum-distance solution, while the corresponding dual scales with the number of retain clients rather than the model dimension.
