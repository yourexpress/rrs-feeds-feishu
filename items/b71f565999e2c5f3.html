From Data Alignment to Parameter Generation: Few-Shot Cross-City Traffic Prediction with Conditional Diffusion

Accurate mobile traffic forecasting is crucial for network service management, supporting proactive capacity provisioning, congestion mitigation, and energy-efficient operation. However, many cities and regions are data-scarce (e.g., newly deployed or poorly instrumented areas), thus making traffic prediction a few-shot learning problem and limiting the practicality of standard data-driven models. Existing cross-city solutions primarily rely on data-space alignment to match traffic distributions between data-rich and data-scarce cities, but such alignment is often brittle under real-world distribution shifts, measurement noise, and city-specific bias. To address these limitations, we propose a Conditional Diffusion-based Parameter Generation (CDPG) framework. Instead of aligning raw data, CDPG learns the conditional distribution of high-performing predictor parameters for source cities and directly generates node-specific model parameters for the target city.
