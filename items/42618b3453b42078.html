Randomized and Oracle-Based Feasible Directions Methods for Nonsmooth Nonconvex Constrained Optimization

arXiv · 2026-11-06 · 论文PDF

Minimizing nonsmooth nonconvex objectives over general convex sets using no derivatives of the objective, together with structural access to the constraint set, poses a fundamental geometric difficulty: at boundary points, the feasible direction cone may fail to be closed, preventing the construction of finite sets of search directions that capture all feasible descent directions. We develop a feasible-directions framework for problems structured as the difference between a continuously differentiable function and a convex function over a closed convex set, and access the objective without knowledge of its decomposition, through exact line searches along feasible segments. To address the geometric obstruction, we introduce $η$-feasible directions, which remain uniformly separated from the boundary of the feasible direction cone, enabling finite-direction search even in the presence of nonlinear constraints. We present two algorithmic realizations of this framework. The first assumes access to an $η$-direction oracle and extends feasible-directions methods from linear to general convex constraints.
